## 6. Regional patterns (EU)

1. **Debt-like wrappers dominate, not equity.** Participative loans (ES), subordinated bonds (DE), profit-participation rights (AT), revenue royalties (SI). Direct SPV equity is rare because Spanish S.L. and German GmbH share transfers need a notary — incompatible with free token transfer — and debt avoids AIFMD "collective investment" questions [I]. RealX should expect to tokenise a *claim*, then work to strengthen it (security agent, land-charge, trustee).
2. **Five different regulatory postures, one continent.** Fully licensed chain (Propvest) · exemption + ESI placement (Reental) · "MiCA-not-MiFID" thesis (Blocksquare) · software-only (Brickken) · outside-perimeter (Domoblock). Only Propvest enjoys passporting. None of the five tokenised programmes uses **ECSPR** or the **DLT Pilot Regime**, while the largest ECSPs (Bricks.co, Exporo crowdfunding, Urbanitae) remain off-chain — a clear white space: **an ECSP-licensed, token-native platform with KIIS and passport**.
3. **Utility tokens are the MiCA exposure.** Three of five run a native token (RNT, BST, BKN) for staking tiers, fee discounts, governance and buy-backs; property tokens themselves are generally MiFID instruments. Mixing utility-token incentives with securities distribution is a recurring conduct risk.
4. **Stablecoin rails lag MiCA.** Payouts/payments use USDT (Reental), DAI (Domoblock, Blocksquare), USDC/USDT (Brickken); no top-5 platform defaults to a MiCA-authorised EUR e-money token (EURC/EURCV) even though ESMA required CASPs to stop offering non-compliant stablecoins in 2025 [I]. Propvest avoids the issue with EUR-only rails.
5. **Wallet UX converged on "no seed phrases".** Passkey ERC-4337 smart accounts with sponsored gas (Reental/HumanWallet), Alchemy account abstraction (Blocksquare), licensed custody (Propvest/Tangany), EMI e-wallets (Domoblock/Mangopay). EU EMIs **Mangopay and Lemonway** are the default fiat rails.
6. **Chains: Polygon + Ethereum mainnet, all EVM.** Polygon (Reental, Domoblock, Brickken), Ethereum (Blocksquare, Propvest, Brickken), Base/BNB (Brickken). Permissioning is mostly custom whitelists; ERC-3643 appears only via infra (Tokeny/BrickMark), and ERC-7943 via Brickken.
7. **Secondary liquidity is thin and self-operated.** P2P bulletin boards with seller-set prices (Propvest trades at 75–80% of par; Brickwise bulletin board), protocol pools (Oceanpoint), DeFi borrowing as a liquidity substitute (Reental RNTlend) — no MTF/DLT-MTF listings among the five.
8. **Distribution & tax:** monthly stablecoin (Reental, Blocksquare), quarterly EUR (Propvest), bullet at exit (Domoblock). Spanish platforms automate **19% withholding**, which makes tax engines a core admin module; German custody-bank setups push tax to the depot bank.

## 7. What this means for RealX (EU lens)
- **Best composite blueprint:** Propvest's licensed-partner sandwich (EMI + depot/custodian + licensed distributor) + Reental's UX (passkey smart wallet, gas sponsorship, monthly stablecoin, P2P, auto-reinvest) + Blocksquare's protocol hygiene (100k-token convention, on-chain registries, IPFS legal docs, claim/push distributions) + Brickken's issuer API semantics (escrow soft/hard caps, KYC state machine, per-token whitelist) + Brickwise's in-rem protection (collective pledge in land register).
- **Avoid:** Domoblock-style rights-less tokens; performance marketing without independent valuation (Propvest); utility-token gating of securities access (Reental/Blocksquare/Brickken).
- **Admin-panel common core (all five):** investor 360 + KYC queue; wallet allow-list/whitelist manager; offering manager with funding thresholds and refunds; fiat/stablecoin reconciliation; distribution engine (snapshot, withholding, batch, claim/push); secondary-market surveillance; document/disclosure centre; exit waterfall; maker-checker on payouts/token ops; audit log.

- Off-chain benchmarks (Urbanitae, Housers, Estateguru) and several longlist names not verified due to the search cap.
- Legal-threshold details: **resolved in QA** — Reg. (EU) 2024/2809 (Listing Act) sets a harmonised €12m per issuer/12-month prospectus-exemption threshold with a Member State option of €5m, applicable from **5 June 2026** (verified on EUR-Lex; see `01-regulatory/eu.md` §2). Spain's former €8m national threshold (Ley 6/2023 art. 35) is therefore superseded; **which option Spain (and Slovenia) applies is still unverified** — the BOE consolidated text could not be fetched in full.

**With a marketplace, the sponsors own the product. RealX handles distribution, the platform, and vetting sponsors.** RealX doesn't run property trusts itself, so it doesn't need to be the licensed scheme operator (responsible entity, RE). This model also fits the gap the pack found: Tokeniser, Australia's main tokenisation platform, serves 160+ issuers but has no property-specific product.

## How it works in Australia

| | Wholesale (now) | Retail (around 8 Apr 2027) |
|---|---|---|
| Who issues | Each sponsor's own wholesale trust, under the sponsor's own licence | One umbrella scheme run by a hired RE, with each sponsor's property as a sub-fund |
| RealX's licence | Arranges deals as an authorised representative (CAR) of a licence holder; takes weeks | Same, plus distributor duties for each property's target-market statement (TMD) |
| Local precedent | Tokeniser: issuers keep their own vehicle and licence | Assetora: 130+ sub-funds under one registration; sponsors propose assets and the RE vets them |

## What changes for the product

1. **Sponsor quality becomes your biggest risk.** RealT failed on property operations; on a marketplace, that's a sponsor failing under your brand. Vet sponsors, require them to keep a stake (Lofty's sellers keep at least 10%), and publish each sponsor's planned vs actual returns.
2. **Revenue comes from sponsors.** Tokeniser charges A$11k a year plus A$2.5k setup for a fund of about 50 investors. The pack warns against charging fees in the property token itself (Blocksquare) and settling in your own token (Tokeniser's LIQ).
3. **Custody decides whether the new Act applies.** Holding tokens for investors may make RealX a licensed digital asset platform from 8 Apr 2027. Tokeniser's own-wallet design appears built to avoid that; this is inferred and needs counsel.
4. **Sponsors can't switch off compliance.** KYC and eligibility rules are enforced across the whole platform, and one KYC works for every sponsor.
5. **The admin panel has two tiers:** a RealX console above a sponsor console. This is archetype A5 in [[00-overview#6. Admin-panel archetypes|00-overview.md]].

## Defaults I'm assuming from the pack (tell me if any are wrong)

- Investors hold tokens in app wallets with no seed phrase and with account recovery.
- AUD first (NPP/PayID), AUD stablecoins later.
- The trustee's unit register is the legal record; the token mirrors it.
- No RealX utility token.
- Publish audited capital-raised figures only.

## Open

- **Q12 Build vs buy the token layer.** The options are in [[Marketplace Notes#4. Build vs buy]]. Asked on 23 Sep 2026 in [[R04 Residential and Commercial]].

**Design questions raised while building [[RealX Product Map]]** (from the extraction agents' reviews of the pack):
- **Sponsor signing share:** does a sponsor hold any custody or signing share, such as a key in the token-agent quorum? The pack leaves this open.
- **Wallet recovery mechanism:** RealX as guardian, passkey sync or delegate key-sharing. The pack lists options without choosing; token recovery by the agent is the fallback.
- **Separation of duties on the wholesale track:** when a sponsor is both trustee and tenant admin, which approvals must stay with RealX or the licensee?
- **For counsel:**
  - Retail cooling-off: none, as in BrickX's PDS, or 14 days, as the pack infers?
  - How does the s601ED 20-member trigger apply to each sponsor's wholesale trust?
  - Which sponsor declarations must a person make, not the sponsor's agent: the application declaration and commitments, fee-agreement acceptance, fit-and-proper and submission declarations, the monthly operator attestation and the title-verification declaration (CQ-09, frozen in A00)?
  - Which investor tax declarations must a person make, not their agent: the FATCA/CRS self-certification, the residency and TIN declaration and the no-TFN acknowledgement (CQ-10, frozen in A00)?

# A28 - Token operations service (mint, burn, freeze, pause, forced transfer, recovery)

**The one service that carries every V1, V2 and V3 token function through the whole of §6.2: approval to `CustodyOp` to policy engine to gateway call to indexer confirmation to registry instruction to targeted reconciliation to closed. Deploy under the V1 owner quorum; mint only against a locked allocation file; burn, freeze, unfreeze and lock-up dates under `4E+Q`; pause as `SGL+R` ratified within 2 hours; unpause, forced transfer and recovery under `6E+Q` with a 48 h timelock that only CCO + CEO with a court order can waive; upgrades at 72 h. The AM2 exit lives here: one mint bound to one approval id round-trips on the local chain, and a replayed id is refused at every layer. Supply never exceeds the allocation; nothing broadcasts on fewer than two distinct hardware-key assertions; a confirmed transaction the register does not reflect is a break, not a success.**

**Build status:** not started. **Milestone:** AM2 (Token and wallet core). **Lane:** Chain. **Planner:** Opus 5.5 (high). **Builder:** Grok 4.7 (xhigh). **Node QA:** Opus 5.5 (high, every loop). **Graph QA:** Fable 5.1 (high, at milestone close).
**Estimate:** 10-14 agent-hours (build, challenge and verify loops).

**Depends on:** [[A27 - Chain adapter, deployment manifests and indexer]], [[A15 - Custody sandbox adapter and policy engine P1-P10]], [[A18 - Unit register (legal record) and registry sandbox adapter]], [[A20 - Three-way reconciliation engine with zero tolerance]], [[A29 - Wallet service- smart-account factory, bundler sandbox, guardians and recovery requests]] (recovery request lookup and wallet identity link; A29 is AM2 and does not depend on A28: it reaches this node only over the public transfer-request route and events, so this node's gate also runs A29's handoff against the real route)
**Unlocks:** [[A38 - Finalise-and-mint and revert-and-refund pipelines]], [[A42 - AML screening, monitoring, cases and SMR clocks (SM-11)]], [[A48 - Halts, kill switches and wind-down]], [[A72 - Console- token operations, forced transfer and recovery desk, wallets and custody, token registry]], [[A91 - P2- resale board market module (SM-09)]]

**Oracle rule:** The node QA writes hidden oracle tests from this spec to `<workspace>\gauntlet\hidden\A28\` before the build starts ([[App Gauntlet]] §2). The builder never reads that folder; the cases are published into the repo only after the gate passes. The oracle scope is fixed by the node summary: a confirmed transaction the register does not reflect opens a break, never a success; the units minted under one approval never exceed the locked allocation and total supply never exceeds the vehicle's supply rule; nothing is handed to the chain adapter with fewer than two distinct hardware-key assertions; a replayed approval id is refused by this service, by custody, by the gateway on the local chain, by the indexer binding (A27) and by this module's event consumer; the 48 h and 72 h timelocks cannot be shortened except by the named waiver.

This node composes what A14, A15, A18, A20, A25 and A27 already prove and adds the token-specific rules from the product map's D04 rows (requirements catalogue D04 and SM-06, SM-07, SM-13; architecture brief §4.1-§4.3 and §6.2). It runs on the local Hardhat chain with sandbox custody, registry and bank adapters and keys that carry no value. Nothing here touches a vendor, a production key or mainnet. This note is not legal advice; every checker set, quorum, timelock and threshold below is a seeded policy row, and counsel's or the founder's answers change rows, not code.

## Build brief

This node is API-only: module `tokenops` in the Fastify monolith (`services/api/src/modules/tokenops/`), with its worker jobs registered from the same folder through A09's job registry. It has no screens; ADM-14, ADM-15, ADM-13 and ADM-40 are built by A72 on top of this module's API, and the pipelines A38, A42, A48 and A91 call it through `packages/sdk`. Every rule is enforced in the API, never only in a screen. The only source of time is A09's injectable clock. Every read contract is in the OpenAPI document from the first commit.

- Lock-in release at the recorded date needs no transaction and no approval: `LockInModule` stops restricting on its own once `unlockAt` passes, and this module records SM-07 `locked → active` as a read-model transition on A09's injectable clock (source `lock_expiry`, one audit event, no op). Where a lock was made with `freezePartialTokens` (the fallback above), a date-passed release is drafted automatically into REGO's queue as an `unlock` request (REGO submits, CCO approves, human V2 signers sign); nothing unlocks under an `AUTO` or `SVCA` signature on V2, so A15's P9 is never weakened.

**2. `TokenOperation` records and their lifecycle**

- `token_operations`: `op_id` in the form `OP-<yyyymmdd>-<6 base32 chars>` (Crockford alphabet, generated server-side; the design's `OP-7731` style ids are seed aliases mapped by the seed table); `tenant_id`; `vehicle_id`; `token_id`; `kind`; `params_canonical` (sorted-key JSON, whole units, addresses lower-cased, no floats) and `params_sha256`; `approval_id` (exactly one, unique across the table: a second op citing the same approval id is refused with `TOP_APPROVAL_CONSUMED` by a unique index, not by application code alone); `custody_op_id`; `vault_code`; `timelock_until`; `waiver_approval_id`; `tx_hash`; `block_number`; `confirmations`; `register_instruction_id` and `register_ack_at`; `recon_run_id`; `state`; `reason_code`, `legal_basis`, `evidence_doc_ids[]` (hashes recorded, never the documents); standard RLS and audit columns from A04.
- States: `requested` → `approved` → (`queued` while a timelock runs) → `awaiting_signatures` → `signed` → `broadcast` → `confirmed` → `register_pending` (only kinds that send a register instruction: `burn`, `burn_final`, `forced_transfer`, `recovery`, `lock_up`, `unlock`; `mint` and `batch_mint` never enter it and go `confirmed → reconciling` once the register verification below passes) → `reconciling` → `closed`; terminal `refused` (precheck or policy), `cancelled` (maker cancel, veto or parent expiry), `failed` (revert or registry rejection before confirmation), and `broken` (confirmed on chain but the register or the reconciliation disagrees). The table lives in `packages/domain` under A03's extension point; anything not listed is refused with `TOP_ILLEGAL_TRANSITION`. `broken` is not terminal: it returns to `reconciling` only when A20 closes the break after a clean re-run (a `6E` register adjustment or a corrective op through V2), and then to `closed`.
- Mapping to A14's SM-00 and A15's `CustodyOp`: `approved` = the request reached `approved` (or `queued`); `awaiting_signatures` = the custody op is `signing`; `signed`, `broadcast`, `confirmed` mirror the custody op one to one; `closed` requires a `reconciled` event and closes the parent request through A14's internal transition endpoint. A `confirmed` op that A20 has not reconciled stays open and visible; it is never reported as success, and A14's `closed` is never called for it.
- Execution hand-off: this node registers an effector with A14 for every `token.*` action code: `execute` creates the `CustodyOp` (vault from the kind, `target` and `selector` from A27's address book, exact calldata from `params_canonical`, `calldata_sha256`, the attested `cap` for mints) and submits it to A15; `postcondition` is "A20's targeted run for this vehicle is clean"; `revert` exists only for `pause` (see §5) and is otherwise `null`, because a chain call either happened or did not.
- Broadcast: when A15 reports `signed`, this node verifies the bundle itself before anything reaches the chain: distinct signer count ≥ the vault's quorum (V1 3, V2 2), or for V3 exactly one signer who is not the `SGL+R` actor (so two distinct hardware-key assertions exist, the actor's `act` decision and the signer's), attestation verified with A15's shared vectors, `approvalId` unconsumed in this table, `calldata_sha256` equal to the op's. A bundle failing any check is refused with `TOP_BUNDLE_INVALID`, the op stays `signed`, and a Sev2 alert goes to SECO through A13. Only then does `POST /operations/:id/broadcast` hand the bundle to A27's `sendGatewayCall`; the call is idempotent on `op_id` and A27 owns re-broadcast of a stuck transaction with the identical bundle. This node never re-signs.
- Confirmation: this node does **not** match raw chain events. A27's indexer already binds every `Executed(approvalId)` to its `CustodyOp` four ways and, on a full match after the configured confirmations, moves the custody op `broadcast → confirmed` through A15's status callback; A15 then emits the outbox event `custody.op.confirmed` (carrying `custody_op_id`, `approval_id`, `vehicle_id`, `tx_hash`, `block_number`). That is the one confirmation event in the system: this module consumes it on `POST /v1/internal/token-ops/events` (service principal only) and moves the matching `TokenOperation` to `confirmed`, writing the audit event `token.op.confirmed`; A20's targeted run and A38's F7 consume the same `custody.op.confirmed` and correlate on `trigger_ref = custody_op_id`. This module emits no second confirmation event. A partial match, a forged or replayed `Executed`, or a supply-changing event with no matched `Executed` is A27's business: A27 records it as `match_state = unmatched` or `binding = unbound`, leaves the custody op untouched, and A20's targeted run raises its existing Sev1 breaks (R7 `qty`, R1 `role_drift`, or the `unbound_changes[]` break). This module only reads A27's `chain.executed.unmatched` outcome to cross-link the break on the op's detail payload; it opens no break of its own and uses no break type A20 does not define. A transaction revert (A15's `custody.op.failed`) moves the op to `failed`; the approval id is spent (A15 consumed it at `signed`) and a new request is needed. Nothing retries silently.
- Register step for mints (`mint`, `batch_mint`): **verify only**. The trustee's register entry already exists (A38's F5 issues it and waits for A18's ack before it calls this service; for a not-from-file mint, REGO's issue instruction under the same approval id must be acked first). This module sends **no** A18 instruction for a mint and never enters `register_pending`. At `confirmed` it reads A18 `GET /internal/register/issued?approval_id=` and requires its lines (investor, units, wallet) and `total_units` to equal the minted lines exactly; on a match it writes `token.op.register_verified` and moves to `reconciling`; on any difference, or on `REG_NO_ENTRY`, it moves the op to `broken` (reason `register_mismatch`) and the targeted run's R2, R3 or R7 `qty` break carries it. The mint step leaves the register's units unchanged.
- Register step for the other kinds: in the same database transaction that records `confirmed`, this module submits the matching instruction to A18 under the same `approval_id`: `redeem` for `burn` and `burn_final`, `transfer` for `forced_transfer` and `recovery`, lot update for `lock_up` and `unlock`. The op sits in `register_pending` until A18's ack; an ack past A18's SLA is R17's business and halts resale for that vehicle through A20; a registry rejection after a confirmed chain call moves the op to `broken` (the chain moved, the legal record did not).
- Reconciliation: A20's targeted run fires on `custody.op.confirmed` and on every registry ack; this module subscribes to `recon.run.completed` for the vehicle and correlates on `trigger_ref = custody_op_id` (and implements the `markReconciled(opId, runId)` / `markBreak(opId, breakIds)` write-back A20 calls on the custody-op owner). A clean run moves `reconciling → closed`, writes `token.op.closed`, and closes the parent approval. Any break moves the op to `broken` and keeps it on ADM-14 and ADM-17.
- Register-first for mints: a mint or batch mint is refused with `TOP_REGISTER_FIRST` unless A18 `GET /internal/register/issued?approval_id=` returns acked issue lines equal to the requested lines (investor, units, wallet) with `total_units` equal to the request; the precheck names the first line that fails. A `submitted` or `rejected` instruction returns `REG_NO_ENTRY` and fails the precheck. The trustee issues units first; the chain mirrors them.

**3. Prechecks registered with A14 (run at draft, again at submit, again before the custody hand-off)**

- Token exists and its SM-06 state allows the kind (`mint` needs `configured`, `issuing` or `active`; `pause` needs `active`; `unpause` needs `paused`; `upgrade` needs `active`; `forced_transfer`, `recovery`, `burn`, `lock_up`, `partial_freeze`, `partial_unfreeze` need `active` or `redeeming`; `burn_final` needs `redeeming`) else `TOP_SM06_STATE`.
- No open Sev1 reconciliation break for the vehicle for value-moving kinds (`mint`, `burn`, `burn_final`, `forced_transfer`, `recovery`, `unlock`, `partial_unfreeze`) else `TOP_BREAK_OPEN` (A20's flag; A14's `APR_RECON_BREAK_OPEN` is the same rule seen from the request); a pre-flight run (A20) is requested for mints and must be clean at hand-off.
- Paused token: `forced_transfer`, `recovery` and `unlock` are refused with `TOP_TOKEN_PAUSED` unless the request references an approved `token.paused_override` (CCO → LEGL, `4E`, legal basis mandatory); `mint` and `burn` while paused are refused outright. This is enforced here whatever the vendored contract allows.
- Supply guard: `sum(units in the request) ≤ remaining units of the referenced locked allocation (file total − units already minted under it)`, the locked allocation read through the `AllocationSource` port (§7), `totalSupply + units ≤ the vehicle's supply rule` (10,000 by default, from the offering); the attested `cap` handed to A15 equals the file total, so P3 and the gateway's per-approval cap enforce the same number. `TOP_SUPPLY_EXCEEDS_ALLOCATION`, `TOP_SUPPLY_RULE`.
- Destination guard for `mint`, `forced_transfer`, `recovery`: every destination wallet is linked to a verified identity through the `IdentityLookup` port (§7: A29's wallet-to-identity link in AM2; A30/A31's off-chain identity registry record and A31's on-chain IR check when they land) else `TOP_DESTINATION_UNREGISTERED`; the recipient's post-operation holding passes G07 (holder cap including associates) and G06 (residency and FIRB for the vehicle) through A19's evaluators, each stored as a `GateEvaluation`; a `canTransfer` simulation through A27 must return true for transfers.
- `burn`: units ≤ the holder's register units (A18) and ≤ the chain balance at the latest indexed block (`TOP_BURN_EXCEEDS_HOLDING`); a burn that would take supply to zero must use `burn_final` (`TOP_BURN_NOT_FULL_SUPPLY` names the kind to use). `burn_final`: requested only by A48's wind-down, units must equal the total supply exactly (`TOP_BURN_NOT_FULL_SUPPLY`), and the approval must carry the owner quorum of its row; a full-supply burn approved by the CFO alone is refused at submit by A14's policy row and again here before the custody hand-off.
- `forced_transfer` and `recovery`: evidence documents present for the request type (probate or court order hash, countersigned trustee instruction, KYCA re-verification record for a lost wallet; document ids and hashes read through the `EvidenceDocs` port, §7), a `lost_wallet` request only when A29's `RecoveryRequest` for that wallet is marked `unrecoverable` (`TOP_RECOVERY_NOT_FALLBACK`), holder cap after transfer, and the register impact preview (units before and after per holder, from A18) attached to the request so the checker sees what ADM-15 shows.
- Every precheck returns `pass`, `warn` or `fail` with a stable code and a fix-it sentence; a `fail` ends in `rejected` at submit and a `warn` must be acknowledged by the checkers (A14).

**4. Timelocks, waivers, veto and notices (SM-13)**

- `forced_transfer` and `recovery` enter `queued` for 48 h and `upgrade` for 72 h from `approved`, with `timelock_until` recorded both on the op and in the audit-anchored `token.op.queued` event; A15's P10 recomputes `executable_at` from that event, so an edit to the row cannot shorten it.
- Waiver: `POST /operations/:id/waive-timelock` (or `POST /transfer-requests/:id/waive-timelock`) creates a `court_order_waiver` request (CCO + CEO, `6E`) that must reference a court-order document hash; without one the request is refused with `TOP_WAIVER_NEEDS_COURT_ORDER`; any other role set is refused by A14's policy row; a request type outside the row's `waivable_types` is refused with `TOP_WAIVER_TYPE_NOT_WAIVABLE` (proposed seed: `lost_wallet` is not waivable; this is a proposal pending the HG-LEGAL and founder answer, not an SM-13 rule). A waiver is accepted in two places, both drawn or implied by SM-13: at `checker_approval`, a granted waiver moves the request `checker_approval → executing` (SM-13's waiver edge; no old-wallet freeze and no timelock on that path) and the op straight to `awaiting_signatures`; while the op is `queued` (SM-13 `timelock`), a granted waiver ends the timelock at once and the op moves `queued → awaiting_signatures`, after which the ordinary `timelock → executing` edge fires on the custody quorum. Either way the waiver is one `token.op.waived` audit event naming both approvers and the document hash, and A15's P10 recomputes `executable_at` from that audit-anchored event.
- Veto: any eligible checker or the trustee may veto during the window (A14's `veto`); this node then moves the op to `cancelled` and instructs A15 to refuse the approval id.
- Freezing the old wallet: for **every** request type the sequence is `checker_approval → old_wallet_frozen → timelock`, as SM-13 draws (the only exception is the waiver edge above). This node submits the `freeze` op for the old wallet first (its own approval under the request's id chain) and refuses to enter `timelock` until that freeze is `confirmed` (`TOP_OLD_WALLET_NOT_FROZEN`).
- Notice: on entering `timelock` the service asks the `HolderNotifier` port (§7) to notify the holder's old contact channels (email, SMS, in-app) with the earliest execution time; the desk shows "notice sent" only when the notifier confirms delivery attempts; execution before the notice is refused with `TOP_NOTICE_NOT_SENT`.

**5. Pause, unpause, halt and ratification (SM-06, G10)**

- `pause` is `SGL+R`: the actor submits with a hardware-key assertion, the request is approved in the same transaction (A14), the custody op goes to V3, one V3 signer who is not the actor signs, and the call broadcasts at once; `ratify_by = act + 2 h`. A lapsed ratification raises Sev2 `approval.ratification_missed` to CCO (A14 and A13) and opens an `unpause` request under the normal `6E+Q` row; **the token stays paused**. Restoring transfers always needs the V2 quorum, so `revert` for `pause` creates the request but never executes it; nothing silently unpauses.
- `unpause` never runs through V3 (`P2_RESTRICT_ONLY` in A15); this service routes it to V2 by the catalogue and refuses a request that names V3.
- While a token is `paused` the service sets the vehicle's G10 halt input for A48 (cause `token_pause`) and clears it on `unpause` confirmed; the marketplace-level `HaltModule` flag is a separate op requested by A48 with cause `manual`, `meeting`, `valuation_stale`, `recon_break` or `distress`.
- SM-06 transitions this node drives, each one audit event: `deploy_requested → deployed` (V1 quorum confirmed) → `configured` (registry, modules and agents set and R1 clean) → `issuing` (first mint confirmed) → `active` (transfers enabled by the offering's go-live); `active ↔ paused`; `active → upgrading → active`; `active → migrating → retired` (critical flaw; holders reissued under a new token through `mint` not-from-file rows); `active → redeeming → retired` (A48's wind-down `burn_final` equal to supply under the owner-quorum row, then A18's register close). `designed → sandbox_deployed → deploy_requested` belong to A27's token registry rows. `deploy_requested → deployed` requires the test report attached and the audit slot filled or marked `open (HG-AUDIT)`.

## Node-specific challenges (node QA)

- **Supply beyond the allocation.** A locked allocation of 9,100 units for Unley Road Cottage; a `batch_mint` of 9,100 approved and closed; then a second mint of 1 unit citing the same approval id, a corrective mint of 900 approved by the CFO alone, and a mint of 9,101 with a fresh file whose lines sum to 9,100. Each must be refused (`TOP_APPROVAL_CONSUMED`; the settled not-from-file row unmet, which under the proposed row is CFO + CCO + CEO and under the map's ADM-14 row is CFO + CCO, so a CFO-only approval fails under every candidate except the "Deliver tokens" custody-quorum-only reading, which Specify must rule out or restate before the node QA fixes the expected code; `TOP_SUPPLY_EXCEEDS_ALLOCATION`), `totalSupply` on the local chain must read exactly 9,100 and R2 and R7 must be clean. Any path that leaves supply at 9,101 fails the node.
- **Confirmed on chain, silent register.** A forced transfer of 12 units reaches `confirmed` and the sandbox registry is told to reject the transfer instruction: the op must go to `broken`, a Sev1 break must open through A20 with resale halted for the vehicle, ADM-14's payload must show the red pill, A14's request must stay open, and a call to A14's `closed` must be refused with `APR_NOT_RECONCILED`. An op that shows "Confirmed" or a request that closes fails the node.
- **Replay at every layer.** After a mint closes, the same approval id is presented (a) to `POST /operations`, (b) to A15 `submitOp` directly, (c) to the gateway on the local chain as the captured signed bundle, and (d) as a forged `Executed` log with a fresh `dataHash` fed to A27's indexer on the local chain, plus a forged `custody.op.confirmed` posted to this module's internal events route with a non-service credential and then with the service credential for an op whose custody op is not `confirmed`. (a) to (c) must be refused with their own codes; for (d) A27 must record the log as `unmatched`, A20's targeted run must raise its existing Sev1 break (R7 `qty` or R1 `role_drift`), the internal route must refuse both posts, and no op may advance. If any layer accepts, the AM2 exit is not met.
- **Mint register step is verify-only.** On the AM2 exit mint, the register's units per holder and `total_units` must be identical before and after the mint op (A38's F5 issue is the only register change), A18 must have received no instruction from `svc-tokenops` for that approval id, and the op must never enter `register_pending`. Then the sandbox registrar is made to report issued lines that differ by one unit from the minted lines: the op must go to `broken` with reason `register_mismatch` and never to `closed`.
- **Recovery and forced-transfer checkers.** A `lost_wallet` recovery approved by the CCO alone, then by the CCO and the CFO without the trustee, and a court-order forced transfer approved by the CCO and the trustee without the CFO: each must stay short of `old_wallet_frozen`, no freeze or custody op may be created for it, and the recovery must never reach V2 signing. The same cases pass only when CCO, then CFO and trustee, have approved, as the settled rows require.
- **Final burn quorum.** A48 requests a `burn_final` of the whole supply approved only by the CFO: refused, no custody op created, supply unchanged. With CFO plus three distinct V1 key holders (none of them the maker) it runs to `closed`, signed on V2 only; a `burn` (not `burn_final`) that would take supply to zero is refused with `TOP_BURN_NOT_FULL_SUPPLY`.
- **SM-13 through the API.** Using only HTTP routes and a LEGL staff token for the maker rows: create a court-order request, triage it, complete triage to `legal_review`, submit the legal review (agent allowed, audited "on behalf of"), then attempt `compliance-approval`, `checker-approval`, `reject` and `waive-timelock` with the token (each refused before scope evaluation with `APR_CHECKER_MUST_BE_HUMAN`); humans complete the approvals; the old wallet is frozen; the request enters `timelock`; an objection recorded by the agent moves it to `disputed` and cancels the op; `legal-review` from `disputed` returns it to `legal_review`. Every SM-13 transition must be observed through the API and the audit log.
- **Partial freeze.** A `LEGAL_HOLD` partial freeze of 100 units on `0x3a…e07` (the ADM-14 mock form) moves the holding `active → partially_frozen`; a `partial_unfreeze` of 101 is refused; a `partial_unfreeze` of 100 by the same human who made the freeze as checker is refused (SoD); a lock-up on the same holder is recorded as `locked` by reason, not as `partially_frozen`; a lock whose `unlockAt` passes on the injectable clock moves `locked → active` with no op and no signature.
- **Timelock and waiver.** A forced transfer approved 6E+Q at hour 0; a `sign` at hour 47 through A15; a waiver request without a court-order document; a waiver by CCO + CFO; and `timelock_until` moved back by 47 h in the database. The first four must be refused with the right code and the last must be caught because P10 recomputes from the `token.op.queued` audit event. A valid CCO + CEO waiver with a court-order hash on a court-order transfer must work at both points: granted at `checker_approval` it moves the request straight to `executing` (no freeze, no timelock); granted while `queued` it ends the timelock at once; each leaves one `token.op.waived` event naming both approvers. A `lost_wallet` recovery with a valid CCO + CEO court-order waiver has the outcome the settled `waivable_types` row gives: refused with `TOP_WAIVER_TYPE_NOT_WAIVABLE` under the proposed seed, accepted if Specify makes `lost_wallet` waivable; the node QA fixes the expected result only after that row is settled.
- **Pause and its lapse.** The CCO pauses Marion Street under `SGL+R`; the CCO then tries to sign the V3 op (refused, P9); nobody ratifies for 2 h: exactly one Sev2 alert, one `unpause` request created and the token still `paused` on chain and in SM-06; a forced transfer submitted while paused is refused with `TOP_TOKEN_PAUSED`, then accepted once a `token.paused_override` is approved; an `unpause` routed to V3 is refused with `P2_RESTRICT_ONLY`. A token that silently unpauses, or a second alert per poll, fails the node.
- **Register first and fewer than two signatures.** A mint whose second allocation line has a register entry of 39 units against 40 requested is refused naming that line; a custody status callback is tampered to report `signed` with one V2 signature, then with two signatures from the same signer: `broadcast` must refuse both with `TOP_BUNDLE_INVALID`, the op must stay `signed` and a Sev2 alert must reach SECO; nothing may reach A27.
- **Agent parity and SoD.** A TOKO staff token holding every staff scope drafts and submits a burn on behalf of TOKO (allowed, audited "on behalf of"), then calls `broadcast`, `waive-timelock` and A15's `approve` (each refused before scope evaluation, recorded as a G22 gate evaluation and emitted as the G22 anomaly event A101 consumes); the human TOKO who made the request is a V2 signer and their signature must not count; the seed's OP-7730 must read `signatures_have = 2`, `signatures_need = 2`, `signers_total = 3`, `vault_code = V2`, `state = signed`, `next_action = broadcast`; the seed's OP-7731 must read `signatures_have = 1`, `signatures_need = 2`, `signers_total = 3`, `state = awaiting_signatures` with `timelock_until` before the seed's as-of date, and a second distinct eligible V2 signature must flip it to `signed` in the same request, matching the prototype's Sign → Broadcast behaviour at n ≥ 2.

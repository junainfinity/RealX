# A31 - On-chain identity and claims service (V4 identity agent)

**The service that turns an approved investor account into on-chain eligibility and takes it away again, as the only caller of the V4 identity agent and the only submitter of the identity recovery key `R`: ONCHAINID creation through A24's `createInvestorIdentity` layout, `registerIdentity` (first wallet, an additional wallet, a J09 recovery wallet), `updateCountry` with A24's G06 country code, `deleteIdentity`, and claim add, remove and revoke, each under a per-function condition; an off-chain `IdentityClaim` mirror confirmed against the chain; the KYC, sanctions and residency/FIRB holding claims and the `WHOLESALE_AU` acquisition claim, all swept nightly; the wholesale claim executed from A32's outbox events under the claim contract written identically in both notes (A32 owns SM-02 and every wholesale decision); the `registry_status` vocabulary and labels, written to A29 through `setRegistryStatus`; the on-chain identity check behind A15's `IdentityRegistryReader` and A28's `IdentityLookup`; the identity snapshot A20's R4 and R5 read; and eligibility previews for residency changes and deletions.**

**Build status:** not started. **Milestone:** AM3 (Chain, identity and the money path). **Lane:** Chain. **Planner:** Opus 5.5 (high). **Builder:** Grok 4.7 (xhigh). **Node QA:** Opus 5.5 (high, every loop). **Graph QA:** Fable 5.1 (high, at milestone close).

**Oracle rule:** The node QA writes hidden oracle tests from this spec to `<workspace>\gauntlet\hidden\A31\` before the build starts ([[App Gauntlet]] §2). The builder never reads that folder; the cases are published into the repo only after the gate passes. The oracle scope: `isVerified` is true only for a wallet whose investor account meets §2's account condition (A30 has approved or whitelisted it, every required member is verified with KYC unexpired, no member is restricted, and an entity has no pending owner), and it turns false on deletion, on `KYC_VERIFIED` removal or expiry and on A30's `kyb.entity.owners_pending`; every V4 and `R` function runs only under its own binding from §1 (so every op works for a whitelisted investor, and no op runs without its source hash, sweep hash or approval ID); a replayed or forged source event after revocation puts no claim on chain; after `WHOLESALE_AU` expiry `isVerified` stays true, a mint or a transfer from another identity reverts on `AcquisitionClaim`, and a sale or a move to the holder's own wallet passes; the A$500k-in-this-offer path creates no standing wholesale claim, so token B cannot reach a ticket investor of offer A by transfer or mint on chain, and a forced transfer is refused off chain by A28's destination guard (a documented on-chain limit); a deleted identity cannot receive; the IR country is A24's G06 code (a citizen living abroad is `36`, a temporary resident is their nationality's code); a J09 recovery across two trusts runs under one A28 approval ID; the on-chain `validUntil` equals the end of the source's `valid_until` date in Sydney local time across leap days and the daylight-saving change; and the identity snapshot makes every planted R4/R5 drift visible to A20's functions.

This node is the bridge between the onboarding decisions (A30 owns the investor account, the `KYCCase`, KYB and SM-01; A32 owns the classification and SM-02) and the ERC-3643 identity layer that A24 ships and A27 deploys and indexes (`IdentityRegistryStorage`, `TrustedIssuersRegistry`, `ClaimTopicsRegistry`, `RealXClaimIssuer`, the ONCHAINID `IdFactory`, `AcquisitionClaimModule`; architecture brief §4.1). It never decides who is verified, who is wholesale or which G06 class someone is in; it executes, on chain, a decision that a human or an owner node's automatic path already made, and it refuses anything else. It runs on the local Hardhat chain and on Base Sepolia only; the production V4 hot key, the production recovery key `R`, the trusted-issuer registration on mainnet and the claim-topic design under the 2027 framework sit behind human gates. This plan is not legal advice.

**1. What the V4 identity agent and the recovery key may do, function by function**

- V4 is the `CustodyAccount` A15 seeds as quorum 0-of-0, hot, `SVCA` sole signer, with a per-minute rate limit and a daily count cap as data. `R` is the recovery key slot of §0 A15 (3). This node is the only caller of both: every call is a `CustodyOp` submitted through A15's `submitOp` with a reference `{ investor_id, source_kind, source_id, source_state_hash, approval_id?, reason_code? }`, where `investor_id` is A30's `INV-` account id and `source_state_hash` is the SHA-256 of the canonical source row as re-read immediately before submit. This node checks the condition first; A15's P1 re-reads the same source through `V4SourceReader` and refuses on any mismatch, so there are always two independent refusals.
- **Account condition** (used by several rows; read from A30's account eligibility record, never from a table): the account is `approved` (first registration only, since whitelisting follows it) or A30's `isWhitelisted` is true (`whitelisted`, or `refresh_due` with `refresh_reason = periodic` inside grace); the account's `kyc_valid_until` is the minimum over every member A30 requires to be verified (holders, trustees, directors, signatories, owners at 25 % or more, required controllers) and is in the future; the account is restricted if any such member's `restriction` is not `none`; `entityStatus` is `not_entity` or `verified` (not `owners_pending`); a joint account has both holders approved.
- Per-function bindings (proposed for A15's policy; §0):

- **Key layout** (A24 item 2, adopted unchanged): A31 calls A24's `createInvestorIdentity(wallet, salt)` with `salt = keccak(investor_id)`, which produces `createIdentityWithManagementKeys(wallet, salt, [R])` and then `R`'s `addKey(V4, 3, 1)` and `addKey(wallet, 1, 1)`. End state: the investor's passkey smart wallet (A26/A29) and `R` are management keys; V4 is a claim key only and can never add or remove a management key (A24 tests this). If the pinned tag only offers `createIdentity(wallet, salt)`, A24's fallback applies (the wallet's first transaction adds `R` and V4, inside A29's wallet creation) and A31 only verifies the key set before its first `addClaim`. `addClaim` and `removeClaim` are called on `Identity(investor)` by V4 as claim key; `RealXClaimIssuer` is the claim's issuer, signing with its own signing key (A24: the V4 identity-agent address locally, a custody-held key slot later). A `KeyRemoved` of V4's claim key by the investor's management key is detected by the snapshot (§5) and raised as A13 Sev2 `identity.claim_key_removed`; claims can then no longer be refreshed and the investor lapses as claims expire, which the alert explains to KYCA. **Who holds `R`:** the deployer key through the `kms` sandbox locally; a V1-controlled custody slot later, never V4 and never a person's own key (HG-KEYS).
- The service account: one `SVCA` principal row `svc-identity-agent`, no login, no API token, a key-custody statement in `README.md` ("V4 hot key and recovery key R held by the kms sandbox now, HSM or custody vendor after HG-KEYS, rotated every 90 days by A15's schedule"), caps (the rate limit and daily count cap as policy data), and a one-line disclosure string the investor-facing eligibility read returns on request ("RealX's identity service records your verification on the identity registry automatically once your checks pass"). Requirement from the OddX QA lesson on undisclosed service accounts (conventions §6 item 8).

**2. Identity registration and life-cycle events**

- Source: A30's query `GET /v1/internal/kyc/accounts?state=approved&unregistered=true`, which returns `INV-` account ids (the hand-off A30 ships; `kyc.case.approved` is an audit event, not a trigger), polled by the `claims.register-pending` job every 30 s, and re-queued at once on A29's `wallet.created`. For each account the job reads the account eligibility record through A30's public interface (never a table read), loads the investor's `passkey_aa` wallets through A29's `index.ts`, and refuses with `CLM_NO_WALLET` (retried on `wallet.created`) when none exists; ONB-08 shows "Created" before "Linked", so an approved account without a wallet is a valid state.
- **`registry_status` is this node's vocabulary, written only through A29.** The five values are {`unregistered`, `registering`, `registered`, `stale`, `deregistered`}. A31 calls A29's `setRegistryStatus(walletId, status, { txHash, event })`: `registering` when the registration op is submitted; `registered` on the confirmed `IdentityRegistered` event; `stale` when the wallet is still in the IR but `isVerified` has turned false on the holding topics (a holding claim expired, was removed or revoked, `owners_pending`, or V4's claim key was removed), set by the sweep or the confirmation that caused it; `stale → registered` when a replacement claim confirms and `isVerified` is true again; `deregistered` on the confirmed `IdentityRemoved` event. The three label strings are this node's, placed in A03's `labels.ts` (§0): "Linked" for `registered`; "Pending identity review" while the account's gating case is in manual or EDD review, or the status is `stale`; "Not yet linked" for `unregistered`, `registering` and `deregistered` otherwise. `GET /v1/me/eligibility` returns the status and its label, so no client carries a rule.
- Execution: create the ONCHAINID if absent (§1 key layout), then `registerIdentity(wallet, identity, country)` with `country = g06CountryCode(investorId)` from A30 (A24 item 2: `36` for anyone who is not a foreign person wherever they live, otherwise the nationality or controlling-jurisdiction code; never the residence address), and `KYC_VERIFIED`, `SANCTIONS_CLEAR` and `RESIDENCY_FIRB` claims in the same batch where A27's adapter allows, otherwise the next op. A `null` code (entity `foreign_person = pending`) refuses with `CLM_SOURCE_NOT_VALID` naming G06. Batches of up to 50 registrations go through `batchRegisterIdentity` under one `CustodyOp`.
- Confirmation: nothing moves on submit except `registering`. After A15's lifecycle reaches `confirmed`, the worker confirms `IdentityRegistry.contains(wallet)` and `identity(wallet)` at the confirmed block through A27's transport, calls `setRegistryStatus(..., registered, { txHash, event: IdentityRegistered })`, then publishes `identity.registered { investor_id, wallet, chain_id, identity_address, tx_hash }` on the outbox; A30 stores `onchain_identity`. When A15's op reaches `reconciled`, A31 calls A30's `POST /v1/internal/investors/{id}/transition` for `approved → whitelisted` (first registration only; A30 re-checks the joint and G03 invariants) and asserts that A27's `Holding` read model shows `eligible_empty` for that wallet on every token bound to the shared storage. It writes no `KYCCase`, `Wallet` or `Holding` row; the `registry_status` field is written only through A29's function.
- A30's `investor.refreshed` (refresh case approved): no second registration; `replaceClaim` for `KYC_VERIFIED` with the new account `kyc_valid_until`, and for `RESIDENCY_FIRB` when its validity is tied to the review date. A30's `investor.suspended`: no chain change; the eligibility read shows `suspended`. A30's `kyc.sanctions.true_match`: A31 removes and issuer-revokes `SANCTIONS_CLEAR` bound to the event's `SGL+R` `approval_id`; the wallet freeze is A42/A28's through V2 (A31 does not consume `investor.freeze_requested`; the A30 text is corrected in §0). A30's `investor.residency.changed`: §4.
- **Entity owners (G03).** On A30's `kyb.entity.owners_pending`, A31 removes the entity identity's `KYC_VERIFIED` claim under §1's `AUTO` owners-pending row (the hash of A30's re-read `entityStatus`), so `isVerified` is false and no mint or transfer can reach the entity's wallets on chain; holdings stay and can be sold. On `kyb.entity.owners_verified` it re-issues `KYC_VERIFIED` under the `addClaim` row once the account condition holds again (idempotent on the source hash, so a concurrent `investor.refreshed` issues one claim, not two). A19's G03 refuses orders off chain at once; the chain follows within one V4 op.
- **J09 token-level recovery.** A29's `wallet.created` for an investor who already has an ONCHAINID, with an `unrecoverable` recovery request naming that wallet, triggers §1's recovery-registration row: `registerIdentity(new, existingIdentity, lostCountry)`, which lets A29's `RECOVERY_NEW_WALLET_UNREGISTERED` guard clear. After A28's recovery approval, A31 executes `R`'s `addKey(new, 1, 1)` and `removeKey(lost, 1)` under that `approval_id`; after A28's forced transfers leave `balanceOf(lost) == 0` on every token, A31 executes `deleteIdentity(lost)` under the same `approval_id` with reason `wallet_recovery`, and sets the lost wallet `deregistered`. The vendored `recoveryAddress` is never used (A24 item 3a). A second device wallet with no recovery request uses the ordinary registration row.
- Offboarding: A31 never gates SM-01's `suspended → offboarded`. When a deletion confirms, A31 publishes `identity.deleted`; A30 applies `offboarded` under its own conditions, including cases where holdings are frozen and the identity stays registered.

**3. Claims: topics, the mirror, confirmation, the claim contract with A32**

**4. Residency change and deletion**

- **Residency change: one approval, executed here.** A30 runs `investor.residency.change` (`4E`, KYCA maker → CCO checker) and shows the impact from `POST /v1/eligibility/preview` before the checker decides: for every token the investor holds or has an open order in, `CountryAllowModule` under the new G06 code, A19's G06 result and the foreign-percentage monitor effect. On A30's `investor.residency.changed { investor_id, approval_id, new_country, residency_status, firb_ref?, firb_valid_until?, cause }`, A31 re-reads `g06CountryCode` and builds one `CustodyOp` bound to that `approval_id` (or, for `cause = kyb_recompute`, to the `AUTO` hash of A30's recomputed entity record): `updateCountry` for every registered wallet of the investor plus `replaceClaim` of `RESIDENCY_FIRB`. There is no A31 route for residency change and no second request. Holdings are never touched: a token that no longer allows the code keeps the holding (`active`), inbound transfers and mints fail on chain, and A19's monitor raises the concentration alert through A13. An order in `eligibility_check` re-runs its gates through A19's hook.
- **Deletion and early claim removal.** `POST /v1/identities/{investorId}/deletion-requests` (M07 request, maker MLRO or CCO) drafts A14's `4E` request on A03's row "Revoke on-chain eligibility" with `variant = delete_identity`; `POST /v1/identities/{investorId}/claim-removal-requests` drafts the same row with `variant = remove_claim` and a topic name in {`KYC_VERIFIED`, `SANCTIONS_CLEAR`, `RESIDENCY_FIRB`}. Reason code mandatory (`misrepresentation`, `sanctions_true_match`, `kyc_failed`, `court_order`, `investor_request`, `error`); `wallet_recovery` is not draftable here and exists only on the J09 execution under A28's approval (§2). On approval V4 executes `deleteIdentity(wallet)` per wallet; after confirmation A31 calls `setRegistryStatus(..., deregistered, { txHash, event: IdentityRemoved })`, publishes `identity.deleted` (A30 decides on `offboarded`) and asserts A27's `Holding` shows `deregistered`. - Both paths are audited twice by design: the approval's events (A14) and this node's execution events carrying the same `approval_id`.

**5. Nightly sweep, sanctions restamp, lapse alerts, the identity snapshot**

- `claims.sweep-expired` (worker, pg-boss cron 02:00 Australia/Sydney, A08's injected `Clock`), the one expiry sweep on the platform for all four claim topics: (1) selects `issued` rows whose `onchain_valid_until` has passed, batches them by investor, submits one V4 `AUTO` `removeClaim` op per batch with the sorted claim id list as the hash input, moves each row `→ expired` after confirmation, sets `stale` on affected wallets and publishes `claim.expired`; (2) moves `pending` and `off_chain_only` rows whose `valid_until` has passed to `expired` with no chain call and publishes `claim.expired`; (3) publishes `claim.expiring` for `WHOLESALE_AU` rows (not `s708_500k_offer`) whose `valid_until` is 60 or fewer, or 30 or fewer, Sydney days away and whose flag for that mark is unset, then sets the flag (so a missed night catches up once, never twice). The chain already treats expired claims as invalid; the sweep keeps the identity clean, the mirror true and SM-02 moving. Idempotent: a re-run the same night makes no chain call, publishes nothing and writes one `claims.sweep.completed` audit event with counts. It never fires an SM-02 transition itself, never deletes an identity and never freezes a holding.
- `claims.sanctions-restamp` (worker, cron 04:30 Australia/Sydney, after A42's 03:00 rescreen): for investors whose `SANCTIONS_CLEAR` claim ends within `sanctions_restamp_window_days` (policy data, default 7), reads the latest result through the `ScreeningResultReader` port (A31 owns the port and a fixture provider for tests; A42 registers the real one, §0) and, when it is `clear` and newer than the claim's source, runs `replaceClaim`. About one in four investors per week is re-stamped, well inside V4's rate limit. With no provider registered the port fails closed (`CLM_PROVIDER_MISSING`): nothing is re-stamped and the lapse alert below fires days before any claim ends, so an outage is an operations incident with time to fix it, not a silent mass de-verification.
- Alerts through A13 (policy data): `identity.claim_sweep_lag` Sev3 when a claim past `onchain_valid_until` is still present on chain after `sweep_lag_alert_hours` (default 6); `identity.claim_lapse_imminent` Sev2 when a `KYC_VERIFIED` or `SANCTIONS_CLEAR` claim ends within 3 days with no replacement queued (the rescreen or refresh path has stalled); `identity.claim_key_removed` Sev2 (§1); `identity.internal_call_refused` Sev3 (§6).
- An expired `WHOLESALE_AU` (removed by the sweep) leaves `isVerified` true, because topic 3 is an acquisition topic, not a holding topic: the investor can hold, sell to another eligible identity and move units between their own wallets; a mint or a transfer from another identity reverts on `AcquisitionClaim`, after A19's G04 has already refused it off chain.
- **R4 and R5 are A20's.** This node supplies facts only: `identityReconSnapshot({ atBlock })` returns `{ block_number, ir_members[] { address, onchain_id, country }, claims[] { onchain_id, topic_name, topic_id, issuer, issuer_trusted, valid_until (decoded from the claim data), revoked (from the issuer) }, identity_keys[] { onchain_id, v4_claim_key_present, r_management_key_present } }` read directly from the chain through A27's transport (`contains`, `identity`, `investorCountry`, `getClaimIdsByTopic`, `getClaim`, `isClaimRevoked`, `isTrustedIssuer`), cross-checked against the mirror. The `claims.recon-snapshot` job (owned worker path) writes one snapshot row after every V4 or `R` op confirmation and at A20's nightly window; A20's `ChainSnapshotSource` (registered by A27) reads `ir_members` and `claims` from it (§0). A31 opens no breaks and ships no recon functions.

# A69 - Console: sponsor admission, licences, sponsors and guardrails, feature flags, entities register

**The five operator-console screens that decide who may list on RealX and what RealX is allowed to switch on: ADM-03 Sponsor admission (the pipeline table, the application detail and the approvals chain from "applied" to "tenant created", every step two-person), ADM-33 Sponsors and guardrails (tenant list and detail, versioned guardrails with "KYC required · Locked on" that no form can change, suspension as a protective act with its 2-hour ratification pill), ADM-39 Entities register and detail (every company, trustee, RE, custodian and firm with people, documents and masked personal data), ADM-04 Licences and permissions (licence table, CAR register, activity-to-flag map, DAF Act exemption meter, T-90/30/7 expiry alerts) and ADM-34 Feature flags and kill switches (flags with their licence binding and state, a refused toggle that says why, the kill-switch banner with the server-timed 2-hour ratification countdown, the time-boxed halt form with reopening criteria and an end date, and the launch-state check). The screens draw what A33, A21 and A48 decide and never decide anything themselves; every action is an SDK operation an agent can call too, under the same gates.**

**Build status:** not started. **Milestone:** AM7 (Operator console). **Lane:** Operator console. **Planner:** Opus 5.5 (high). **Builder:** Grok 4.7 (xhigh). **Node QA:** Opus 5.5 (high, every loop). **Graph QA:** Fable 5.1 (high, at milestone close).
**Estimate:** 8-12 agent-hours (build, challenge and verify loops).

**Depends on:** [[A53 - Operator console shell, hardware-key sign-in and approval action pattern]], [[A33 - Sponsor tenancy, entities, licences of sponsors and admission pipeline]], [[A21 - Licences, feature flags and licence perimeter (G21)]], [[A51 - Design extension B- operator console screens not in the prototype]], [[A48 - Halts, kill switches and wind-down]]
**Also uses (no graph edge yet; the edges are requested in `final-graph.json`, see §9 (m)):** [[A06 - Seed data and scenario simulator]] (seed, personas and scenario fixtures), [[A98 - Partner principals- trustee-RE, valuer, property manager, auditor, regulator observer and licensee users]] (the real TP-LIC persona), [[A101 - Agent guard- anomaly detection, sensitive-action notices and tiered kill switches]] (the tenant-tier agent hold shown on ADM-33) and [[A104 - MCP server generated from the OpenAPI document]] (MCP tool names in `parity.ts`).
**Unlocks:** [[A78 - Operator journey J20 end to end in Docker (AM7 exit)]], [[A80 - End-to-end journeys- sponsor and operations]]

**Oracle rule:** this is not an oracle node (`oracle: no`): no money, supply or holdings move on these screens, although what they switch on decides whether money may move elsewhere. The node QA still writes hidden tests and Challenge fixtures from this frozen spec to `<workspace>\gauntlet\hidden\A69\` before the build starts ([[App Gauntlet]] §2): Playwright specs against the running Docker stack with A11's sandbox authenticator and A06's seed, its own crops of the extension-B frames for ADM-03, ADM-04, ADM-33, ADM-34 and ADM-39, a request-tampering fixture (a devtools-style proxy that rewrites form payloads, response bodies and the browser clock), a two-console race fixture, and the persona sets for maker-is-checker, agent-as-maker and view-as-tenant. The builder never reads that folder, and the tests are published into `app/` only after the gate passes.

This note is not legal advice. Every threshold these screens show (the DAF Act low-value exemption A$10m and A$5k per client, the 10 percent retained stake, the 30-day billing pause, the sprawl count, the halt maximum days) is a data row owned by A21, A33 or A48 and is rendered with its "counsel to confirm" or "proposed" caption; the console never hard-codes one.

## Build brief

**1. What this node is, what it reuses, and what it may not add**

- This node is UI only: pages under `apps/console/app/(platform)/` and feature code under `apps/console/src/features/sponsors-perimeter/`. It adds no endpoint, no table, no job, no rule and no audit write. Data comes only through `packages/sdk` (A09's generated client; the console's lint rule forbids any other `fetch`), from the contracts frozen upstream: A33's `tenancy` and `entities` modules (applications, checks, tenants, guardrails, pauses, users, fee schedules, entities, vehicles, sponsor licence rows), A21's `perimeter` module (licences, CAR appointments, flags, bindings, visibility, exemption meter), and A48's `halts` module (kill switches, halts, runbooks). Approvals are A14's, reached only through A53's `PrivilegedActionFlow` (draft, precheck, submit, diff and simulation, evidence, SM-00 status card) and `HardwareKeyConfirm` (approve, reject, ratify, act); A53's lint rule forbids this node from importing the SDK's `approvals.create`, `approvals.submit`, `approvals.decide`, `ratify`, `veto` or `auth.freshAssertion` operations directly, and this node passes the SDK call it needs into those two components as a callback.
- Shell primitives are A53's and are reused, never copied: `ConsolePage` (mono code eyebrow, Newsreader 40 h1, subtitle, KpiGrid, content at `max-width 1280`), `ConsoleTable` (server cursor paging, URL-synced filters, loading, empty and error states with `request_id`, `invalid_cursor` reset), `StatusCell`, `IdCell`, `MoneyCell`, `WhenCell`, `MaskedCell`, `AgentMarker`, the view-as-tenant banner, `registerBadgeSource` and `registerSearchProvider`. Visual blocks are A05's catalogue plus A51's extension-B blocks in `packages/ui/src/ext/` (`SplitListDetail`, `ApprovalsChain`, `ExemptionMeter`, `MaskedField` and `UnmaskButton`, the kill-switch band). A block that is missing is requested from A51 as a follow-up loop; nothing here is drawn in raw hex (A05's lint rule runs over this folder).
- Every status string, tone, reason code and label on these screens is a field the API returns (`state` plus `display_tone`, `toggle_blocked_reason`, `release_blocked_reason`, the error envelope's `code`, `gate` and `reason_codes`). The console maps codes to sentences through one table per upstream module (`copy/tenancy.ts`, `copy/perimeter.ts`, `copy/halts.ts`) generated from the OpenAPI error catalogue at build time, and a test fails if any error code of A33, A21 or A48 has no sentence. The console computes no eligibility, no percentage of the meter, no "is this flag allowed on", no countdown from the browser clock, and no count by paging a list.
- If a field or route a screen needs is missing from an upstream contract at Specify, the builder does not invent it: it records the gap in `app/evidence/A69.qa.md` and requests a follow-up loop on the owning node (A33.1, A21.1, A48.1). The spec points that are already known are listed in §9.

**2. Routes, page contract and navigation**

- Routes (the console has its own origin, so the product map's `/admin` prefix is dropped): `/sponsors/admission` and `/sponsors/admission/[applicationId]` (ADM-03), `/tenants` and `/tenants/[tenantId]` (ADM-33), `/entities` and `/entities/[entityId]` (ADM-39), `/entities/licences` (ADM-04, the product map's route), `/flags` (ADM-34). All live in the `(platform)` route group this node owns; ADM-03 and ADM-39 sit in the sidebar's "Onboarding & listings" group and ADM-04, ADM-33 and ADM-34 in "Platform", which is A53's `nav.ts` data (the entries already exist with the `soon` pill; landing these pages replaces the placeholders without editing `nav.ts`). Each page exports `screen` (`ADM-03`, `ADM-33`, `ADM-39`, `ADM-04`, `ADM-34` from A02's `screens.ts`) and `designKey` (`design-ext:ADM-03` and so on).
- Visibility follows A11's `GET /iam/me`: a page renders only for a caller with `V` on its module (M02 for ADM-03, ADM-04 and ADM-39; M27 for ADM-33 and ADM-34); action buttons render only for callers whose effective cells include the drafting action, and the approve, ratify and act slots render only where the API's detail response lists the caller as an eligible checker or actor. The product map's role lists (ADM-03 PART, KYCA, CCO, LEGL; ADM-04 CCO, LEGL, SUPER, TP-LIC; ADM-33 PART, SUPER, CCO, LEGL; ADM-34 SUPER, CCO, CEO, CFO, plus SECO for the kill-switch section because A48 names CCO or SECO as actors) are the expected result of the matrix, asserted by a generated test over the seeded personas; a mismatch is a spec defect on A03's matrix, not a console override. Partner principals (TP-LIC, TP-OBS, TP-AUD) are A98's, built in AM3 before this node (the graph edge is requested in §9 (m)): the ADM-04 read is proven with A98's seeded TP-LIC persona, which must see every table and no action. Only if A98's persona is absent at Specify does the test fall back to A11's fixture PARTNER principal holding `licences:read`, and the gap is recorded in evidence.
- Deep links: every id (`SPA-`, `TEN-`, `ENT-`, `LIC-`, CAR appointment ids, `VEH-`, `HLT-`, `APR-`) is an `IdCell` that links to its page; an `APR-` id opens the ADM-30 detail (A66) or, before A66 lands, the `PrivilegedActionFlow` status card inline. The ADM-29 "Open sponsor" link (A76) and the ADM-01 emergency-pause deep link (A66) land on `/tenants/[tenantId]` and `/flags#kill-switches`.

**3. ADM-03 Sponsor admission (M02; J11 steps 2 and 4)**

- Layout is extension B's ADM-03 frame on A51's `SplitListDetail`: left, the pipeline `ConsoleTable` over A33's `GET /v1/admin/sponsor-applications` with the product-map columns Sponsor / KYB / Licence / Background / Keeps / Status plus Owner and Age from the extension frame (filters by stage, owner and age; stage pills from A33's `display_tone`: `applied`, `kyb`, `background`, `info_requested` amber, `fee_agreement` brick, `approved` and `tenant_created` green, `declined` red, `withdrawn` grey); right, the application detail from `GET /v1/admin/sponsor-applications/{id}`: a `KeyValueList` (entity, ABN or ACN masked, AFSL or hired RE with the licence row's verification state, directors and owners at 25 percent or more with verified pills from the linked A30 KYB cases, track record as planned versus actual labelled "sponsor-declared", references, retained-stake commitment against the guardrail default), the evidence documents as `DocumentRow`s, the checks as `GateRow`s (licence, fit-and-proper and background, references, track record, retained stake, fee agreement, each with its result and checker), and the `ApprovalsChain` built from the application's state and its approval requests: PART review → KYCA verifies people → CCO approves KYB (`sponsor.kyb.approve`, `4E`) → tenant created with guardrails (`tenant.create`, `4E`). Chain card states are the API's (`DONE`, `YOUR TURN`, `WAITING`, `BLOCKED` with the reason, never a client inference).
- Actions, each an SDK call into A33, drafted and submitted through `PrivilegedActionFlow` where A33 makes it an approval request and posted directly where A33 makes it a single-person act: open KYB (`POST /v1/admin/sponsor-applications/{id}/kyb/open`, PART), record a background check with evidence document ids and a result (PART), request documents or information with a coded list (PART or KYCA; the application moves to `info_requested` and the chain shows who owes what), decline with a mandatory reason code (PART or KYCA; the reason-code list comes from A33's catalogue), attach the fee schedule and terms at `fee_agreement` (PART), draft `sponsor.kyb.approve` (KYCA maker; the CCO decides with `HardwareKeyConfirm`), and "Create tenant" (§4). The submit of `sponsor.kyb.approve` shows A33's precheck result as `GateRow`s; `APPLICATION_KYB_INCOMPLETE`, `APPLICATION_LICENCE_UNVERIFIED` and `APPLICATION_FEE_AGREEMENT_MISSING` render as the design's inline `Banner` with the code and the missing items, and the chain card turns `BLOCKED` with the extension frame's copy ("G03 · 1 owner unverified").
- G21 at admission: the licence check row shows the `GateEvaluation` A33's licence-check path wrote through A21's `evaluateG21` (`enforced_at = admission`, result, reason codes, licence version ids) with a link to the licence on ADM-04. The console reads it and never calls `POST /v1/perimeter/evaluate` to decide anything.
- Sprawl signal: a tenant whose vehicle count exceeds the guardrail `max_vehicles` shows the open A13 alert `sponsor.sprawl` as an amber chip on its application and tenant rows ("Sprawl signal · 21 trusts · review"), read from the alerts summary A53's shell already consumes; the chip never blocks anything because A33 makes it a signal.
- Tipping-off: an application declined with reason `kyb_failed` shows exactly that class and a link to the KYB cases on ADM-10 for roles A30 lets see them; no hit detail, list name or match score appears on ADM-03 for any role, because A30 keeps those on the need-to-know audit path and ADM-25.
- States (the extension frame's set): default (the seed's three rows: Kestrel Lane Property `approved`, Saltbush Commercial `fee_agreement`, Wattle & Stone Living `kyb` with 2 of 3 directors verified, where the unverified director also holds 30 percent and is therefore the one owner at 25 percent or more who is unverified, so the chain copy "G03 · 1 owner unverified" and the pipeline cell "3 · 2 verified" describe the same person; the extension frame's "Yarra Bend Partners at background" row is drawn from a scenario fixture, not the standard seed), blocked (an owner not yet verified), not eligible (PART viewing the CCO stage: the approve slot is absent and the chain card reads "Waiting for CCO"), info requested, declined with reapply date, empty ("No sponsor applications." in the dashed empty box), loading and error.

**4. Create tenant (shared by ADM-03 and ADM-33; J11 step 5)**

- One component, `CreateTenantFlow`, rendered on an `approved` application's detail (ADM-03) and from the "Create tenant" button on ADM-33's list, which opens a picker of `approved` applications without a tenant. It drafts A33's `tenant.create` (`4E`, PART or SUPER maker, CCO checker) through `PrivilegedActionFlow` with the tenant fields A33's effector takes (legal name, short name and slug, plan, the fee schedule copied from the application) and shows guardrails version 1 as a read-only preview of the platform defaults A33 returns, with "KYC required · Locked on" as a locked row and no input at all for it.

**5. ADM-33 Sponsors and guardrails (M27, M02; J11 step 5)**

- List: `ConsoleTable` over A33's tenant list with the product-map columns Sponsor / Status / Listings / Investors / Plan plus Open pauses (the `tenant_listing_pause` reasons as pills) and Sprawl; filters by status and pause reason. The seed shows Harbour & Co Property, Northline Capital and Yarra Bend Partners, `active`.
- Detail (`/tenants/[tenantId]`, extension B's KeyValueList cards): **Tenant** card (status with tone, admitted on and the admitting approval id, listings, investors, users with "all 2FA" derived by A33 from passkey and identity state, plan, and a read-only **Agent traffic (tenant tier)** row from A101's `GET /v1/agent-guard/switches?scope=tenant&subject={tenantId}`: state (engaged or clear), `source` (`staff`, `sponsor_owner` or `tenant_suspension`), `ratification_state` with its countdown, and the link "Manage on ADM-35", because A77 owns the actions and this row is the same source of truth ADM-35 and ADM-34 read); **Guardrails** card (current version, effective from, approval id, and the fields `kyc_required` shown as "KYC required · Locked on", whitelisting mode, eligibility packs, holder-cap ceiling, investor fee cap, sponsor management fee cap, allowed jurisdictions, rails, max vehicles, retained stake minimum, branding, listing visibility mode, each with A33's plain-English label); **Versions** (every guardrail version with its approval id, readable after it is superseded, with a field-by-field diff between any two versions); **Approval rules** (the tenant's current `tenant_approval_rule` versions against the platform minimums, read-only; weakening requests arrive in the ADM-30 inbox for the CCO as A33 defines); **Users** (members with role labels "Sponsor owner", "Operator", "Fund accountant", "Investor relations" and the A98 valuer and property-manager roles when present, state, 2FA, joined); **Billing** (plan lines, current fee schedule version with effective date and notice, invoices with `paid` and `due <date>` pills, open `billing_overdue` pause, and a link "Change on ADM-34" to the fee-schedule editor, §8); **Pauses** (every open and cleared listing pause with reason, set by, set at, cleared at).

The fee-schedule change is not drafted here: A33 makes `tenant.fee_schedule.set` a CFO → CEO request and the product map places "Change fee schedule" on ADM-34, whose role list carries the CFO, so the Billing card links to that editor (§8). "Hold listings" and "Release hold" draft A33's `tenant.listing_pause.set` for the `watchlist_hold` reason (`4E`, PART maker, CCO checker). "Suspend tenant" is the protective act A33 defines as `tenant.suspend` (`SGL+R`): a `danger` button opening the act form (reason code, the extension frame's warning "This revokes every sponsor session and every sponsor agent token now; approved distribution runs, live offers and investors' holdings are untouched"), then `HardwareKeyConfirm` with purpose `act`; the success toast appears only after the API's 2xx, and the card turns to the extension frame's suspended state (grey card with the ratify pill "Ratify by 14:32 · CEO, SECO or LEGL", counting down from the server's `ratify_by` through the shared `useServerClock()` helper, §9 (i)). After the reinstatement closes, the Agent traffic (tenant tier) row still reads engaged with source `tenant_suspension` until A101's separate `4E` release, and the console never shows it cleared before A101's read does.
- A suspended tenant whose ratification lapsed stays suspended and shows the red "Unratified" pill and A14's Sev2 alert link; there is no button that lifts a suspension other than "Reinstate".
- View as tenant: under A53's view-as-tenant grant the list contains only the acting tenant (A04's RLS through A33), the banner stays visible, and no guardrail, pause or suspension action renders, because staff act on tenants as staff, never through the sponsor view.

The covering licence and its state must be fields of A21's read; the console does not search the licence list for authorisations. If A21's read does not return them at Specify, the rows show `daf_status` only and the gap is requested as A21.1 (spec point (n)).
- States: default (seed: licensee AFSL and CAR `active`, the three sponsors' licences matching the design facts "Own AFSL" for Harbour & Co and Northline and "Trustee: hired RE" for Yarra Bend, no market licence, the hired-RE P2 row absent until HG-LEGAL supplies one, DAP and TCP at `low_value_exemption` with no licence), T-7 expiry, meter over 80 percent, a lapsed binding, empty register (every G21 evaluation fails closed and the page says so), loading and error.

**8. ADM-34 Feature flags and kill switches (M27; J20 step 12, J21 step 2)**

- Flags table: `ConsoleTable` over A21's `GET /v1/flags` with the columns Flag / Scope / Licence binding / State exactly as A21 fixes them, plus phase, kind, `auto_reverted_at` with `auto_revert_reason`, and `changed_by_approval_id` as an `IdCell`. Keys are whatever A08's flag table holds (A21's seven plus A48's `platform.marketplace_suspend`), rendered from the response, never from a list in the console. A flag with `toggle_allowed: false` shows a disabled switch with the reason sentence (extension B's blocked state "No licence binding · cannot turn on (N10, G21)" for `G21_NO_BINDING`; "Not before P2" for `G21_PHASE_NOT_REACHED`); a flag auto-reverted by A21's binding sweep shows a red "Auto-reverted · licence suspended · 10:00" line; kill-switch rows show the red "ENGAGED" pill instead of on or off. "Toggle" drafts A21's `flag.toggle` (SUPER maker, CCO checker, `4E`) with A21's precheck report; "History" opens `GET /v1/flags/{key}/history`.
- Refetch policy (one rule for this page and ADM-04): the flags, kill-switch, halt and launch-state queries refetch on A53's 30-second tick and on window focus, and are invalidated at once after any licence, CAR, binding, flag, kill-switch or halt write on these screens returns 2xx. No query on these screens has a stale time longer than the tick.

## Node-specific challenges (node QA)

- **KYC off from the console (compliance, G03).** The node QA opens "Set guardrails" as CCO, adds `kyc_required: false` to the request through the proxy, and submits; then it edits the guardrail diff response to show "KYC required · Off" and approves as LEGL. The first must come back `GUARDRAIL_LOCKED` rendered as a banner with no request created; the second must still show "Locked on" after the reload because the page re-reads the version from the API after the decision; the DOM must contain no input, select or toggle bound to `kyc_required` on any page. Any request id created, or "Off" rendered after reload, fails the node.
- **A flag on without a licence (compliance, G21).** As SUPER it removes the `disabled` attribute on the `offers.retail` switch and submits a `flag.toggle` draft. Then, from the seed where `offers.wholesale` is already on, it makes the sequence explicit: a SUPER draft to turn it on must render `FLAG_ALREADY_IN_STATE`; a SUPER draft turns it off and a CCO approves (`4E`); a SUPER draft turns it back on and the deputy CCO approves; then, in a second tab, the CCO suspends the licensee licence row that the `offers.wholesale` binding names (Example Licensee Pty Ltd's AFSL) under `SGL+R`. A21 already specifies that this lapses the binding and makes `car_active` false, because the binding names both the CAR appointment and the licensee licence. The forced toggle must render `G21_NO_BINDING` and create nothing that can be approved; within one 30-second refetch tick after the suspension (at once in the tab that suspended, because the write invalidates the queries) the flags table must show `offers.wholesale` off with "Auto-reverted · suspended", the launch card must turn Fail with the reason, and the public disclosure link must show `car_active: false`. A page that keeps showing "on" from a cached query fails.
- **Kill switch timing and the unratified switch (security, G13).** CCO engages the inflow freeze; the node QA moves the browser clock forward three hours and then back five: the countdown must follow `ratify_by` against `server_now` through `useServerClock()`, not the browser; the same CCO tries to ratify (disabled with the SoD note; a forced call renders `APR_MAKER_IS_CHECKER`); the node QA lets the 2 hours lapse under the injectable clock: the band must show red "Unratified", the switch must remain engaged, and no control may release it except the `4E` "Release" with criteria. For the engaging CCO the Release button is disabled with the `KS_ACTOR_CANNOT_RELEASE` sentence, and a forced release draft by her, or by her agent token, returns `KS_ACTOR_CANNOT_RELEASE`; the deputy CCO drafts the release and the CEO approves it. A CCO agent token calling the engage route directly gets `AGNT_NOT_ALLOWED` on the API response, and no switch changes (the `agents.traffic` `self_subject` refusal is A77's challenge). In the `p2-board-ratification` fixture the band also shows the board pill counting down from `board_ratify_by`.
- **A halt that could drift (compliance, J21).** It opens a halt with no criteria, then with criteria and no end date, then with an end date in the past and one 90 days out, bypassing the disabled button through the proxy each time: every attempt must render A48's code verbatim and no `HLT-` id may exist; a valid halt must show its criteria, end date, ratification countdown and later `review_due` exactly as `GET /v1/halts` returns them, and no screen text for the holder notice may differ from `haltNotice()`.
- **The maker approves through a second route (compliance, G13).** A KYCA's agent token drafts `sponsor.kyb.approve`; the KYCA opens ADM-03 and the chain card must show the agent marker and no approve slot for her; a PART's agent drafts `tenant.create` and the PART, now also granted CCO for the test, must see a disabled approve with the SoD note because the agent counts as its human; a CCO who is the maker of a guardrail change is granted LEGL mid-flight and must still be refused. A console that enables any of these buttons or hides the refusal fails.
- **Tenant creation around an unverified owner (compliance, G03).** Wattle & Stone Living has 2 of 3 directors verified; the node QA drafts `tenant.create` from ADM-33's picker and, separately, tries to open the ADM-03 "Create tenant" button by URL: the picker must not list the application, the forced draft must render `APPLICATION_ILLEGAL_TRANSITION` (the application is in `kyb`, not `approved`), a forced `sponsor.kyb.approve` draft must render `APPLICATION_KYB_INCOMPLETE`, the chain must show `BLOCKED` "G03 · 1 owner unverified", and no tenant row may appear on ADM-33.
- **Suspension against a live sponsor session and agent (security).** With Harbour & Co's SP-FIN signed in and its agent mid-call on a second API process, CCO suspends the tenant on ADM-33: the success toast must appear only after the 2xx, the card must turn to the suspended state with the ratify pill, the tenant's users must show `suspended`, the Agent traffic (tenant tier) row must read engaged with source `tenant_suspension`, and "Reinstate" must say tokens are not restored and the agent hold stays; after the CEO approves the reinstatement the tenant is `active` and the tenant-tier row still reads engaged until A101's release on ADM-35; under view-as-tenant for Northline, Harbour & Co's card must never appear.

# A30 - Investor onboarding, KYC cases and KYB (SM-01)

**The person, their investor accounts and the one KYC that works for every sponsor: one reusable person identity per human, any number of `INV-` investor accounts per person (individual, joint with an either-or-both signing rule, SMSF, company, trust), citizenship, visa status, ordinary residence and tax residency captured separately so the FIRB foreign-person flag can be computed, a `KYCCase` per verification event that runs the 15-state SM-01 machine against the identity and sanctions sandboxes, a 24-hour identity SLA and a 30-day request-for-information clock, fraud auto-reject, PEP clearance as a two-person act (KYCA to MLRO, reason code mandatory, tighter than the design's one click), enhanced due diligence as `6E`, a true sanctions match that freezes silently, both holders verified before a joint account opens, beneficial-owner and controller cases for entities (owners at 25 % or more, or a controller when no one reaches 25 %; the entity cannot invest until every person is verified), periodic refresh, suspension and reinstatement with preconditions, masked PII with reason-coded unmask, and every endpoint callable under a scoped token where the act is delegable.**

**Build status:** not started. **Milestone:** AM3 (Business services). **Lane:** Services. **Planner:** Opus 5.5 (high). **Builder:** Grok 4.7 (xhigh). **Node QA:** Opus 5.5 (high, every loop). **Graph QA:** Fable 5.1 (high, at milestone close).
**Estimate:** 12-18 agent-hours (build, challenge and verify loops; raised from 8-12 for the joint, multi-account and KYB scope; the graph's estimate field must carry the same figure).

**Depends on:** [[A22 - Identity-DVS and sanctions sandbox adapters]], [[A11 - Authentication, sessions, RBAC and segregation of duties]], [[A14 - Approvals engine and maker-checker (SM-00)]], [[A19 - Rules engine- gates G01-G22, rule packs, threshold monitors, simulator]]
**Unlocks:** [[A31 - On-chain identity and claims service (V4 identity agent)]], [[A32 - Classification- wholesale certificates and target-market hooks (SM-02)]], [[A33 - Sponsor tenancy, entities, licences of sponsors and admission pipeline]], [[A41 - Tax engine- profiles, withholding, AMMA and CRS-FATCA]], [[A42 - AML screening, monitoring, cases and SMR clocks (SM-11)]], [[A44 - Support, complaints (SM-12) and Investor 360 read model]], [[A49 - Money-path smoke through the SDK (AM3 exit and founder checkpoint)]], [[A56 - Onboarding flow ONB-01 to ONB-10 with entity branch]], [[A68 - Console- identity review, wholesale certificates, investor directory and Investor 360]], [[A97 - P3- minors, development and debt vehicles, licensed-market venue mode]]

**Oracle rule:** this is not an oracle node (`oracle: no`): no money, supply or holdings move here, but eligibility does, so the node QA still writes hidden tests from this frozen spec to `<workspace>\gauntlet\hidden\A30\` before the build starts (an SM-01 transition oracle, the decision and segregation-of-duties matrix, the silent-freeze and tipping-off probes, the entity-gating and joint-gating property tests, the reinstatement preconditions, the foreign-person vectors, the clock vectors and the PII scans); the builder never reads that folder, and the tests are published into `app/` only after the gate passes ([[App Gauntlet]] §2).

This note is not legal advice. Every threshold, risk point, review cadence, SLA and definition below (what counts as a PEP, a foreign person, ordinary residence, a beneficial owner, a controller, an acceptable fallback document) is a row of data marked "set with counsel"; counsel's or the founder's answers change rows, not code.

## Build brief

This node is API-only: module `investors` in the Fastify monolith (`services/api/src/modules/investors/`, worker jobs registered from the same folder through A09's job registry). It draws no screen. ONB-01 to ONB-04 and ONB-10 are built by A56, ADM-09 by A44's read model and A68, ADM-10 by A68, on top of this module's API. Every rule below is enforced in the API, never only in a screen; every read contract is in the OpenAPI document from the first commit so `packages/sdk` and the MCP tool catalogue (A12, A104) see it before the module passes. The only source of time is A09's injectable clock (`Date.now()` and argument-less `new Date()` are grepped by `npm run verify`). Every vendor is reached through A22's `identity` and `sanctions` adapters and A08's `storage`; no other adapter and no vendor SDK is imported. This node edits nothing outside its owned path: its tunables live as data inside the module (§1), and what it needs from A03, A14 and A13 is either already theirs or listed under "Upstream data this node needs" below.

**1. Data and identifiers (tables are A04's; this node adds `person_identity`, `joint_invite` and `unmask_grant` only through A04's module-schema registry)**

- **Person and account are separate.** A person is a human principal from A11 (`PRN-H-…`). An investor account is an `investor` row with an `INV-` id; A02 defines `INV-` as "an investor account, not a person", and every other node's `investor_id` (holdings, orders, cash accounts, on-chain identity, A12's `SignatoryPort` `accountId`) means this account id. One person may hold many accounts (their own individual account, a joint account, an SMSF, a company), and one account may have several people. The person is verified once and that verification is reused by every account they belong to and by every sponsor (the "one KYC, many sponsors" model; ONB-02 copy "You can add more accounts later").
- `person_identity` (one per human principal, keyed by `principal_id`): the reusable person record. Encrypted PII only in `_enc` columns with blind indexes (`email_bidx`, `phone_bidx`; `tfn_bidx` belongs to A41); `citizenships[]` (ISO country codes), `visa_status` (`citizen`, `permanent_visa`, `temporary_visa`, `no_visa`, `not_applicable`), `ordinarily_resident_au` (the answer to the ordinary-residence questions: 200 or more days in Australia in the last 12 months and presence not limited in time, as declared), `country_of_residence`, `tax_residency_countries[]` (the ONB-01 "Country of tax residence" field, handed to A41; it is not used for FATA), `individual_foreign_person` (`yes` or `no`, derived by the data rule below), `age_confirmed_at`, `terms_consent_version`, `privacy_consent_version`; `identity_state` (the current person case's SM-01 state from `started` to `approved`, plus `expired` and `rejected`); `current_case_id`; `risk_rating` (`low`, `medium`, `high`), `pep` flag, `kyc_valid_until`, `refresh_tier`; `restriction` (`none`, `under_review`, `restricted`; staff-only, never in an investor-facing response; the reason is never on this row, tipping-off rule N08).
- `investor` (the `INV-` investor account; A02 id) and A04's `account` table as its one-to-one header (`account.investor_id` unique): `account.account_type` in {`individual`, `joint`, `smsf`, `company`, `trust`} (`minor_guardian` exists in the enum for A97 and is refused with `PHASE_NOT_ENABLED` in P1 and P2), `account.signing_rule` (`either`, `both`; joint only), `account.created_by_principal_id`; `investor.state` (SM-01: before whitelisting it mirrors the account's gating case as defined in §3; from `whitelisted` on it holds the account-level states `whitelisted`, `refresh_due`, `suspended`, `offboarded` itself); `investor.refresh_reason` (`periodic`, `owner_added`; set only in `refresh_due`), `investor.refresh_grace_until`; `investor.suspension_reason` (`aml_restriction`, `sanctions_true_match`, `refresh_lapsed`, `refresh_rejected`, `protective`, `estate`, `member_suspended`; staff-only); `investor.foreign_person` (`yes`, `no`, `pending`), `investor.firb_flag`; `investor.onchain_identity` (filled by A31); `investor.tenant_id` (platform tenant).
- `account_member` (`investor_id`, `principal_id`, `member_role` in {`holder`, `joint_holder`, `trustee`, `director`, `signatory`, `beneficial_owner`, `controller`, `guardian`}, `ownership_bp`, `is_verified_signatory`, `person_case_id`): an individual account has one `holder`; a joint account has exactly two `joint_holder` members once the invitation is accepted; an entity account has at least one `signatory` and every person the KYB lookup and the owner list name. Trustees, directors, signatories, owners and controllers of an entity are people with a `person_identity` and a person case; they do **not** get an investor account of their own unless they open one. `is_verified_signatory` is what this node's `SignatoryPort` implementation returns to A12 for `TOKEN_SIGNATORY_REQUIRED` and the joint co-sign path (this node registers the implementation with A12 from its own path).
- `joint_invite` (`investor_id`, `invitee_email_bidx`, `state` in {`pending`, `accepted`, `declined`, `expired`, `withdrawn`}, `expires_at` = sent + `onboarding.joint_invite_days`, default 14 calendar days, a config key).
- `kyc_case` (id `KYC-<yyyymmdd>-<6 base32>`; the design's "Case INV-00912" is a seed case whose subject account is INV-00912, and the seed maps both spellings): `subject_type` (`person` or `entity`), `subject_ref` (a `PRN-H-` principal for a person case, an `INV-` entity account for an entity case), `kind` (`initial`, `refresh`, `profile_change`, `rfi_reply`, `owner_added`), `provider` (A22's implementation name), `provider_ref`, `provider_decision` (`pass`, `refer`, `fail`, `fraud`), `dvs_sources_matched` (0-2), `liveness_score` (basis points), `extracted_fields` (encrypted; the document's name, DOB, address, document number) beside the entered fields so the console can render "extracted vs entered" with per-field `match` (`exact`, `order`, `mismatch`), `risk_points`, `risk_rating`, `state` (SM-01), `referred_at`, `sla_clock_id`, `rfi_clock_id`, `rfi_origin` (`manual_review` or `edd_review`), `restricted_view` (true after a pre-whitelist sanctions true match; see §4), `decided_by`, `decision_reason_code`, `approval_id` (set when a decision went through A14), `qa_sampled`, `dvs_consent_version`, `tenant_id` (platform tenant; sponsors never own a case). Cases are per person: a joint account needs two approved person cases, an entity needs one entity case plus an approved person case for every listed person. A person case that is `approved` and inside `kyc_valid_until` is reused when the same person joins another account; no new case is opened.
- `screening_hit` (insert-only rows plus a disposition column): `case_id`, `list_code` (`DFAT`, `PEP`, `ADVERSE_MEDIA`), `matched_name`, `confidence_bp`, `dob_match`, `detail_ref` (the adapter's payload id, never PII in clear), `disposition` (`open`, `false_positive`, `true_match`, `need_info`), `disposed_by`, `approval_id`, `reason_code`, `resolution_ref` (a counsel-recorded delisting or permit reference, see reinstatement in §3).
- `beneficial_owner` (A04): `entity_investor_id`, `person_principal_id`, `ownership_bp`, `role` (`owner`, `trustee`, `director`, `signatory`, `controller`), `control_basis` (for a controller: `appointor`, `settlor`, `senior_managing_official`, `other_control`; counsel-owned list), `is_foreign_person`, `person_case_id`, `state` (`invited`, `verifying`, `verified`, `failed`, `removed`); `entity_check` is the KYB result: `abn`/`acn` (encrypted), `entity_name`, `entity_type`, `registry_status`, `deed_doc_id`, `screen_result`, `verified_at`, `submitted_at`.
- `tenant_consent` (A04): `investor_id`, `tenant_id`, `scope` (`kyc_status` only in P1), `granted_at`, `revoked_at`. Created by this module, never by a sponsor: it consumes A37's `orders.placed` outbox event (idempotent on the order id) and creates the consent for the offering's tenant; it also exports `grantConsent(investorId, tenantId, sourceRef)` for any other flow in which an investor applies to a sponsor (the same idempotent function). No stub is involved.
- Module data (inside the owned path, `investors/data/`, versioned rows with `set_with_counsel: true`, loaded and validated with zod at boot; A03's data format is copied, A03's files are not edited): `risk-points.ts` (PEP +40, adverse media +20, high-risk residency country +30 from a counsel-owned list, address matched by one source only +10, extracted-vs-entered mismatch on name or DOB +15, entity with a non-resident owner +10, document fallback used +5; thresholds `auto_approve_below = 30`, `edd_at_or_above = 60`); `review-cadence.ts` (refresh high 12 months, medium 24, low 36; grace 30 days); `decision-reasons.ts` (the KYC decision reason codes of §3 and §4, distinct from A03's refusal codes); `foreign-person-rule.ts` (below); `kyb-rules.ts` (owner threshold 2,500 bp, controller rule, foreign substantial-interest thresholds). Clock policies are registered with A13 at boot through `registerSlaPolicies` from `investors/data/sla-policies.ts`: `kyc_rfi_30d` (`calendar_days`, 30, reminder at day 23), `kyc_refresh_grace_30d` (`calendar_days`, 30) and `kyc_joint_invite_14d` (`calendar_days`, 14); the queue clock uses A13's seeded "identity check 24 h" row.
- **Foreign-person rule (G06), data not code.** Individuals, default (counsel to confirm, HG-LEGAL): an Australian citizen is not a foreign person wherever they live; a non-citizen is not a foreign person only when they hold a permanent visa **and** declare themselves ordinarily resident (200 or more days in Australia in the last 12 months and presence not limited in time); every other person is a foreign person. Accounts: an individual account takes the holder's value; a joint account is `yes` when either holder is a foreign person (counsel to confirm); an entity account is `pending` at creation and is computed at KYB submit and again on every change to the owner list: `yes` when one foreign person (with associates, as declared) holds 2,000 bp or more, or foreign persons together hold 4,000 bp or more (the FATA substantial-interest defaults, counsel-owned rows), otherwise `no`. A flip on any account emits `investor.residency.changed`. `firb_flag` equals `foreign_person = yes`; `pending` is treated by A19 as "not yet eligible for a listing that restricts foreign persons". Test vectors the hidden tests carry: an Australian citizen living in London for three years (`no` under the default); a permanent visa holder with 250 days (`no`); a temporary visa holder with 250 days (`yes`); a New Zealand citizen on a temporary visa with 300 days (`yes` under the default, flagged for counsel); an SMSF with one member at 2,000 bp who is a foreign person (`yes`); a company with two unrelated foreign owners at 1,500 bp each (`no`) and three at 1,500 bp each (`yes`).

**2. Creating people and accounts (the ONB-01 and ONB-02 hand-off from A11)**

- `POST /v1/investors` is called once by the person after A11's email-verification-plus-passkey sign-up (`requiresHuman: true`; an AGNT principal gets A11's `AGNT_NOT_ALLOWED`) with `country_of_residence`, `citizenships[]`, `visa_status`, the ordinary-residence answers, `tax_residency_countries[]`, the 18+ declaration, the terms and privacy consent versions and the first `account_type`. It creates the `person_identity`, the first `investor` account in state `started` with its `account` header and the holder or first joint-holder `account_member`, derives `individual_foreign_person` and the account's `foreign_person` and `firb_flag`, records the consent versions and timestamps on `person_identity` (A35 owns disclosure acknowledgements and agent standing consent, not these sign-up consents), writes `investor.created` to A10 and emits the outbox event. A second call by the same principal is `INVESTOR_EXISTS` (409) naming `POST /v1/accounts`. Under 18 is refused with `INVESTOR_UNDERAGE` and nothing is created. A non-resident or foreign person is not refused (ONB-01 product-map state: "Non-resident: can continue; every listing then shows whether a foreign person may join it"); the G06 capture is what this node guarantees and A19 enforces the listing rule.
- `POST /v1/accounts` (`requiresHuman: true`) opens a further account for an existing person with `account_type` and, for a joint account, the invitee's email. A second `individual` account for the same person is `ACCOUNT_EXISTS` (409); joint and entity accounts are not limited. The new account reuses the person's current approved case when it is inside `kyc_valid_until`, so a verified person opening an SMSF goes straight to KYB.
- **Joint accounts.** The creator names the second person's email; the module sends the invitation through A11's invitation path and opens the `kyc_joint_invite_14d` clock. The invitee's `POST /v1/investors/{id}/joint/accept` (`requiresHuman`) adds them as the second `joint_holder`; they then verify through their own person case (or reuse an approved one). `POST /v1/investors/{id}/joint/decline` (`requiresHuman`) sets the invitation `declined`; the clock breach sets it `expired`. After a decline, an expiry, or a second holder whose person case ends `rejected`, `auto_rejected` or `expired`, the account cannot be whitelisted: the creator's ONB-10 `members` row shows `action_needed` with `JOINT_INVITE_DECLINED`, `JOINT_INVITE_EXPIRED` or `JOINT_PARTNER_NOT_VERIFIED` (never the other person's reason or case detail), and the creator may send a new invitation (`POST /v1/investors/{id}/joint/invite`, which withdraws any pending one) or close the account. **Joint invariant, parallel to G03:** a joint account reaches `approved` or `whitelisted` only when it has exactly two `joint_holder` members and both have an approved person case inside `kyc_valid_until`; the service enforces it and the database trigger `account_requires_verified_members` (below) enforces it when the service is bypassed. `signing_rule` is set at creation and may be changed only by `POST /v1/investors/{id}/joint/signing-rule` carrying fresh assertions from both holders over the same canonical payload (`FRESH_ASSERTION_REQUIRED` names the missing holder; `JOINT_BOTH_REQUIRED` when the second holder has not yet accepted); the route is `requiresHuman`. A12 reads `signing_rule` through this node's `SignatoryPort`.
- **Entity accounts** (`smsf`, `company`, `trust`): `POST /v1/kyb/entities` runs A22's `verifyEntity` on the ABN or ACN for an entity account the person already opened (sandbox fixtures), stores the entity record, runs `resolveUbo` to pre-fill people at 25 % or more, and returns the people list for ONB-04. `POST /v1/kyb/entities/{id}/people` adds trustees, directors, signatories, owners and controllers (ownership in basis points; the sum of owner rows may not exceed 10,000, `KYB_OWNERSHIP_EXCEEDS_100`; an owner below 2,500 bp is optional but allowed); each person is invited to their own ONB-03 through A11 and gets their own person case (or reuses an approved one). **Controller rule (set with counsel):** when no owner reaches 2,500 bp, and always for a discretionary trust, at least one `controller` (appointor, settlor or senior managing official, per the counsel-owned `control_basis` list) must be listed and verified; otherwise submit is refused with `KYB_CONTROLLER_REQUIRED`. `POST /v1/kyb/entities/{id}/submit` requires the deed upload (A08 storage, PDF only; `KYB_DEED_REQUIRED`), at least one signatory (`KYB_SIGNATORY_REQUIRED`), the controller rule, and every listed person in `verifying` or better; it records the signatory's declaration, runs `screenEntity`, computes the entity's `foreign_person` from the owner data (§1), and creates the entity's own `kyc_case` (`subject_type = entity`), which enters `manual_review` unless every person is `approved` and the entity screen is clear, in which case it follows the person path to `auto_approved`. After submission the people list and ownership are locked: any change to people or `ownership_bp` on a not-yet-whitelisted entity is refused with `KYB_SUBMITTED_LOCKED` (409) until a KYCA puts the entity case into `rfi_pending` with the coded request `OWNERSHIP_CHANGE`, which reopens the people endpoint; the entity stays not whitelisted throughout. **G03 invariant:** the entity account reaches `whitelisted` only when its own case is `approved` **and** every `beneficial_owner` row not `removed` is `verified` **and** the controller rule holds; it is enforced by the service and by the database check trigger `account_requires_verified_members` registered here through A04's module-schema registry, which covers both `joint_holder` members of joint accounts and every trustee, director, signatory, owner at or above 2,500 bp and controller of entity accounts.
- **A person added to a whitelisted entity** (`kind = owner_added` case for that person): the entity account moves `whitelisted -> refresh_due` with `refresh_reason = owner_added`, `entityStatus` returns `owners_pending` at once (so A19's G03 hook refuses every order, transfer and withdrawal immediately), `isWhitelisted` returns false (§3), and the module emits `kyb.entity.owners_pending` so A31 suspends the entity's eligibility claim until the person is verified. When the person's case is `approved` the entity returns `refresh_due -> whitelisted` (`refreshed`), `kyb.entity.owners_verified` is emitted and A31 restores the claim; if the person's case fails, the entity stays `refresh_due` with `owners_pending` until the person is removed (a KYCA-reviewed change) or the grace clock suspends it.

**3. SM-01 lifecycle, from A03**

- The state table is A03's, not this node's: 15 states (`started`, `docs_submitted`, `provider_checks`, `auto_approved`, `manual_review`, `auto_rejected`, `rfi_pending`, `expired`, `edd_review`, `approved`, `rejected`, `whitelisted`, `refresh_due`, `suspended`, `offboarded`) and the 21 transitions from `data_states.py`, plus the extensions listed under "Upstream data this node needs". Every transition goes through `packages/domain` `transition()`; anything else is refused with `SM01_ILLEGAL_TRANSITION`.
- **Which row holds which state.** A `kyc_case` runs the case states (`started` to `approved`, `auto_rejected`, `rejected`, `expired`). `person_identity.identity_state` mirrors the person's current case. `investor.state`, before whitelisting, mirrors the account's gating case: for an individual account, the holder's person case; for a joint account, the least advanced of the two holders' person cases (order: `started` < `docs_submitted` < `provider_checks` < `manual_review` = `rfi_pending` = `edd_review` < `auto_approved` < `approved`), and `approved` only when the joint invariant holds; for an entity account, the entity case, and `approved` only when the G03 invariant holds. From `whitelisted` on, `investor.state` holds `whitelisted`, `refresh_due`, `suspended` and `offboarded` itself. A `refresh` or `rfi_reply` case runs its own copy of the case states without moving a `whitelisted` account backwards, except as stated below.
- **`isWhitelisted(investor_id)`** takes the `INV-` account id, is exported from `index.ts`, and is what A19's G01 hook reads (no deposit, order, transfer or distribution credit when it is false). It returns true only in `whitelisted`, and in `refresh_due` only when `refresh_reason = periodic` and the clock's now is before `refresh_grace_until`. It returns false in every other state, for `refresh_due` with `refresh_reason = owner_added`, and for `refresh_due` past grace even before the sweep has moved the account to `suspended`. A profile change does not move the account to `refresh_due` (below). Note: A03's `SM-01.canTransact(state)` is true only in `whitelisted`; this rule needs A03's helper to take the refresh reason and grace (see "Upstream data this node needs"); until then A19 must read `isWhitelisted`, not `canTransact`, for G01.
- `POST /v1/kyc/cases` (`requiresHuman`: identity verification is non-delegable under D04) takes the ONB-03 fields and the DVS consent text version, creates a person case for the calling principal in `started`, moves it to `docs_submitted` and then `provider_checks` in one transaction by calling A22's `startVerification`, opens the A13 queue clock on the "identity check 24 h" policy (kind `queue`, owner role KYCA, breach raises `queue.sla_breach` Sev3), and writes one audit event per state (`kyc.case.created`, `kyc.case.docs_submitted`, `kyc.case.checks_started`). Missing consent is `KYC_CONSENT_REQUIRED` (422) and no provider call is made. `POST /v1/kyc/cases/{id}/documents` (`requiresHuman`) attaches the fallback licence or passport image and the liveness capture through A08 storage (jpg, png, pdf, magic-byte checked) and re-submits to the provider when the case is `docs_submitted` or `rfi_pending`.
- Provider result: A22's webhook (`POST /v1/internal/kyc/provider-webhook`, service-principal auth, signed, idempotent on `provider_ref` plus result hash) and `getResult` polling by the worker job `kyc.poll_provider` (every minute for cases in `provider_checks` older than 2 minutes) both land in one function `applyProviderResult(case, result)`. It stores `provider_decision`, DVS sources, liveness score and extracted fields, runs `screenPerson` through A22's sanctions adapter, writes `screening_hit` rows, computes `risk_points` from the module's risk table, and applies exactly one transition: `provider_checks -> auto_approved` when the decision is `pass`, there are no open hits and `risk_points < auto_approve_below`; `provider_checks -> manual_review` (sets `referred_at`) when the decision is `refer`, or `pass` with any hit or any risk points at or above the threshold, or `fail` (a provider fail is a referral, not a rejection: a human rejects); `provider_checks -> auto_rejected` only on the provider's `fraud` signal (synthetic identity, tampered document, velocity), terminal, with `person_identity.restriction = under_review` and an outbox event A42 consumes. A second result for the same case is a no-op (`processed_event`).
- `auto_approved -> approved : QA sample` is settled at the Specify step (spec point (a)): the proposed reading is that the transition is `AUTO` (decider `SVCA`) in the same transaction, and a deterministic sample (`qa_sample_bp`, default 500, seeded per case id) sets `qa_sampled = true`; sampled cases appear in the MLRO's own view `GET /v1/kyc/queue?qa_sampled=true` and the module emits `kyc.case.qa_sampled`, which A43 may turn into a task (no stub is involved; the queue view works without A43). The case does not wait. If the user prefers a blocking sample, the case stays in `auto_approved` until a KYCA reviews it and the oracle is written against that reading instead.
- `manual_review`: KYCA (or MLRO) decides through `POST /v1/kyc/cases/{id}/decide` with `verdict` in {`approve`, `reject`, `request_info`, `escalate_edd`} and a mandatory `reason_code` from `decision-reasons.ts` (`DVS_2_SOURCES`, `DOC_MANUAL_MATCH`, `PEP_FALSE_POSITIVE`, `ADDRESS_UNCONFIRMED`, `DOC_ILLEGIBLE`, `NAME_MISMATCH`, `PEP_CONFIRMED`, `HIGH_RISK_COUNTRY`, `FRAUD_SUSPECTED`, `OWNERSHIP_CHANGE`, ...; missing is `KYC_REASON_REQUIRED`; a free-text `note` of at most 500 characters is optional and PII-scanned). No other role holds a decide cell; a CCO, CEO or SUPP call is A11's `FORBIDDEN`. `approve` is allowed only when every `screening_hit` is `false_positive` (an open hit refuses with `KYC_OPEN_HIT`). Approving a case whose `provider_decision` is `fail` is an override and is an A14 request `kyc.case.approve_override` (`4E`, KYCA maker, MLRO checker); the case moves to `approved` only when A14's effector runs. `reject` is terminal (`rejected`); the investor sees "We could not verify your identity" with the support path and never the reason code. `request_info` moves to `rfi_pending` (`rfi_origin = manual_review`), opens the A13 clock `kyc_rfi_30d` (calendar days; a reminder outbox event at day 23 for A43), and records what was asked as a coded list (`UTILITY_BILL`, `SECOND_DOCUMENT`, `SOURCE_OF_FUNDS`, `DEED`, `OWNERSHIP_CHANGE`, ...) so the investor screen can say what to upload; `rfi_pending -> docs_submitted` on `POST /v1/kyc/cases/{id}/rfi-reply` (`requiresHuman`, with documents) restarts the provider path (`docs_submitted -> provider_checks`), meets the RFI clock and reopens the 24 h queue clock; `rfi_pending -> expired` is applied by the worker job `kyc.rfi_expiry` (every 5 minutes, idempotent) when the A13 clock breaches, with a notification event; an expired case is terminal and the person starts a new one. `escalate_edd` moves to `edd_review`.
- `edd_review`: the MLRO may ask for more (`verdict = request_info` from `edd_review`, typically `SOURCE_OF_FUNDS`), which moves `edd_review -> rfi_pending` with `rfi_origin = edd_review`; the reply moves `rfi_pending -> edd_review` directly (no provider re-run unless identity documents were asked for, in which case the path is `docs_submitted -> provider_checks` as above and the result lands back in `manual_review`). `edd_review -> approved : MLRO and senior manager` is an A14 request `kyc.case.approve_edd` under policy `6E`: three distinct humans (the maker, an MLRO checker and a senior-manager checker from {CCO, CEO}), each with a hardware-key decision; the maker is the KYCA who escalated or an MLRO; when the maker is the only MLRO, the two checkers are the CCO and the CEO; the maker identity set includes the maker's agents as A14 does. The effector applies the transition and records `approval_id` on the case. `edd_review -> rejected : MLRO` is a single MLRO decision with reason code (no checker, per the product map). Any case where the subject's `risk_rating` is `high` or a hit is `PEP` with disposition `true_match` (a confirmed PEP, not a sanctions match) may only be approved through this path; a plain `approve` on such a case is refused with `KYC_EDD_REQUIRED`.
- `approved -> whitelisted : registerIdentity` is not applied by this node: A31's V4 job reads accounts in `approved` and calls `POST /v1/internal/investors/{id}/transition` (service principal only) with the transaction reference after the on-chain registration is confirmed. The endpoint re-checks the joint and G03 invariants and refuses with `KYB_OWNERS_PENDING` or `JOINT_BOTH_REQUIRED` even for the service principal. This node ships the endpoint, the read query A31 subscribes to (`GET /v1/internal/kyc/accounts?state=approved&unregistered=true`, returning `INV-` ids), and a fixture that drives the transition in tests.
- `whitelisted -> refresh_due : periodic review` is applied by the worker job `kyc.refresh_sweep` (daily 04:00 Sydney, idempotent) when a member person's `kyc_valid_until` (set at approval from the cadence table by `risk_rating`) is within the grace window; it creates one `refresh` case for that person, moves every account the person belongs to (as holder, joint holder, trustee, director, signatory, owner or controller) to `refresh_due` with `refresh_reason = periodic`, opens one `kyc_refresh_grace_30d` clock per person and emits `investor.refresh_due` per account for A43. `refresh_due -> whitelisted : refreshed` when the refresh case reaches `approved` (A31 re-issues the claim; no second `registerIdentity`); `refresh_due -> suspended : not refreshed` (`suspension_reason = refresh_lapsed`) when the grace clock breaches. A refresh case that ends `rejected` moves the accounts to `suspended` with `suspension_reason = refresh_rejected`. A `profile_change` case (name or address change through `PATCH /v1/investors/me/profile`, `requiresHuman`) keeps the accounts `whitelisted` while it runs and moves them to `suspended` only if it ends `rejected`.
- `whitelisted -> suspended : AML restriction` and `refresh_due -> suspended : AML restriction` come from A42 (and from the estate path in A44) through the internal transition endpoint with a `suspension_reason`, or from `POST /v1/investors/{id}/suspend` on ADM-09, an A14 `SGL+R` protective request `investor.suspend` (MLRO or CCO acts with a hardware-key assertion; the other ratifies within 2 h; reversible: the AUTO revert is `investor.reinstate` when ratification lapses) with `suspension_reason = protective` and an optional `freeze_wallets` flag that emits `investor.freeze_requested` for A31 and A28. A person suspension (sanctions, AML) suspends every account the person belongs to with `member_suspended` on the accounts where they are not the only member.
- **Reinstatement.** `suspended -> whitelisted : MLRO then CCO` is `POST /v1/investors/{id}/reinstate`, an A14 request `investor.reinstate` under `4E` with a fixed order (MLRO maker, CCO checker; a CCO maker is refused with `KYC_REINSTATE_ORDER`). The request is refused at creation, and again inside the effector, with `KYC_REINSTATE_BLOCKED` and one detail code when: a `DFAT` hit on the person or any member is `true_match` and carries no `resolution_ref` (a counsel-recorded delisting or permit reference, attached through the `4E` hit update, MLRO maker and CCO checker) — `SANCTIONS_MATCH_UNRESOLVED`; an AML case is open for the person or account — `AML_CASE_OPEN` (asked through `registerAmlCaseProbe(probe)`, a registration point A42 fills from its own path; until one is registered the answer is "open", fail-closed in every environment, and this node's tests use an in-module fake); `kyc_valid_until` has passed for any member and no refresh case for that member reached `approved` after the suspension — `REFRESH_REQUIRED`; another member of the account is still suspended — `MEMBER_SUSPENDED`. The effector clears `suspension_reason` and `person_identity.restriction` only when every precondition holds.
- **Closing an account.** `POST /v1/investors/{id}/close` (`requiresHuman`; the ACC-01 close path built by A59) moves `approved`, `whitelisted` or `refresh_due` to `offboarded` (`account closed`, an extension) after the checks A44 defines; `suspended -> offboarded` is the catalogue's transition and is staff-only. A close before `approved` is refused with `ACCOUNT_CLOSE_NOT_AVAILABLE`: the person's case either completes or is expired by the abandonment sweep, and a pre-approval account with no case activity for 90 days is closed by the same sweep. `offboarded` is terminal and, when it is the person's last open account, revokes every token of the principal through A12's event subscription (`investor.offboarded`).
- Spec gaps to settle at Specify, before the node QA writes the hidden tests (spec point (b)): the catalogue's SM-01 has no transition from `auto_rejected` or `rejected` for a person who was wrongly rejected, and no `cancelled` for an abandoned case. Proposed: no new state; a wrongly rejected person starts a new case whose creation requires an MLRO `4E` unlock (`kyc.case.reopen`, reason code) when the previous case ended in `auto_rejected` (fraud) and needs nothing when it ended in `rejected`; abandoned cases in `started` or `docs_submitted` for 90 days are `expired` by the sweep. The user decides; if declined, a rejected person cannot re-apply and the gap is recorded in `A30.qa.md`. Either way the settled entries must be in A03's `extensions.ts` before A03 is built (below).

**4. Screening, PEP and sanctions (G02 at onboarding; A42 owns re-screening and cases)**

- Hits are dispositioned through `POST /v1/kyc/cases/{id}/hits/{hitId}` with `disposition` and a mandatory `reason_code`. `false_positive` on a `PEP` or `ADVERSE_MEDIA` hit is an A14 request `kyc.screening.clear_hit` (`4E`: KYCA maker, MLRO checker; the design's one-click "Clear PEP hit and verify" by the CCO is superseded, design-analysis §4 item 29 and §6.2; a CCO call is `FORBIDDEN` because the CCO holds no maker cell here); the disposition is written by A14's effector and only then may the case be approved. `false_positive` on a `DFAT` sanctions hit follows the same `4E` row but the checker set is MLRO only and the request carries the list version. `need_info` moves the case to `rfi_pending` as in §3. `true_match` on a `PEP` hit (KYCA or MLRO, reason code) records a confirmed PEP (`person_identity.pep = true`, `risk_rating = high`) and forces the EDD path.
- `true_match` on a `DFAT` sanctions hit is the silent freeze: an A14 `SGL+R` protective request `kyc.sanctions.confirm_match` (MLRO acts, CCO ratifies within 2 h; irreversible, so a lapse escalates through A13 instead of auto-reverting) whose effector, in one transaction: sets `person_identity.restriction = restricted`; moves every whitelisted or refresh-due account of the person to `suspended` (`suspension_reason = sanctions_true_match`); for a case still before whitelisting, leaves it in `manual_review` with `restricted_view = true`, which removes it from the KYCA queue (`GET /v1/kyc/queue`) and places it in the MLRO's need-to-know view (`GET /v1/kyc/restricted`, MLRO and CCO only), and meets the 24 h queue clock with reason `restricted` so no `queue.sla_breach` alert is raised for it; emits `kyc.sanctions.true_match` for A42 to open the AML case and for A31 and A28 to freeze wallets; and writes the audit event with `sensitivity = need_to_know`.
- **What the investor sees (tipping-off, rule N08).** No investor-facing response carries `restriction`, `suspension_reason`, `restricted_view` or any hit, score or reason code. The investor's status is derived by one pure function from the case state alone, the same for an ordinary referral and a restricted case: `manual_review`, `edd_review` and `provider_checks` map to `reviewing`; `expected_by` is computed by one pure function `expectedBy(referred_at, policy, now)` (the policy duration after `referred_at`, rounded up to the hour, rolled forward in whole policy periods once passed) and never from the live clock row, so a met, cancelled or breached clock cannot change it. No notification is sent for a restriction. The investor's `GET /v1/investors/me`, `GET /v1/investors/me/status`, `GET /v1/kyc/cases/me` and `GET /v1/kyc/cases/{id}` responses are byte-for-byte the shape of an ordinary referral. Support roles (SUPP, IRCM) see a generic `restriction` of `none` or `under_review` (`restricted` is shown to them as `under_review`) and nothing else (RLS need-to-know policy from A04 plus a response shaper here).
- G02 at this node covers onboarding and profile changes only; the nightly re-screen, the list-update hits and "screen before every payout" are A42 through A22's `subscribeListUpdates`. This node exports `screeningStatus(investor_id)` (`clear`, `open_hits`, `restricted`, over every member of the account) for A19's G02 hook.
- Investor-facing status codes (returned as codes the screens map): `verified`, `reviewing` (with `expected_by`), `action_needed` (RFI, with the coded list, or a joint or entity detail code), `expired`, `could_not_verify`. There is no code for a hit, a fraud signal, a restriction or a freeze.

**5. Gates, rules and the AML boundary**

- This node registers three data providers with A19 at boot: G01 (`isWhitelisted` as defined in §3, `identityStatus` with `kyc_valid_until` and `refresh_reason`), G02 (`screeningStatus`), G03 (`entityStatus`: `not_entity` for an individual account, `owners_pending` with the count and the names masked for an entity or joint account with any unverified member or an `owner_added` refresh, `verified`), and supplies the G06 inputs (`country_of_residence`, `foreign_person` including `pending`, `firb_flag`) that A19 evaluates per listing. A19 records the `gate_evaluation` rows; this node records none itself. Every provider is a pure read over the tables with no side effect, so A19's simulator can call them freely.
- Segregation of duties beyond A11's ten rules: a KYCA or MLRO whose `person_id` is the subject, a member of the subject account (holder, joint holder, owner, controller or signatory), or who is listed as a related party on the case, is refused every decision on it with `KYC_SOD_OWN_CASE` (research-pack rule #5, carried in A11's data file); the refusal is audited (`investor.sod.violation`). An analyst who made the `request_info` decision may still approve after the reply (same case, one analyst) because the checker rules for PEP clearance, override and EDD are what protect the outcome.
- KYC cannot be turned off: `tenant_guardrail.kyc_required` is locked on by A04; this module has no flag, no tenant setting and no environment variable that skips a case, and a test asserts that no route of the module accepts a `skip`, `bypass` or `preverified` field.

**6. PII: masked by default, unmasked by reason**

- Every read endpoint returns PII through A04's `mask()` codec ("Alex M.", DOB as year, address as suburb and state, licence number as last three digits, email as `a•••@example.com`), the shapes the design shows on ADM-09 ("Alex N. (masked)") and `adKyc` ("Surry Hills NSW"). Extracted document fields are masked the same way; the "extracted vs entered" table on ADM-10 renders the `match` column without the values until unmasked.

A grant used after expiry or from another session is `UNMASK_GRANT_INVALID`. Bulk exports of investor PII belong to A102 and do not exist here.

- Audit count rule (A10, hash-chained): exactly one event per case or account state transition (a decision that applies a transition writes that transition's event, carrying the decision fields, and no second event); exactly one event per act that does not transition (a hit disposition, an agent draft, an A14 request created, an unmask grant, an invitation sent, accepted, declined or expired, a consent created, a refused SoD attempt).

# A36 - Offerings: offer terms, listing review, raise monitor (SM-04)

**The offering record and its lifecycle up to the close: a sponsor's offer terms (regime, unit price, soft and hard caps, tickets, per-investor cap, lock-in, close date, allocation rule, residency rule, distribution policy, retained-stake commitment) validated against the rule pack and simulated on every save; a submission chain of operator, sponsor owner (with the owner's personally signed sponsor declaration), then RealX; a listing review that shows pack constraints read-only, blockers, warnings and the due-diligence, legal, valuation and trustee sign-offs and can return the listing with comments; go-live approval as `4E+Q` with the platform CCO (J12 step 8), followed by token configuration under its own approval (J12 step 9) and an automatic opening at `opens_at` only when the token is configured; extend or material change as `4E` with investor re-confirmation within 5 business days; pause as `SGL+R` and holds for halts, kill switches and tenant suspensions; the live raise funnel as a read model; and the rule that nothing is public until approved. Close, fail, finalise, mint and refund are triggered by [[A38 - Finalise-and-mint and revert-and-refund pipelines]] through this module's exported transition functions. The public and sponsor read contracts (listings by status, open offers, raise bars) and the `OfferingTerms` type A37 reads go into the SDK before the engine is built.**

**Build status:** not started. **Milestone:** AM3 (Business services). **Lane:** Services. **Planner:** Opus 5.5 (high). **Builder:** Grok 4.7 (xhigh). **Node QA:** Opus 5.5 (high, every loop). **Graph QA:** Fable 5.1 (high, at milestone close).
**Estimate:** 9-13 agent-hours (build, challenge and verify loops).

**Depends on:** [[A34 - Properties, valuations and proof of property (SM-03)]], [[A35 - Documents, disclosure library, e-sign and acknowledgements (G12)]], [[A19 - Rules engine- gates G01-G22, rule packs, threshold monitors, simulator]], [[A21 - Licences, feature flags and licence perimeter (G21)]], [[A43 - Communications, notifications, tasks and marketing claims (G17)]]
Also read through their `index.ts`, already in this node's dependency closure: [[A33 - Sponsor tenancy, entities, licences of sponsors and admission pipeline]] (through A34) and [[A27 - Chain adapter, deployment manifests and indexer]] (through A35 and A28).
**Unlocks:** [[A37 - Orders, escrow, allocation and refunds (SM-05)]], [[A48 - Halts, kill switches and wind-down]], [[A54 - Public site- home, investments, property page, sponsor profile, fees]], [[A63 - Sponsor console- new listing wizard SPN-03 to SPN-06 and offer monitor SPN-07]], [[A71 - Console- listing review, live raise monitor, orders and allocation]], [[A90 - P2- registered scheme, sub-funds, PDS and TMD, target market (G05), retail cooling-off]]

**Oracle rule:** oracle node (`oracle: yes`). No money, supply or holdings move inside this module (escrow moves in A37, units are minted by [[A38 - Finalise-and-mint and revert-and-refund pipelines]]), but this node fixes the cap and ticket arithmetic that bounds what A37 may accept into escrow, the facts that decide when a close is due, and the go-live approval that releases a listing to the public. The node QA therefore writes hidden tests from this spec to `<workspace>\gauntlet\hidden\A36\` before the build starts ([[App Gauntlet]] §2), at least one case for each of oracle items (a) to (m) below. The builder never reads that folder; the cases are published into the repo after the gate passes.

This note is not legal advice. Every default (5 percent cap, 90-day lock-in, 75 percent soft cap, 5 business days to re-confirm, 3 reserve months, 90-day valuation age at submission) is a row of data with a `set_with_counsel` or `set_by_founder` marker; answers change rows, not code.

## Build brief

**Scope and placement**

- Module `offerings` in the API (`services/api/src/modules/offerings/`), following the A09 module pattern: a public `index.ts` (the only import other modules may use, enforced by the boundary lint), zod schemas that feed the OpenAPI document from the first commit, Drizzle schema and migration in the module's own folder registered with A04's per-module registry, and job definitions exported from the module and registered with the worker through A09. The only source of time is A09's injectable clock; `Date.now()` and argument-less `new Date()` are forbidden in the owned path and grepped by `npm run verify`. All dates typed by a sponsor are `Australia/Sydney` civil dates converted to instants once, at save.
- This node ships no screen. It is the data and the rules behind SPN-05, SPN-06 and SPN-07 ([[A63 - Sponsor console- new listing wizard SPN-03 to SPN-06 and offer monitor SPN-07]]), ADM-07 and ADM-08 ([[A71 - Console- listing review, live raise monitor, orders and allocation]]), the property aside and listing cards on PUB-01, PUB-03 and PUB-04 ([[A54 - Public site- home, investments, property page, sponsor profile, fees]]), the offer-progress line of the buy stepper (INV-01) and the "Listings" table on SPN-02. It fixes the record shapes, status labels, column names and reason codes so those nodes render without translation, and it publishes the read contracts and the `OfferingTerms` type into `packages/sdk` in the first commit so A37, A54 and A63 can be built against them while this engine is still in its gauntlet.
- What this node does not do: it never creates an order, moves escrow, locks an allocation, mints or refunds (A37 and A38 do); it never decides **when** to close, fail, finalise, mint or refund (A38 owns those triggers: its `settlement.close_sweep` at `closes_at`, the hard-cap trigger, the `offer.close_early` and `offer.revert` approvals, allocation lock, reconciled mint and refund completion) and only executes those SM-04 transitions, under A03's guards, when A38 calls the exported functions in item 5; it never evaluates a gate itself (A19 does; this node supplies facts and reads verdicts); it never decides an approval (A14 does); it never configures a token (A27 and A28 do under the ADM-14 approval).

**1. The offering record and its versions (module M04).**

- `offering` row: `id` (`OFR-<6 base32>`, Crockford, server-generated), `code` (short listing code such as `KST01`, unique per platform, used in routes and as the token symbol stem), `tenant_id`, `vehicle_id` and `property_id` (A34; exactly one property per vehicle per offering), `version` (integer, starts at 1), `regime` (`s708_wholesale` | `au_mis_retail`), `unit_price_cents`, `total_units` (the tokenised units offered, which are all the units the offering creates; default `offer.default_supply_units` from the A19 pack, 10,000), `hard_cap_units` (always equal to `total_units`, stored for A37), `hard_cap_cents` (always `unit_price_cents × total_units`, computed by `packages/domain` money math and never typed), `soft_cap_bps` (default 7,500) with derived `soft_cap_units` (rounded up to a whole unit) and `soft_cap_cents` (`soft_cap_units × unit_price_cents`), `min_ticket_units` (default 1), `max_ticket_units` (default = `holder_cap_units`), `holder_cap_bps` (the listing's per-investor cap; default `holder_cap.default_bps` = 500, never above A33's `tenant_guardrail.holder_cap_ceiling_bp`), `lock_in_days` (default `lock_in.default_days` = 90), `opens_at`, `closes_at`, `allocation_rule` (`first_come` | `pro_rata`), `oversubscription_cap_bps` (0 for `first_come`; default 2,000 for `pro_rata`), `allow_oversubscription` (derived, true exactly when `oversubscription_cap_bps > 0`), `cancellable_until_close` (default true, `set_by_founder`; A37 lets an investor cancel a funded order while the offer is `live` only when it is true), `cooling_off_days` (default 0; A90 sets it for `au_mis_retail`), `fund_at` (`after_reflection` default, `confirm` variant; meaningful only for retail, A37's rule), `residency_rule` (`residents_only` | `after_firb_check` | `open`; the default comes from A34's property type through A19's G06 matrix and a sponsor may only tighten it), `distribution_policy` (`monthly`, `payment_rule` = `10th_business_day`), `reserve_months` (default 3), `sponsor_retained_stake_bps` (see below), `fee_schedule_doc_id` and the three fee lines it must carry (`sponsor_mgmt_fee_bps_pa`, `platform_fee_bps_pa`, `fee_bps` = 50, the investor transaction fee A37 charges), `failure_mode` (`refund_all`), `extension_count`, `rule_pack_id` (A19's pack version id) and `rule_pack_code` (display, e.g. `AU-W-2026.1`), `compliance_rule_id` (A19), `tmd_id` (P2, null in P1), `document_set` (A35's `getListingDocumentSet(offering)` members with the effective version at submission: IM or PDS, trust deed, fee schedule, risk statement, valuation report, and the signed `sponsor_declaration`), `marketing_copy_ids` (A43 claim items), `out_of_limits[]` (see item 2), `state` (SM-04), `state_reason`, `published_at` (null until `approved`), `go_live_approval_id`, `last_simulation_sha256`, `last_simulation_register_version`, plus the standard `tenant_id`, `created_*` and `updated_*` columns under row-level security.
- **Retained stake (A06's resolution, kept).** `sponsor_retained_stake_bps` is the sponsor's commitment to keep that share of the vehicle's equity, recorded on the offering and on A33's sponsor admission, and held **outside the tokenised units** (equity in the sponsor's own vehicle or a separate non-tokenised class). It creates no unit, no register lot, no token balance and no holder count: `total_units` is the whole tokenised supply, `hard_cap_cents = unit_price_cents × total_units`, and the design's figures (KST01 hard cap 10,000 × A$238.40 = A$2,384,000; "Tokens on offer 10,000") use exactly that. The stake therefore never meets G07. The placement is a data row `offer.retained_stake_placement = outside_tokenised_units` marked `set_with_counsel`; if counsel requires the stake inside the trust, the answer is a spec change for this node, A06 and A38 (offered units would become `total_units − retained_units`), not a code toggle.
- `OfferingTerms` (exported type in `index.ts`, generated into `packages/sdk`): exactly the fields A37 reads, with A37's names: `regime`, `unit_price_cents`, `total_units`, `soft_cap_units`, `hard_cap_units`, `min_ticket_units`, `max_ticket_units`, `holder_cap_bps`, `fee_bps`, `allocation_rule`, `allow_oversubscription`, `oversubscription_cap_bps`, `acceptance_ceiling_units` (item 5), `cancellable_until_close`, `cooling_off_days`, `fund_at`, `opens_at`, `closes_at`, `rule_pack_id`, `state` (the effective state, item 5). A contract test in this module compiles a copy of A37's documented import (`import type { OfferingTerms } from '@realx/api/offerings'` and a read of every field above) so a rename here fails this node's gate, not A37's.
- `offering_version` row: an immutable canonical snapshot of every term above, written at every submit and every approved material change, with `terms_sha256` (sorted-key JSON, integer cents, whole units, NFC strings) and `reason`. Every A14 request this node opens carries the `terms_sha256` of the version it concerns, so a checker approves exactly the terms the sponsor saw and the audit can replay them.
- `offer_request` row (sponsor-side requests that need RealX): `kind` (`extend`, `close_early`, `cancel`, `material_change`, `minor_edit`), `proposed_terms` (diff), `reason` (mandatory), `requested_by`, `owner_approved_by` (tenant-side approval through A14 under A33's `tenant_approval_rule` for the codes `offer.request.extend`, `offer.request.close_early`, `offer.request.cancel`, `offer.request.material_change`, `offer.request.minor_edit`), `state`, `approval_id` (the RealX request it led to: this node's `offer.extend`, `offer.material_change` or `offer.edit_minor`, or A38's `offer.close_early` or `offer.revert`).
- `offering_hold` row (holds that stop order intake or opening without a state change; item 5): `kind` (`pause`, `halt`, `kill_switch`, `tenant_suspended`, `reconfirm`, `closing`), `source_ref` (approval id, A48 halt id, A33 pause id), `reason_category` (published on the property page), `set_by`, `set_at`, `cleared_at`, `cleared_by`.
- `listing_review` row, one per submitted version: `constraints_snapshot` (the pack parameters that bound this offering, rendered read-only on ADM-07 in the product map's order: `eligibility` (e.g. "Wholesale only, verified"), `disclosure_type` (e.g. "IM (s708)"), holder cap, lock-in, `residency` (the resolved `residency_rule` with its property-type text), `documents_required[]` (from A35's listing submission set for the regime), plus valuation maximum age, member-count trigger and DAF meter state), `blockers[]` and `warnings[]` (each `{code, source: rules|dd|docs|terms|claims|perimeter|hold, detail, fix_it}`), `sign_offs[]` (`{kind: due_diligence|legal|valuation|trustee, user_id, role, at, note}`), `comments[]` (`{author, at, text, field?}`), `returned_at`, `resubmitted_from_version`, `state` (`open` | `returned` | `cleared`).
- `raise_snapshot` read model, one row per offering, recomputed on every `orders.*` event A37 publishes on the outbox (`orders.placed`, `orders.rejected`, `orders.awaiting_funds`, `orders.funded`, `orders.expired`, `orders.cancelled`, `orders.revoked`, `orders.refund_pending`, `orders.refunded`; idempotent on event id) and by `offerings.raise_sweep` every minute: `applications_count`, `applications_today`, `investors_count` (distinct investors with a funded or pending order; ADM-08's "Investors" KPI), `pending_units` (orders in `submitted`, `eligibility_check`, `reflection_period` or `awaiting_funds`), `funded_units`, `funded_cents` (Σ order subtotals of funded orders; the 0.5 percent fee is excluded), `refunded_units`, `remaining_to_hard_cap_units` (`max(0, hard_cap_units − funded_units − pending_units)`), `remaining_acceptable_units` (`max(0, acceptance_ceiling_units − funded_units − pending_units)`; equal to the previous field for `first_come`), `to_soft_cap_units` (`max(0, soft_cap_units − funded_units)`) and `to_soft_cap_cents`, `raised_pct_of_hard_cap`, `largest_holding_bps` (largest funded-plus-pending holder including associates, from A19's holder-cap monitor), `funnel` (`viewed`, `verified`, `committed`, `funded` counts), `days_left`, `computed_at`, `source_register_version`. This node computes **no** client-money figure: ADM-08's "client money trust account = funded commitments, Δ 0" line is bound by A71 to A37's `GET /v1/offerings/{id}/escrow` (Σ order total − fee over escrow-held orders against the `offer_escrow` balance), and any non-zero value is shown there, never hidden.
- Application rows on the monitor (item 7): `amount_cents` is the order total (subtotal plus fee, the design's A$2,395.92 for 10 × A$238.40), while `raised_cents` and `funded_cents` are Σ subtotals (the design's A$1,525,760 = 6,400 × A$238.40). The five design rows are the test vectors, and the monitor's summary figures must equal A37's `GET /v1/offerings/{id}/orders` summary field for field (asserted again in A37's gauntlet).

**2. Terms validation, limits and simulation (SPN-05 behaviour).**

- `POST /v1/offerings/{id}/validate` runs on every save and again at submit and returns `{blockers[], warnings[], out_of_limits[]}`. Pure checks in `terms.ts` with unit tests: `hard_cap_units = total_units`; `hard_cap_cents = unit_price_cents × total_units`; `soft_cap_units = ceil(total_units × soft_cap_bps / 10,000) ≤ total_units`; `min_ticket_units ≥ 1`; `min_ticket_units ≤ max_ticket_units ≤ holder_cap_units` where `holder_cap_units = floor(total_units × holder_cap_bps / 10,000)`; `holder_cap_bps ≤` A33's `holder_cap_ceiling_bp`; `fee_bps ≤` A33's `fee_cap_investor_bp`; `sponsor_retained_stake_bps ≥` A33's `retained_stake_min_bp` and `< 10,000` (`OFR_RETAINED_STAKE_BELOW_MIN`), with a unit test that no retained unit appears in `total_units`, the hard cap or any mint input; `oversubscription_cap_bps = 0` when `allocation_rule = first_come`; `closes_at > opens_at`; `opens_at` not in the past at submit; every date a real civil date; every amount an integer; `regime = au_mis_retail` requires `tmd_id`.
- RealX limits are data in `offer_limits` (seeded, changed by CCO -> LEGL 4E through A14 as `offer.limits_change`): `holder_cap_bps_max` (500 unless the pack's holder cap is higher), `lock_in_days_min` (90), `soft_cap_bps_min` (5,000), `offer_period_days_min` (14, `set_by_founder`), `offer_period_days_max` (90), `extension_days_max` (30), `extensions_max` (2), `oversubscription_cap_bps_max` (2,000), `min_ticket_cents_min` (A$1,000), `reserve_months_min` (3), `unit_price_cents_min` (A$1.00), `valuation_max_age_days_at_submit` (90, `set_with_counsel`; stricter than A19's G20 key `valuation.max_age_days` = 183, which still governs G20 at go-live). A term outside a limit is not refused: it becomes an `out_of_limits[]` entry `{field, value, limit, reason}` and the sponsor must type a reason (submit is refused with `OFR_OUT_OF_LIMITS_NO_REASON` otherwise). Every out-of-limits item is a blocker on the listing review until a RealX reviewer records a decision on it through `POST .../out-of-limits/{field}/decide` (`accept` with note, or `reject` which returns the listing); that endpoint needs scope `offerings:limit_decide` (CAPM, CCO), a passkey step-up and a human principal, because accepting a term outside RealX's limits is approval-like (A03 kind `approve`, `delegable: false`; the route is `requiresHuman`, so a token is refused `AGNT_NOT_ALLOWED` (reason code `G22_SCOPE_NON_DELEGABLE`), per A12 §5). The go-live approval payload lists the accepted items so the CCO approves them explicitly.
- `POST /v1/offerings/{id}/simulate` builds the draft `ComplianceRule` (holder cap, lock-in, residency policy, member cap, property type) and calls A19 `POST /v1/rules/simulate` for the vehicle; the response (gate verdicts for a hypothetical order at `min_ticket` and at `max_ticket`, holders that would be in breach if the vehicle already has holders, monitor states) is returned to the wizard and its hash and register version are stored on the offering. Submit is refused with `OFR_SIMULATION_STALE` when the stored hash is older than the latest term change or the latest register version. Only users of the offering's own tenant with `offerings:draft` or `offerings:read`, staff with `offerings:review`, and their agents under the same scopes may call it; any other principal (investors included; they use A37's quote) gets 404 for an offering that is not public, so the endpoint never reveals that a `draft` or `compliance_review` offering exists. It writes no evaluation rows (A19 guarantees that).
- Regime and phase: an `au_mis_retail` draft is allowed in P1 with the warning `OFR_REGIME_NOT_ENABLED` so a sponsor can prepare for the retail launch; submit and go-live are refused until A21 reports `offers.retail` on and PHASE=P2, and [[A90 - P2- registered scheme, sub-funds, PDS and TMD, target market (G05), retail cooling-off]] adds the PDS, TMD and cooling-off fields on top of this record without changing its state table.

**3. Submission chain: operator drafts, owner signs and approves, then RealX (SPN-06 behaviour).**

- Preconditions of `POST /v1/offerings/{id}/submit`, all checked server-side and each producing a named blocker or warning:
  - validation has no blockers; simulation fresh;
  - A34 reports the property at `spv_formed` or later, a title search present, and a valuation no older than `valuation_max_age_days_at_submit` (these are blockers: `OFR_DD_INCOMPLETE` naming the item); any **other** due-diligence item that is `missing` or `expired` (insurance certificate, building and pest, lease, rent roll) is a **warning** at submission (`OFR_DD_INCOMPLETE` as a warning naming the item) and becomes a **blocker** on the listing review and on go-live, the same pattern as G17. This is how the seeded Marine Parade listing reaches `compliance_review` with "Insurance certificate · Not uploaded · Missing" shown red on ADM-07;
  - A35's `getListingDocumentSet(offering)` reports every document member present with an effective version (`OFR_DOCS_INCOMPLETE` naming the member);
  - the `sponsor_declaration` member is in state `signed`: an A35 e-signature envelope signed by an SP-OWN or a director of the sponsor, with a passkey step-up, over the offering's current `terms_sha256` (`OFR_DECLARATION_MISSING` otherwise). While A03 marks this act `delegable: "counsel"` it is human-only: a token attempting to sign it is refused with `G22_SCOPE_PENDING_COUNSEL`, and the question is logged under HG-LEGAL (below), not silently blocked;
  - A43's claims check (`comms.checkClaims` over the IM text from A35 and the listing copy, returning each flag with `phrase`, `location` and `state`) has run; open flags are warnings at submission and blockers at go-live, matching the design's "Review" amber pill on SPN-06 and the two flags on ADM-07;
  - A33's `canSubmitListing(tenantId)` is allowed; an `attestation_overdue` reason (G19 stale for any of the sponsor's properties, A19) is returned as `OFR_SPONSOR_PAUSED_G19` naming the overdue property (the design's "Late reports pause new offers from this sponsor"); any other reason (`billing_overdue`, `watchlist_hold`, `suspended`) is passed through as A33's `TENANT_LISTINGS_PAUSED`;
  - A21 `evaluateG21` for `issue_wholesale_units` on the vehicle's trustee or RE and `arrange_wholesale_units` for the platform is a pass or a `counsel_pending` warning;
  - no active `offering_hold` (`OFR_ON_HOLD`).
- The submit call by SP-OPS or SP-OWN opens an A14 request `listing.submit` (policy `4E`, tenant-scoped). The checker is resolved by A33's `evaluateSponsorApproval({tenantId, actionCode: 'listing.submit', submitter, approver})` against the tenant's `tenant_approval_rule` row for `listing.submit` (platform minimum: one SP-OWN who is not the submitter, their delegate or their agent; the design's "Sponsor owner approval (G13) · Mia Chen · Required"). When A33's `eligibleSponsorCheckers` returns nobody, the request is refused with `OFR_SECOND_HUMAN_REQUIRED` and the fix-it points to adding a second SP-OWN on the sponsor team page. The owner's approval is the effector that runs `draft -> compliance_review` (A03 guard `sponsor_owner_approved`), freezes `offering_version`, opens `listing_review`, opens the A13 clock `listing.review` (5 business days, Sev3 to CAPM on breach), emits `offering.submitted`, opens the CAPM queue task and notifies it through A43 (`comms.openTask`, `comms.notify`; design: command-centre task "Listing waiting for review · Marine Parade Apartment · Harbour & Co").
- While in `compliance_review` the sponsor's terms are read-only; the sponsor sees the review's warnings and any comments as they are posted. `POST /v1/offerings/{id}/cancel` by SP-OWN (tenant 4E) or CAPM (with reason) runs `compliance_review -> cancelled`; a draft is deleted-soft, never cancelled (the state table has no `draft -> cancelled`).

**4. Listing review, sign-offs, return with comments and go-live approval (ADM-07 behaviour, J12 step 8).**

- `GET /v1/console/offerings/{id}/review` returns the ADM-07 shape: header line "{property} · {sponsor} · submitted {date} by {submitter}" where `{submitter}` is the sponsor user who submitted (in the seed, HCO's SP-OPS Ravi Nair; the design's "by Tom H." names the RealX CAPM who is the maker of APR-2290, so the seed deliberately differs here and A06's resolution table records it), the "Gates" rows as one A19 evaluation set of kind `listing` (G03, G06, G17, G19, G20, G21 in the design's display order, each with the design's description wording: "Trustee under own AFSL; wholesale only", "Panel valuer · A${valuation} · {month}", "Established residential · foreign persons blocked" as the resolved `residency_rule`, G17 with the exact flagged phrases and locations from A43), then this node's own rows: due-diligence items with their A34 states, document set with versions and the declaration's signer and date, the constraints panel, the out-of-limits items with their decisions, the sponsor-owner approval (Done, with name and time), the sign-offs, any active hold, and the comment thread. Every red or missing row is a blocker; the review cannot be cleared while one exists (`OFR_BLOCKERS_OPEN`).
- Roles on ADM-07: CAPM, CCO, LEGL and VALO act as below; INVM (a product-map ADM-07 role, who ran screening and the investment committee on ADM-05) reads the review and may post comments but holds no sign-off and no decision.
- Sign-offs: `POST /v1/console/offerings/{id}/sign-off` records `due_diligence` (CAPM), `legal` (LEGL), `valuation` (VALO) and `trustee` (an identity holding TP-RE for the vehicle; for a sponsor that is its own trustee, its authorised signatory with that role binding, as A06 seeds for APR-2288). Each needs a passkey step-up and a human principal (a token is refused with `APR_CHECKER_MUST_BE_HUMAN`, the A14 rule, because a sign-off is an attestation, not a draft). The same person cannot hold two sign-offs on one review (`OFR_SIGNOFF_DUPLICATE_PERSON`), and the CAPM who signs due diligence cannot be the CCO checker on go-live (A03 SoD; the catalogue's "the offering team cannot approve its own compliance review"). A03's guard on `compliance_review -> approved` is `signoffs([[CCO],[LEGL],[TP-RE]])` unordered with distinct identities; this node feeds it the go-live checker's decision as the CCO sign-off, the `legal` sign-off and the `trustee` sign-off.
- `POST /v1/console/offerings/{id}/return` (reason and at least one comment mandatory) runs `compliance_review -> draft`, keeps the version, marks the review `returned`, emits `offering.returned`, and through A43 opens the sponsor's task and notifies the sponsor; the sponsor's dashboard task reads "Respond to RealX review · {n} items · Changes requested" (brick pill, design SPN-02) and the listing row reads "Changes requested". Actors: A03's table lists CCO and LEGL (human-only) for `compliance_review__draft`; the product map's ADM-07 "Return with comments" names CAPM or CCO. This node accepts exactly A03's actors until A03 adds CAPM (spec defect reported against A03). The sponsor's next submit creates version n+1, and the new review shows the diff against the returned version and carries the old comments forward as resolved or open. A sponsor agent may prepare the resubmission; the declaration and the owner's approval are human.
- Go-live approval: `POST /v1/console/offerings/{id}/request-go-live` (maker CAPM, or CAPM's agent as maker) opens A14 `listing.go_live`, policy `4E+Q`, checker set `[[CCO]]` (the platform CCO is mandatory on every tenant offering, SoD #15; a tenant checker can never satisfy it). The `+Q` custody leg resolves to `not_applicable` on this request, the same pattern A38 uses for `offer.revert`: **no custody operation is bound to it**, because the token's deployment and compliance configuration are J12 step 9, a separate ADM-14 approval under owner quorum executed by A27 and A28. The request may be opened while blockers exist (its payload lists them, as the design's APR-2290 does), but the checker's approve is refused by the precheck hook this node registers with A14 unless: review `cleared`; the `due_diligence`, `legal`, `valuation` and `trustee` sign-offs present; every due-diligence item present and current; zero G17 flags open; G19 and G20 pass (G20 under A19's `valuation.max_age_days`); G21 pass (not `counsel_pending`); every out-of-limits item decided; document versions and the declaration still effective; rule pack still effective; simulation fresh; `published_at` null; no active hold; and the dates still leave a real offer period: `closes_at − max(now, opens_at) ≥ offer_period_days_min` and `closes_at − opens_at ≤ offer_period_days_max`, else `OFR_DATES_STALE` (fix-it: return to draft with new dates). The approved effector, in one transaction, runs `compliance_review -> approved` and then `approved -> scheduled` (A03 actor CAPM: the maker's request schedules the offer, the product map's "Schedules the offer"), stamps `go_live_approval_id` and `published_at`, emits `offering.approved` and `offering.scheduled`, and from that instant the public read contract may show the listing as "Coming soon". The design's APR-2290 "Listing publication" with checks "Valuation current (G20) Pass · Proof of property (G19) Pass · Marketing claims (G17) 2 flags · Residency rule set" is this request's payload; in the seed (A06) APR-2290 stays open and unapprovable, blocked by the two G17 flags and the missing insurance certificate.

**5. Opening, live raise, holds and pause, extension, material change, and the close functions A38 calls (SPN-07 and ADM-08 behaviour, J12 steps 9-12).**

- **Opening is automatic (recorded decision, `offer.open_mode = automatic`, `set_by_founder`).** `offerings.schedule_sweep` (every minute under the clock, idempotent) runs `scheduled -> live` at `opens_at` (or on the next tick when `opens_at` already passed) under A03's `start_reached` guard, and only when all of these hold: the vehicle's token row in A27 is SM-06 `configured` (J12 step 9, recorded by A27's `verifyConfigured`) and its `rules_hash` equals A19's `expectedOnchainParams(vehicleId)` hash (`OFR_TOKEN_NOT_CONFIGURED`); A43 reports the listing page and claims approved (J12 step 10, `OFR_MARKETING_NOT_APPROVED`); no active hold (`OFR_ON_HOLD`); and the offer-period rule above still holds (`OFR_DATES_STALE`). J12 step 11's "Goes live" G13 act is the `listing.go_live` approval that scheduled the offer, and its on-chain half is the configured-token condition; if the founder wants a separate human act at `opens_at`, that is a new A14 row and an A03 guard, not a flag here. When `opens_at` has passed and a condition fails, the offer stays `scheduled` (public "Coming soon"), the sweep raises Sev2 `offer.open_blocked` to CAPM (and TOKO for the token condition) once per cause, and opening happens on the first tick after the cause clears. Each transition fires once, with one audit event, one outbox event and, at `live`, the A13 clock `offer.close` opened with deadline `closes_at`.
- **Caps.** On every snapshot recompute this node runs `live -> soft_cap_reached` the first time `funded_units ≥ soft_cap_units` (A03 guard `soft_cap_reached`, fed `raisedCents = funded_units × unit_price_cents`, subtotals only). Acceptance for G11 is bounded by `acceptance_ceiling_units`: `hard_cap_units` for `first_come`; `hard_cap_units + floor(hard_cap_units × oversubscription_cap_bps / 10,000)` for `pro_rata` (orders beyond the hard cap are accepted only to be scaled back at allocation, A37). The **hard-cap close** is due for both rules when `funded_units ≥ hard_cap_units` (A03 guard `end_or_hard_cap`, raised ≥ hard cap); for `first_come` G11 makes this an exact equality, for `pro_rata` funded may pass the hard cap up to the ceiling before the close lands. Cap facts for G11 come from `getOfferFacts(offeringId, asOf)` in this module's public interface, registered with A19's `FactProvider`: `state` (the **effective** state: `suspended` whenever a hold is active, so any G11 implementation that accepts only `live` or `soft_cap_reached` refuses held offers with `OFFER_NOT_LIVE`), `accepting_orders`, `remaining_acceptable_units`, `remaining_to_hard_cap_units`, `min_ticket_units`, `max_ticket_units`, `allocation_rule`, `acceptance_ceiling_units`, `holder_cap_units`, `soft_cap_reached`, `hard_cap_reached`, `close_due` (`closes_at` passed or `hard_cap_reached`), `close_blocked_by` (`reconfirm` | `suspended` | null). The buy stepper's G11 row "Within hard cap · funds held until soft cap" and "Offer closes {date}. Money waits in escrow; refunded automatically if the soft cap isn't reached." are rendered from these facts.
- **Exported transition functions (the only way the close, fail, finalise, mint and refund transitions run).** `index.ts` exports, each checking A03's guard with facts it computes itself and refusing otherwise with `SM04_ILLEGAL_TRANSITION` or the named code: `closeOffering({offeringId, trigger: 'end_date' | 'hard_cap' | 'close_early', triggerRef})` (`live -> closed` when the soft cap is missed at `closes_at`; `soft_cap_reached -> closed` at `closes_at`, at the hard cap, or on an approved `offer.close_early`; `close_early` from `live` refused with `OFR_CLOSE_EARLY_BEFORE_SOFT_CAP`; refused with `OFR_RECONFIRM_PENDING` while a re-confirmation window is open; idempotent: a second call for the same trigger returns the existing result), `markFinalising(offeringId, allocationId)` (`closed -> finalising`, guard `allocation_locked` and soft cap reached), `markMinted(offeringId, reconRunId, approvalId)` (`finalising -> minted`, only on a reconciled mint with the client-money delta zero), `failOffering(offeringId, reason: 'soft_cap_missed' | 'revert', approvalId?)` (`closed -> failed`, or `live -> closed -> failed` in one transaction for an approved revert of a live offer), `markRefunding`, `markRefunded`, `withdrawDuringReconfirmation(offeringId, approvalId)` (`reconfirming -> refunding`, guard `offer_withdrawn`). A38 calls them in process through `index.ts`; `POST /v1/internal/offerings/{id}/transition` exposes the same functions to service principals for tests and the outbox consumer, with an allow-list: A37 (`soft_cap_check` recompute only), A38 (`close`, `finalising`, `minted`, `failed`, `refunding`, `refunded`, `withdrawn`), A48 (`hold`, `release`). Any other service, and every human or token principal, gets 403. A mint request for an offering not in `finalising` is refused by this node's fact (`OFR_NOT_FINALISING`) and by A14's supply guard; the design's OP-7730 "Mint on offer close · Unley Road" while the offer is still open (design bug 6) is resolved by A06 (UNL01 closed 21 Sep and `finalising`), and this node's tests refuse the transition an open offer would need.
- **Holds (no state change) and pause.** SM-04 has only `live -> suspended` and `suspended -> live`, both human-only for CCO in A03. So every stop that is not a human pause of a `live` offer is an `offering_hold` row, valid in any non-terminal state, and never a transition:
  - Human pause, `offer.pause`, `SGL+R` (CCO acts with a passkey; the other role ratifies within 2 hours through A14 and A13), reason mandatory (published on the property page as "Offer paused · {reason category}"). On a `live` offer: `live -> suspended` plus a `pause` hold. On a `soft_cap_reached` offer: a `pause` hold only; the state stays `soft_cap_reached`, so the soft-cap fact is never lost. A pause is a protective act (A03 kind `act`, `delegable: false`): a staff agent can neither execute nor draft it, and a token calling `POST .../pause` is refused `AGNT_NOT_ALLOWED` (reason code `G22_SCOPE_NON_DELEGABLE`) on the `requiresHuman` route. The product map names "CAPM or CCO" for the act; A03's actor cell names CCO only, so this node accepts CCO until A03 adds CAPM (spec defect reported against A03).
  - Resume, `offer.resume`, `4E` (maker CAPM or CAPM's agent as maker, checker CCO): clears the `pause` hold and, when the state is `suspended`, runs `suspended -> live` with the CCO checker as actor; if funded units crossed the soft cap while suspended, the same transaction's recompute runs `live -> soft_cap_reached`. A lapsed ratification opens the restorative `offer.resume` request as A03 §4.3 describes, and its human decision resumes the offer once.
  - A48's kill switches, any G10 halt for the vehicle, and an A33 tenant suspension place `kill_switch`, `halt` or `tenant_suspended` holds through `setHold`/`releaseHold` in `index.ts` (service principals, so they never run the human-only `live -> suspended`). A hold on `live` or `soft_cap_reached` stops intake (effective state `suspended` for G11); on `approved` or `scheduled` it blocks `scheduled -> live`; on `draft` or `compliance_review` it blocks submit and the go-live approval (`OFR_ON_HOLD`). Existing escrow is untouched in every case.
  - The public and sponsor labels read "Paused" whenever any hold is active on a public state.
  - **Close while suspended.** SM-04 has no `suspended -> closed`. When `closes_at` passes on a `suspended` offer, `close_blocked_by = suspended`, A38's close is refused, and this node raises Sev2 `offer.close_blocked` to CAPM and CCO once. The humans either extend (`offer.extend`) or resume: a resume past `closes_at` places a `closing` hold in the same transaction as `suspended -> live`, so no order is accepted, and A38's next close sweep closes the offer. A holds-only design needs no change to A03's table; adding `soft_cap_reached -> suspended`, `suspended -> soft_cap_reached` and `suspended -> closed` to A03 would simplify this and is reported as an optional A03 change, not assumed.
- **Extension and material change.** `offer.extend` (`4E`, CAPM -> CCO, sponsor request first) moves `closes_at` by at most `extension_days_max` and at most `extensions_max` times (`OFR_EXTENSION_LIMIT`); `offer.material_change` (`4E`, CAPM -> CCO) changes any field in `material_fields.ts` (unit price, total units, soft cap, tickets, holder cap, lock-in, close date, allocation rule, residency rule, distribution policy, reserve months, retained stake, fee lines, IM or PDS version, TMD version). Both are allowed only from `live` or `soft_cap_reached` (and `offer.extend` also from `suspended`), write a new `offering_version`, emit `offering.material_change` with the diff, and open a re-confirmation window: every order in `funded`, `awaiting_funds` or `submitted` must be re-confirmed by its investor within 5 business days (`offer.reconfirm_days`, `set_with_counsel`). On a `live` offer the window is A03's extension (b): `live -> reconfirming` (guard `linked_approval_closed`, SVCA), then `reconfirming -> live` at the deadline (guard `reconfirm_window_closed`, run by the schedule sweep) or `reconfirming -> refunding` if A38 withdraws the offer. On a `soft_cap_reached` offer A03 has no edge to `reconfirming`, so the window is a `reconfirm` hold with the same deadline (intake stops, `close_blocked_by = reconfirm`), released at the deadline. This node exposes `GET /v1/offerings/{id}/reconfirmations` (order id, deadline, state) and the deadline clock through A13; A37 owns the investor's re-confirm action and refunds any order not re-confirmed at the deadline; the offer cannot close, be extended again or go to `finalising` while any window is open (`OFR_RECONFIRM_PENDING`; A38's close sweep defers and this node raises Sev3 `offer.close_deferred` to CAPM). A non-material edit (`offer.edit_minor`: description text, photo captions, contact details) is `4E` with no new version and no re-confirmation, and the field list is the complement of `material_fields.ts`, tested exhaustively.
- **Close early, cancel and revert belong to A38.** A sponsor's `close_early` or `cancel` request (item 1) is the evidence for A38's `offer.close_early` (`4E`, CAPM -> CCO) or `offer.revert` (`4E+Q`, CAPM -> CFO), drafted by CAPM through A38's `POST /v1/settlement/offerings/{id}/close` or `/revert`; A38's effectors call `closeOffering` or `failOffering` above. This node registers no effector for those codes and has no `offer.cancel_and_refund` or `offer.revert_and_refund` code.

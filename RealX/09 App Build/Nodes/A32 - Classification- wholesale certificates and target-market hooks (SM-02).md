# A32 - Classification: wholesale certificates and target-market hooks (SM-02)

**The service that owns the facts behind "may this investor buy?" under the wholesale test: an `InvestorClassification` per investor with its basis (accountant's certificate valid two calendar years from the certificate date, A$500k in this offer with a CCO check, sophisticated, professional), the CLSR review queue behind ADM-11, the SM-02 lifecycle driven by human decisions and by A31's claim events, the batched certificate-expiry alerts at T-60 and T-30, renewal that never removes a valid claim, rejection and revocation under one approval, the G04 facts A19's evaluator reads, and the target-market questionnaire model that the P2 scheme node fills.**

**Build status:** not started. **Milestone:** AM3 (Business services). **Lane:** Services. **Planner:** Opus 5.5 (high). **Builder:** Grok 4.7 (xhigh). **Node QA:** Opus 5.5 (high, every loop). **Graph QA:** Fable 5.1 (high, at milestone close).
**Estimate:** 8-12 agent-hours (build, challenge and verify loops).

**Depends on:** [[A30 - Investor onboarding, KYC cases and KYB (SM-01)]], [[A31 - On-chain identity and claims service (V4 identity agent)]]
**Unlocks:** [[A56 - Onboarding flow ONB-01 to ONB-10 with entity branch]], [[A68 - Console- identity review, wholesale certificates, investor directory and Investor 360]], [[A90 - P2- registered scheme, sub-funds, PDS and TMD, target market (G05), retail cooling-off]]

**Oracle rule:** The node QA writes hidden oracle tests from this spec to `<workspace>\gauntlet\hidden\A32\` before the build starts ([[App Gauntlet]] §2). The builder never reads that folder; the cases are published into the repo only after the gate passes. The oracle scope is the authority this node decides: who may buy under G04 (the G04 facts at every clock instant, whatever the sweep has done), the calendar arithmetic of `valid_until` for every basis, the SM-02 table under A03's engine, the maker-checker paths (the CCO approval on the ticket path, the single approval on revocation), the decision-safety matrix (no decision by an agent, token or service principal) and the claim-agreement invariant with A31 (eventual, see below).

This note is not legal advice. The wholesale tests (s708, s761G, s761GA), the two-year certificate life, the six-month certificate age at upload, who may assess a sophisticated investor, how long a sophisticated or professional classification lasts, how the A$500,000 test applies to several orders or a scaled-back allocation, and whether the wholesale declaration must be personal are counsel questions; each is a data row with a default, and counsel's answers change rows, not code.

## Build brief

This node is API-only: module `classification` in the Fastify monolith (`services/api/src/modules/classification/`) with its worker jobs and outbox consumers registered from the same folder through A09's job registry. It draws no screen. ONB-06 and the ONB-10 "Wholesale status" row are built by A56, ADM-11 and the ADM-09 "wholesale to" chip by A68, ONB-07 and the outside-TMD report by A90, the ACC-01 "Wholesale status · Valid to" row and the ACC-03 reminder by the account and notifications screen nodes, all on top of this module's API. Every rule below is enforced in the API, never only in a screen; every read contract is in the OpenAPI document from the first commit so `packages/sdk` and, later, the MCP catalogue see it. The only source of time is A09's injectable clock; `Date.now()` and argument-less `new Date()` are forbidden in the owned path and caught by `npm run verify`.

**Who owns what (single sources of truth)**

- **A19 owns the G04 and G05 evaluators and every threshold they read.** The rule pack `AU-W-2026.1` holds `wholesale.min_offer_investment_cents` (A$500,000), `wholesale.expiring_warn_days` (30) and the certificate life. This node defines no evaluator, no reason-code table of its own and no copy of those thresholds; it implements the classification slice of A19's `FactProvider` interface and reads the three parameters through A19's pack reader. The certificate life must be expressed as `wholesale.certificate_valid_years = 2` (calendar years), not `certificate_valid_days = 730`: 12 Mar 2026 plus two calendar years is 12 Mar 2028, which is 731 days. That parameter change is an A19 follow-up loop (A19.n) that must land before this node's Specify step is frozen; this node never edits A19's path. A cross-node test in this module loads A19's effective pack and fails if `certificate_valid_years` is missing, is not 2, or if a `certificate_valid_days` key is still present, and fails if A31's 30-day `expiring` marking disagrees with `expiring_warn_days`.
- **A31 owns the on-chain claim and the one expiry sweep.** A31 issues, replaces and revokes the `WHOLESALE_AU` claim, runs the only expiry sweep (`claims.sweep-expired`, 02:00 Australia/Sydney) and emits `claim.expiring` (once at 60 and once at 30 days) and `claim.expired`. This node never calls A31 synchronously and never runs a second expiry sweep: it publishes classification events on the outbox and consumes A31's claim events to set `claim_state` and fire SM-02 transitions.
- **This node owns** the classification rows, the reviews and their decisions, the SM-02 transitions, the investor reminder wording, the batched CLSR alert, and the time-derived G04 facts.

**Basis vocabulary (one enum for A19, A31, A32 and A68)**

- The basis enum is A31's frozen set: `certificate`, `s708_500k_offer`, `sophisticated_s761GA`, `professional`. This node uses it exactly in its model, routes, events, facts and tests. The enum should live in `packages/domain` (A03's path); until A03 hosts it (an A03 follow-up request), this node imports A31's exported constant and a test asserts the two are identical. A19's current name `offer_500k` must become `s708_500k_offer` in the same A19 follow-up loop as the certificate life.
- ONB-06 has three option cards and the model has four bases. `requested_option` records the card the investor chose: `certificate` (card 1), `s708_500k_offer` (card 2), `sophisticated_or_professional` (card 3). For cards 1 and 2, `basis` is set at request time. For card 3, `basis` stays null until the CLSR's `approve` decision, which must name `sophisticated_s761GA` or `professional` (`CLS_BASIS_UNRESOLVED` otherwise); `classification.valid` always carries a final basis from the enum. The contract test A56 reuses covers all three cards, including a card-3 approval that names each final basis.

**Data and identifiers**

- `investor_classification` (A04 table, one row per SM-02 instance): `id` (`CLS-`), `tenant_id` (platform tenant; classification is platform-level, one KYC and one classification for every sponsor), `investor_id`, `requested_option`, `basis` (enum above, nullable only for card 3 before the decision), `offer_id` (mandatory for `s708_500k_offer`, null otherwise), `certificate_date` (civil date, `certificate` only), `decision_date` (civil date in `Australia/Sydney`), `valid_until` (civil date in `Australia/Sydney`; never null once the row is `valid`, rule per basis below), `accountant_name`, `accountant_body_and_number`, `accountant_verified` (`unverified`, `verified`, `not_verifiable`), `evidence_doc_ids[]` (A35 document ids once A35 lands; A04 `document` rows until then), `declaration_id` (the investor's own passkey-signed declaration, see agents), `state` (SM-02 enum from A03), `decided_by`, `decision_reason_code`, `approval_id` (the A14 request on the ticket path and on revocation), `claim_id` (the current A31 `IdentityClaim` for topic `WHOLESALE_AU`), `claim_state` (`pending`, `issued`, `replacing`, `expired`, `revoked`), `renews_id` (the previous classification when this row is a re-application after `expired`), standard `created_*`, `updated_*` and RLS columns. If A04's row lacks any of these columns the node requests them as A04's follow-up loop (A04.n) before Build, per A04's own rule; no column is added inside this module.
- `classification_review` (module-own additive table through A04's module-schema registry, insert-only except `state` and `decision*`): one row per review act: `kind` (`initial`, `rfi_reply`, `renewal`, `periodic`, `referral_cco`), `opened_at`, `opened_by` (a human, or `SVCA` for automatic routing), `routing_reason` (for example `accountant_not_verifiable`; routing has no decision meaning), `assigned_role` (CLSR, or CCO on a referral), `evidence_doc_ids[]`, `draft_notes` (may be written by a staff agent as maker), `decision` (`approve`, `reject`, `request_info`, `refer_cco`; human only), `final_basis` (card 3), `reason_code`, `decided_at`, `decided_by`, `assertion_id` (the decider's fresh WebAuthn assertion), `state` (`open`, `awaiting_cco`, `decided`, `withdrawn`). Reviews are the queue objects ADM-11 lists; the classification row is the standing fact G04 reads.
- `tmd_questionnaire` and `tmd_response` (module-own additive tables, P2 data model only): `tmd_questionnaire` per offering: `offering_id`, `tmd_doc_id`, `version`, `questions[]` (code, text, answer type, options), `criteria` (a JSON rule over answers: objectives income or growth, timeframe, need to sell quickly, share of total investments), `effective_from`; `tmd_response` per investor per questionnaire version: `answers`, `result` (`in_market`, `outside_market`), `evaluated_at`, `declaration_id`, `flagged_report_id` (filled by A90). This node ships the tables, the zod schemas and one pure function `evaluateTmdResponse(answers, criteria) -> in_market | outside_market` tested on fixture questionnaires. A90 owns every TMD route (including `POST /v1/tmd/responses`, its body, validation and `requiresHuman` flag), ONB-07, ADM-26 and the outside-TMD report; A19 owns the G05 evaluator that reads the stored `result`.
- `classification_policy` rows (data, seeded from this note, each with a `set_with_counsel` marker). They hold only what A19's pack does not: `max_certificate_age_days_at_upload = 183` (the design's "signed within the last 6 months"); `valid_months_by_basis = { sophisticated_s761GA: 24, professional: 24 }`; `periodic_review_months_by_risk_tier = { high: 12, medium: 24, low: null }` (null means no extra periodic review; the basis interval still applies); `sophisticated_assessor = licensee` (who assesses under s761GA); `joint_account_rule = per_investor_record` (a joint account is one investor record and one classification); `ticket_amount_rule = per_order` (each order must reach the threshold on its own; the counsel alternative `sum_in_offer` adds an investor's orders in the same offer); `ticket_scale_back_rule = exclude_below_min` (a ticket-basis order whose allocated amount falls below the threshold is excluded and refunded in full; the counsel alternative `no_scale_back` allocates ticket orders in full or not at all). A seed test asserts no key here duplicates an A19 parameter.

**`valid_until` per basis (never null once `valid`)**

- `certificate`: `certificate_date` plus `certificate_valid_years` calendar years (29 February lands on 28 February; 12 Mar 2026 gives 12 Mar 2028; 30 Jun 2026 gives 30 Jun 2028), using the `packages/domain` civil-date function A31 also uses.
- `s708_500k_offer`: the last day of the offer's settlement window (the offer's close date plus its settlement window, read from the A04 offering row through a read-only `OfferFacts` port with a fixture adapter in tests until A36 populates it). Ticket rows are offer-scoped: they are left out of investor reminders, out of the CLSR alert and out of the `expiring_*` counts, and they end through their own trigger (spec gap (v)).
- `sophisticated_s761GA` and `professional`: `decision_date` plus `valid_months_by_basis[basis]`, brought forward to the next periodic-review date by risk tier when that is earlier. This is the "periodic-review date" A31 expects in the payload.
- A classification is valid through the end of its `valid_until` date in Sydney time; its expiry instant is 00:00:00 Australia/Sydney local time (AEST or AEDT) on the following day.

**SM-02 lifecycle, verbatim from A03**

- The state table is A03's `sm-02-wholesale-certificate.ts`: 9 states (`requested`, `evidence_submitted`, `under_review`, `rfi`, `valid`, `expiring`, `expired`, `rejected`, `revoked`) and 11 transitions (`requested -> evidence_submitted`, `evidence_submitted -> under_review`, `under_review -> rfi : insufficient evidence`, `rfi -> evidence_submitted`, `under_review -> valid : CLSR issues claim`, `under_review -> rejected : CLSR`, `valid -> expiring : 30 days before expiry`, `expiring -> valid : renewed`, `expiring -> expired : two years reached`, `valid -> revoked : misrepresentation`, `expired -> requested : re-applies`). Every change goes through `packages/domain` `transition()`; anything else is refused with `SM_FORBIDDEN` wrapped as `SM02_ILLEGAL_TRANSITION`. The guard `certificate_two_years_reached` and the rights helper `SM-02.rights(state) -> { buy, sell, hold }` come from A03; this node adds no state, no transition and no guard. `rights` is: `valid` and `expiring` buy, sell, hold; `expired`, `revoked`, `rejected` and every pre-approval state sell and hold only (holdings acquired under an earlier valid classification are never touched).
- `POST /v1/classification` (investor, delegable to a scoped agent): creates the row in `requested` with `requested_option` and, for `s708_500k_offer`, the `offer_id`; refuses a second open row for the same investor and option (`CLS_ALREADY_OPEN`) and a ticket row for an offer that is not `open` (`CLS_OFFER_NOT_OPEN`); an entity investor's request is accepted only on behalf of a verified signatory recorded by A30's KYB (`CLS_SIGNATORY_REQUIRED`); an investor not yet past A30's identity verification is refused (`CLS_IDENTITY_FIRST`, G01 before G04, matching the ONB step order).
- `POST /v1/classification/{id}/evidence` (investor, delegable to a scoped agent): attaches evidence and validates it per option. `certificate` needs the certificate document, accountant name and body number, and a `certificate_date` that is not in the future (`CLS_CERTIFICATE_DATE_FUTURE`) and not older than `max_certificate_age_days_at_upload` (`CLS_CERTIFICATE_TOO_OLD` names the cut-off date); `s708_500k_offer` needs nothing beyond the declaration (the amount is a G04 fact on each order); `sophisticated_or_professional` needs the assessor's written assessment document (who signs is data) or the evidence of the professional-investor limb claimed. Missing items return `CLS_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE` naming them.
- `POST /v1/classification/{id}/declaration` (investor only, `requiresHuman: true`, fresh passkey assertion `purpose: classification_declaration` over the canonical hash of the row's option, offer and evidence set): records `declaration_id`. The row fires `requested -> evidence_submitted -> under_review` in one transaction only when both the complete evidence and a declaration over that exact evidence set exist, whichever arrives second; evidence changed after the declaration makes it stale (`CLS_DECLARATION_STALE`) and the person declares again. The transaction opens an `initial` review assigned to CLSR. From `rfi`, the same pair fires `rfi -> evidence_submitted -> under_review` and the review row is a new `rfi_reply`.
- `POST /v1/classification/reviews/{id}/decide` (staff; CLSR, or CCO on a referral): takes `decision`, `final_basis` (card 3 on `approve`), `reason_code` (mandatory on `reject` and `request_info`), and a fresh WebAuthn assertion from A11 (`purpose: classification_decision`, no older than 5 minutes, challenge = the review's canonical hash), so a decision cannot be made by a token, an agent or a replay (`CLS_DECIDER_MUST_BE_HUMAN` before any scope check, then `FRESH_ASSERTION_*` from A11). `request_info` fires `under_review -> rfi` and tells the investor what is missing (the reason code's plain-English text). `reject` fires `under_review -> rejected` (terminal for this row; the investor may start a new row). `approve` on `certificate`, `sophisticated_s761GA` and `professional` fires `under_review -> valid` directly (the product map's "no checker for certificates"); on `s708_500k_offer` it creates an A14 `ApprovalRequest` with `action_code = classification.ticket.approve`, policy `4E` (maker CLSR, checker CCO, the product map's "CCO on the A$500k-in-this-offer path"), whose effector fires `under_review -> valid` when the CCO approves, and the review sits in `awaiting_cco` until then. A reviewer who is the investor, a delegate of the investor or the agent's human is refused (`CLS_SELF_REVIEW`; A11's SoD, nobody classifies themselves); a CCO checker who opened or made the review is refused by A14 (`APR_*`).
- Accountant verification: `POST /v1/classification/reviews/{id}/accountant-check` (CLSR or the CLSR's staff agent as maker) records only `accountant_verified` from a lookup through the `identity` adapter's professional-register stub (sandbox: magic name suffixes give `verified`, `not_verifiable`) plus a routing flag. It writes no `decision`. When the result is `not_verifiable`, an automatic routing rule executed by `SVCA` opens a `referral_cco` review (`opened_by = SVCA`, `routing_reason = accountant_not_verifiable`, no decision meaning) and the row stays `under_review` (the product map's "Accountant not verifiable: referred to CCO"); approval is then the CCO's human decision on that review with the same assertion rule. A human CLSR may also refer by deciding `refer_cco` with a fresh assertion.
- Periodic review by risk tier: the daily job `classification.periodic_review` (idempotent) opens a `periodic` review for every `valid` row whose investor's risk tier has a non-null interval and whose last decided review is older than that interval; the row's state does not change. A `request_info` decision on a periodic review is refused as a transition (no `valid -> rfi` in the table); the reviewer records the reason code, the investor is asked for the document, and the classification stays valid (spec gap (iii)).

**Claim agreement with A31 (stub contract, recorded in both notes)**

This section is the agreement with A31. It is written into `app/services/api/src/modules/classification/CONTRACT-A31.md` and must match A31's README stub for `classification.valid`. Where A31's frozen spec does not yet say the same thing, the difference is a follow-up loop on A31 (A31.n), listed at the end of this section; this node builds against the contract, not around it.

- **Published by A32 on the outbox (A09), never as a synchronous call:** `classification.valid { classification_id, investor_id, basis, certificate_date?, valid_until, offer_id?, approval_id? }`; `classification.renewed { classification_id, investor_id, basis, certificate_date?, valid_until, previous_claim_id, approval_id? }`; `classification.revoked { classification_id, investor_id, claim_id, reason_code, approval_id }`. The `valid_until` in the payload is the value A31 stores on the claim; a contract test asserts A31's own calendar computation from `certificate_date` gives the same date.
- **Consumed by A32 from A31:** `claim.issued { claim_id, investor_id, topic, source_id }` sets `claim_id` and `claim_state = issued` (after a renewal, the new claim becomes current and `claim_state` returns from `replacing` to `issued`); `claim.expiring { claim_id, days_left }` at 60 and 30; `claim.expired { claim_id }`; `claim.revoked { claim_id, approval_id }` sets `claim_state = revoked`. Each consumer is idempotent on `(event_id)` and ignores an event whose `claim_id` is not the row's current claim (for example the old claim of a renewed row expiring later).
- **SM-02 transitions driven by those events (actor `SVCA`, code `AUTO`, recorded as transition audit events, not as review decisions):** `claim.expiring` with `days_left: 60` writes `classification.reminder` (`days_left: 60`) for the investor; with `days_left: 30` it fires `valid -> expiring : 30 days before expiry` and writes `classification.reminder` (`days_left: 30`). `claim.expired` fires `expiring -> expired` under guard `certificate_two_years_reached` (evaluated against the clock and `valid_until`); a row still `valid` because the 30-day event was missed moves `valid -> expiring -> expired` in one handler with both audit events. Ticket rows ignore `claim.expiring` (no reminder, no `expiring` state) and end on `claim.expired` through spec gap (v). Holdings, register entries and the ability to sell are untouched; only `rights.buy` turns false.
- **Rows without an issued claim.** If the investor has no on-chain identity yet (ONB-08 not done), A31 holds the claim in `pending`. The contract requires A31's sweep to emit `claim.expiring` and `claim.expired` for `pending` claims too, so SM-02 still moves. Buying never depends on this: G04 reads the time-derived facts below, and the mint-time claim check in A19 and A27 means an order placed in the gap is never minted without the claim.
- **Eventual agreement, not a transaction.** An on-chain operation cannot sit inside a Postgres transaction, so the invariant is eventual: after the outbox is drained and A31's next sweep has run, every row in `valid` or `expiring` has a current claim in `issued` (or `pending` while the identity does not exist), and every `expired` or `revoked` row has no unrevoked claim. During the gap `claim_state` is `pending` or `replacing` and G04 is still correct from the time-derived facts. A failed publish or a failed A31 consumer is retried from the outbox; nothing is rolled back.
- **Drift check (detection only).** The daily job `classification.expiry_digest` at 05:00 Australia/Sydney (after A31's 02:00 sweep) compares each row's stored state and `claim_state` with the time-derived state and with A31's claim row. A row that still disagrees one full cycle after the event that should have moved it writes one audit event and one outbox event `classification.state_lag { classification_id, stored_state, derived_state, claim_state, since }`. The job never fires a transition and never touches a claim.
- **A31 follow-ups this contract needs (not edited here):** A31 must consume `classification.renewed` (issue the new claim, then revoke the old one, in that order) and `classification.revoked` (bind its `CustodyOp` to the carried `approval_id` instead of opening its own request); publish `claim.issued` and `claim.revoked` on the outbox, not only as audit events; emit `claim.expiring` and `claim.expired` for `pending` claims; take `valid_until` for the sophisticated and professional bases from the payload; and drop its statement that A13 aggregates `claim.expiring` into the ADM-01 task, because this node raises that alert (below).

**Expiry alerts and investor reminders**

- `classification.expiry_digest` (same 05:00 job, idempotent, replayable) raises A13's registered alert type `investor.certificate_expiring` (Sev4, owner CLSR, `target_screen` ADM-11) twice when the counts are non-zero, each batched and deduplicated per Sydney date and threshold: the T-30 batch (dedup key `certificate_expiring:30:<date>`) counts non-ticket rows whose derived state is `expiring`, with the design's title "N wholesale certificates expire within 30 days" and description "Investors will be blocked from new offers (G04)", so the `adCmd` row reads exactly as the design when N = 14; the T-60 batch (dedup key `certificate_expiring:60:<date>`) counts non-ticket rows with 31 to 60 days left, titled "N wholesale certificates expire within 60 days" with the same description. Parameters carry counts only, never names (A13 refuses PII).
- Investor reminders are the `classification.reminder` outbox events above (T-60 and T-30, non-ticket rows only); A43 renders ACC-03 "Certificate expires in 60 days · Renew your wholesale certificate". Delivery to an investor's agent through webhooks is verified by A46 and A100, not here.

**G04 facts (the classification slice of A19's `FactProvider`)**

- `classificationFacts({ investorId, offeringId, actionKind, ticketAmountCents?, now }, params)` in `facts/g04-facts.ts`, a pure function over the investor's classification rows, the offering id and the clock instant passed in, with A19's `certificate_valid_years` and `expiring_warn_days` passed as params. It returns `{ effective_classification_id, basis, stored_state, derived_state, valid_until, expires_at, days_left, scope_offer_id, ticket_amount_cents, claim_state }`. It never reads a clock or a database; the calling adapter loads rows and passes `now`.
- `derived_state` is computed from time, whatever the sweep has done: `expired` once `now >= expires_at` (00:00:00 Sydney on the day after `valid_until`), for every basis including ticket rows past their settlement window; `expiring` when `days_left <= expiring_warn_days` and the row is not past `expires_at`, even while the stored state is still `valid`; otherwise the stored state. The effective classification is the row with the latest `valid_until` among those whose stored state is `valid` or `expiring`, and for `s708_500k_offer` only the row whose `offer_id` equals the offering.
- `ticket_amount_cents` is the amount the calling service passes for the action: the order amount at placement, the allocated amount at allocation and at mint, following `ticket_amount_rule` (`per_order` by default). Re-running G04 on the allocated amount at allocation (after scale-back) and at mint is required for ticket orders under `ticket_scale_back_rule = exclude_below_min`; the calls live in A37 and A38 and are listed as follow-ups there, not built here.
- The G04 contract A19's evaluator must honour over these facts (recorded for A19's follow-up loop; the reason codes are proposed here because A19's spec names none): `pass` when `rights(derived_state).buy` and, for the ticket basis, `ticket_amount_cents >= min_offer_investment_cents`; `warn` `G04_EXPIRING` with the date when `derived_state = expiring`; `fail` with `G04_NO_CLASSIFICATION` (fix-it: complete ONB-06), `G04_UNDER_REVIEW` (fix-it: "usually within 1 business day"), `G04_EXPIRED` (fix-it: "you can hold and sell but not buy; renew to buy again"), `G04_REVOKED`, `G04_REJECTED`, `G04_TICKET_BELOW_500K` (names the shortfall), `G04_TICKET_OTHER_OFFER`. A retail-regime offering (P2) is `not_applicable` for G04.
- G05 is A19's. In P1, and whenever `offers.retail` is off or the regime is wholesale, its result is exactly `{ gate_id: 'G05', result: 'not_applicable', reason_codes: ['G05_PHASE_OFF'], fix_it: 'gate.not_applicable_phase' }`, matching A03's challenge and A19's registry rule (`not_applicable` is neither pass nor fail). With the flag on, A19's G05 reads the investor's latest `tmd_response.result` for the offering's current questionnaire version; the reason-code names for P2 (`TMD_ANSWERS_MISSING` in A19, outside-market variants) are settled by A90 with A19.
- `classificationFor(investorId, offerId?, now)` exported from `index.ts` for A56, A68 and A90: returns `{ basis, requested_option, state, derived_state, valid_until, rights, claim_state, days_left }` or `null`; the ONB-10 row, the buy-step G04 GateRow, the ADM-09 chip and the ACC-01 row are projections of this one shape.

**Agents and tokens (G22, aligned with A11 and A12)**

- Parity with the UI: a scoped investor agent may create the request, attach evidence, open a renewal draft, re-apply after expiry and read `GET /v1/classification/me`. The one personal act is the declaration route, which carries `requiresHuman: true` (A11 refuses agents with `AGNT_NOT_ALLOWED`) because the wholesale-status declaration carries `"counsel"` in A03's `delegable.ts`; A12 refuses any attempt to put it in a token with `SCOPE_PENDING_COUNSEL`, and the message tells the person the declaration must be made by them. A row an agent prepared moves to `under_review` only after the human declaration. If A03's action list has one ONB-06 action flagged `"counsel"` rather than a delegable "prepare classification request" action and a counsel-flagged "make wholesale declaration" action, the split is an A03 follow-up request (A03.n); until it lands, the create, evidence, renew and reapply routes stay `requiresHuman` and the node records that in `A32.qa.md`.
- Staff agents (a CLSR's own tokens) may list the queue, run the accountant check and write `draft_notes` (maker within the human's role); `decide`, `revoke` and the CCO ticket approval refuse any AGNT, token or service principal before scope evaluation (`CLS_DECIDER_MUST_BE_HUMAN`), and the fresh-assertion requirement makes it impossible even with a hypothetical scope. Segregation of duties counts the agent as its human. Every agent call, read or refused, is audited "agent <token prefix> on behalf of <person>" through A10 and appears in the person's activity.

**Renewal, re-application and revocation**

- Renewal (J17 step 5, ACC-01): `POST /v1/classification/{id}/renew` (investor or scoped agent as a draft) is allowed from `valid` and `expiring` (`CLS_NOT_RENEWABLE` otherwise); the renewal review opens after fresh evidence, validated exactly as an initial submission, and a fresh human declaration. The row's state does not change and the existing claim is not touched. On approval from `expiring` the effector fires `expiring -> valid : renewed` with the new `valid_until`, `certificate_date`, evidence and accountant fields; from `valid` (renewed early, before T-30) no transition exists in the table, so the row's fields are updated under the review's decision with the audit event `classification.renewed` (spec gap (iv)). In both cases the node publishes `classification.renewed` and sets `claim_state = replacing`; A31 issues the new claim before revoking the old one, so the investor never lacks a valid claim. A rejected renewal leaves the row, `valid_until` and the claim exactly as they were, and the investor is told what is missing. After `expired`, `POST /v1/classification/{id}/reapply` fires `expired -> requested` and the row runs the table again (`renews_id` links the history; the old claim is already expired).
- Revocation (misrepresentation), one approval end to end: `POST /v1/classification/{id}/revoke` by MLRO or CCO (the maker set of A31's "revoke claim" row; a CLSR or KYCA who suspects misrepresentation refers it through a review note) creates one A14 `ApprovalRequest` under the "revoke claim" policy row (`4E`, checker a second approver from that row's set who is not the maker), reason code `misrepresentation` mandatory. This node builds against that row id as data from A03; if A03's row cannot carry a classification object, A03 adds `classification.revoke` with identical role sets through its own follow-up loop (A03.n), never as a seed inside this module. The effector registered by this node fires `valid -> revoked`, publishes `classification.revoked` carrying the `approval_id`, and emits `classification.rights_changed` so open orders are re-checked by A19 (an order not yet allocated fails G04 and refunds through SM-05's ordinary path). A31 binds its `CustodyOp` to that same `approval_id`, so the admin approval and the custody signature share one ID. Nothing here touches the register or holdings; a revocation is never a forced transfer.

**Spec gaps to settle at the Specify step, before the node QA writes the hidden tests**

(i) `expiring -> revoked` is absent (only `valid -> revoked`), so a misrepresentation found in the last 30 days cannot be revoked by the table; (ii) `rfi` has no expiry, unlike SM-01's 30-day RFI; (iii) no transition leads from `valid` to `rfi` for periodic reviews; (iv) whether `expiring -> valid : renewed` is also the model for an early renewal from `valid`; (v) ticket rows need their own end: proposed `valid -> expired : ticket_scope_ended` (guard: the offer's settlement window has closed), because `certificate_two_years_reached` is the wrong guard for them; (vi) the guard `certificate_two_years_reached` must read as "the `valid_until` expiry instant has passed" so it also serves the sophisticated and professional bases. The proposed resolution is an A03 `extensions.ts` entry with this node as its source: `expiring -> revoked : misrepresentation` and `valid -> expired : ticket_scope_ended`, plus the guard reading in (vi); (ii), (iii) and (iv) are handled without new transitions as described above. The user decides and A03 records it; if declined, the node implements the table verbatim (the effector refuses a revocation at `expiring` with `SM02_ILLEGAL_TRANSITION`; a ticket row past its window keeps its stored state and G04 fails it from `derived_state`) and records the gap in `A32.qa.md`. The hidden tests are written against the settled table.

## Node-specific challenges (node QA)

- **Buying on a dead claim (compliance).** An investor's certificate `valid_until` is yesterday. At 00:00:01 Sydney (before A31's 02:00 sweep), at 01:59, and at 04:59 (after the sweep, before the digest), and again with A31's sweep disabled for three days, A19's G04 over this node's facts must fail with `G04_EXPIRED` and the fix-it text while the stored state may still read `expiring`; the investor's existing holdings and a sell (P2 stub) must still pass. After the sweep runs, the `claim.expired` event must move the row to `expired` with one audit event per transition. A pass on the order at any of those instants fails the node.
- **Warning before the state moves (compliance).** A row still stored as `valid` with 30 days left because the 30-day claim event has not arrived yet: G04 must return `warn G04_EXPIRING` with the date. A pass without the warning fails.
- **Renewal that deletes the safety net (compliance).** A renewal is approved for an investor in `expiring`: the node must publish `classification.renewed` and set `claim_state = replacing`; with A31's consumer stopped, the old claim stays valid and G04 still passes; when the consumer restarts, the new claim is issued before the old one is revoked. A later `claim.expired` for the old claim must be ignored and must not expire the renewed row. A renewal rejected must leave the row, `valid_until` and the claim exactly as before. Any window in which a `valid` or `expiring` row has no unrevoked claim, other than a revocation in flight, fails.
- **Outbox retry, not rollback (resilience).** `classification.valid` is published while A31 is unavailable: the row is `valid` with `claim_state = pending`, the event is retried from the outbox until A31 consumes it exactly once, and a duplicate delivery of `claim.issued` changes nothing. A missing retry, a duplicate claim, or a row stuck in `pending` for more than one cycle without a `classification.state_lag` event fails.
- **Ticket path without the CCO, or below the line (compliance).** A CLSR approves an SMSF's `s708_500k_offer` classification and no A14 request is created, or the CCO approving it is the same person who opened the review, or the investor then places an order for A$400,000 that passes G04: each must be refused (`APR_*` from A14, `CLS_SELF_REVIEW`, `G04_TICKET_BELOW_500K` naming the shortfall). An A$600,000 order whose allocation-time facts carry an allocated amount of A$400,000 must fail G04 at the fact level so the calling service excludes and refunds it. A ticket classification that behaves as a standing claim for a second offer, or appears in the expiry reminders or the certificates alert, fails.
- **The agent as classifier (security, G22).** A CLSR's staff token holding every M07 scope calls `decide` with an assertion captured from its human's earlier decision, and separately an investor's agent calls `POST /v1/classification/{id}/declaration` with a mandate that names the route: both refused before scope evaluation (`CLS_DECIDER_MUST_BE_HUMAN`, `AGNT_NOT_ALLOWED`), one `gate_evaluation` for G22 each, and a refusal audit event plus an outbox event carrying the token prefix and `on_behalf_of_id` exist (the feed A101 later consumes). The staff agent's accountant check must write no `decision`, and a `not_verifiable` result must open the CCO referral as `SVCA` routing. A 200, a refusal that appears only after scope evaluation, or a decision row from a non-human principal fails.
- **Clock and calendar edges (correctness).** Certificates dated 29 Feb 2028 and 31 Aug 2026, a sophisticated classification decided 31 Jan 2027 for a high-risk investor, a digest run at 04:59 and 05:01 Sydney across the daylight-saving change on 4 Oct 2026, and a `valid_until` that falls on that Sunday: `valid_until` must be 28 Feb 2030, 31 Aug 2028 and 31 Jan 2028 (12 months by risk tier, earlier than 24 by basis); the derived state must become `expiring` exactly 30 days before and `expired` at 00:00:00 Sydney on the day after `valid_until`; running the digest twice must not double any event or alert. A UTC day boundary or an off-by-one on either side fails.
- **Design fidelity of the data contract.** With the standard seed, `GET /v1/classification/me` for Alex Morgan must return `basis: certificate`, `valid_until: 2028-06-30`, `state: valid` and the three option title and description pairs byte-exact from design line 819; the reviews endpoint must return `counts.expiring_60 = 14` and `counts.expiring_30 = 14`, and the T-30 alert title "14 wholesale certificates expire within 30 days"; every status string must be in the vocabulary the ADM-11 design extension maps to TONE pills. A new status string, a bare 4xx, a string with ASCII apostrophes, or a count that is typed rather than computed fails the contract test.

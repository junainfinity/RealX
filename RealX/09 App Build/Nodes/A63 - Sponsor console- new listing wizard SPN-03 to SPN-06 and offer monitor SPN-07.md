# A63 - Sponsor console: new listing wizard SPN-03 to SPN-06 and offer monitor SPN-07

**The sponsor's way onto RealX and the view of a raise once it is live: the prototype's four-step listing wizard with one route per step, SPN-03 Property (address, title, type and sub-type, configuration, trust, trustee and custodian, photos; the property type sets the residency floor and the screen says so), SPN-04 Due diligence (the full required pack for the property type, dates and expiry, a Missing or expired item that blocks submission, valuation age for G20 and the lease and rent roll for G19), SPN-05 Offer terms (the design's six fields plus the product map's remaining terms, RealX limits beside each field, an out-of-limits reason box, validation on every save and a live rule simulation), SPN-06 Documents and submit (the information memorandum, trust deed, fee schedule and sponsor declaration, the listing copy with the G17 claims check showing each flagged phrase and where it is, and the G13 sponsor-owner approval that must come from a second person in the tenant), the in-review, changes-requested and withdrawn views of the same wizard, and SPN-07 Offer monitor (the four KPI tiles, the progress card with the soft-cap marker, the latest applications table, escrow and investor count, every SM-04 state after approval, and requests to extend, close early, cancel and refund or change terms that need the sponsor owner and then RealX) — with every figure, blocker, verdict and state coming from A34, A35, A36, A43 and A14 through the public SDK, so the screens decide nothing and a sponsor's agent can do everything the screens do except approve.**

**Build status:** not started. **Milestone:** AM6 (Sponsor console). **Lane:** Sponsor console. **Planner:** Opus 5.5 (high). **Builder:** Grok 4.7 (xhigh). **Node QA:** Opus 5.5 (high, every loop). **Graph QA:** Fable 5.1 (high, at milestone close).
**Estimate:** 12-16 agent-hours (build, challenge and verify loops).

**Depends on:** [[A52 - Web app shells, routing and passkey sign-in (public, investor, sponsor)]], [[A36 - Offerings- offer terms, listing review, raise monitor (SM-04)]], [[A34 - Properties, valuations and proof of property (SM-03)]], [[A35 - Documents, disclosure library, e-sign and acknowledgements (G12)]], [[A43 - Communications, notifications, tasks and marketing claims (G17)]], [[A37 - Orders, escrow, allocation and refunds (SM-05)]] (SPN-07's application rows, escrow balance, `Allotted` and `Refunded` statuses), [[A38 - Finalise-and-mint and revert-and-refund pipelines]] (the `failed`, `refunding`, `refunded` and `minted` states), [[A06 - Seed data and scenario simulator]] (the Marine Parade and Kent Street seed and the scenario simulator), [[A23 - Whole-stack Docker boot (AM1 exit)]] (the Docker stack the Playwright runs use), [[A98 - Partner principals- trustee-RE, valuer, property manager, auditor, regulator observer and licensee users]] (the TP-VAL and TP-PM role table), [[A104 - MCP server generated from the OpenAPI document]] (the MCP catalogue the parity check reads), [[A50 - Design extension A- investor and sponsor screens not in the prototype]] (the drawings of this node's undrawn states, A50 §10 supplements, spec point (b)). All are in earlier milestones (AM0 to AM4), so there is no cycle; the graph's `deps` row for A63 must carry the same list.
**Unlocks:** [[A65 - Sponsor journeys J11, J12, J13, J14, J19 end to end in Docker (AM6 exit)]], [[A80 - End-to-end journeys- sponsor and operations]]

**Oracle rule:** oracle node (`oracle: yes`; the graph's `oracle` flag for A63 must be `true` too). No money, supply or holdings move in this node itself (A36 fixes the terms, A37 holds escrow and A38 mints, and each carries its own hidden tests), but authority is involved: this is the screen where a sponsor commits the terms investors will buy under, where the G13 second person approves `listing.submit`, the withdrawal and every SPN-07 request, and where "Cancel and refund" is requested. So the Oracle stage runs for this node and the node QA writes hidden tests to `<workspace>\gauntlet\hidden\A63\` before the build starts ([[App Gauntlet]] §2): a scripted mock of the A34, A35, A36, A43 and A14 routes that returns planted wrong figures, blockers, stale simulations, block and review flags, second-person refusals, out-of-order validation responses, a reviewer's internal note and an agent-prepared draft (to prove the screens trust only the server and render nothing they were not meant to); the reference-region crops of the prototype's `spNew` (four steps) and `spOffer` at 1280 px and 375 px; the seed-string check for Marine Parade and Kent Street; and the parity checker that diffs this node's action manifest (with its required scope per row) against the SDK, `openapi.json` and A104's MCP catalogue. The builder never reads that folder; the cases are published into the repo after the gate passes.

This note is not legal advice. The sponsor declaration text, whether the declaration must be made by the person rather than their agent, whether terms outside RealX's limits must be disclosed to investors, the re-confirmation window after a material change and the maximum valuation age at listing are counsel or founder rows owned by A33, A35 and A36; this node renders whatever text, parameters and refusal codes those modules return and never hard-codes a legal sentence, a limit or a day count.

## Build brief

**Scope and placement**

- Routes under A52's sponsor shell (`app/(sponsor)/s/[tenant]/`: light sidebar 240 px with the tenant card "Harbour & Co Property · Mia Chen · Sponsor owner", passkey session required, a principal that is not a `sponsor_user` of `[tenant]` gets the group's 404, never a 403). The paths are the frozen rows of A52's `routes.ts`: SPN-03 to SPN-06 `/s/[tenant]/listings/new/[step]` with `step` in `property`, `diligence`, `terms`, `submit` (the product map's route stems) and the draft carried as `?listing=OFR-…` so a refresh, a deep link from the SPN-02 task "Respond to RealX review" (A43 `review.changes_requested`, target screen `spNew`) or a teammate's link resumes the same draft without changing A52's table; SPN-07 `/s/[tenant]/offers/[code]` where `[code]` is the offering short code (Kent Street Terrace = `KST01`). Every `page.tsx` calls A52's `defineScreen({ map, design, title })` with the product-map code of its own step (`SPN-03` … `SPN-07`) and the design key (`spNew`, `spOffer`); the PageHeader eyebrow prints the step's own code, not the prototype's combined "SPN-03–06" (design analysis §6.1, Appendix B: one wizard, each step keeps its ID and route). The sidebar entries "New listing" and "Offer monitor" (design line 864) are A52's; this node supplies their targets.
- One feature folder `apps/web/src/features/sponsor-listing/`: `api.ts` (the SDK calls below and nothing else; no `fetch` outside it, A52's lint), `actions.ts` (the parity manifest read by [[A100 - Agent-parity suite- every UI action has an API and MCP equivalent under the same gates]]), `state/` (the wizard reducer, per-step dirty tracking, the validation and simulation request sequencer, the idempotency-key store), `labels.ts` (the one state -> label -> tone table for listings and offers, fed by A36's `status_labels` vocabulary and A05's `toneFor`, with a test against A36), `errors.ts` (a human sentence for every stable code of A33, A34, A35, A36, A43, A14 and A12 this node can receive), `components/` (`ListingWizard`, `WizardStepper`, `PropertyStep`, `DiligenceStep`, `TermsStep`, `SubmitStep`, `LimitHint`, `OutOfLimitsReason`, `SimulationResult`, `ClaimsFlagList`, `OwnerApprovalRow`, `ReviewCommentsBanner`, `ReadOnlyListing`, `OfferMonitor`, `OfferSwitcher`, `MonitorStateBanner`, `EscrowLine`, `ApplicationsTable`, `RequestDialog`, `RequestsList`, `ReconfirmPanel`, `AgentMarker`), `monitor/OfferMonitorPage.tsx` (the SPN-07 page the `offers\` route re-exports), `__tests__/` (Vitest and Testing Library against the mocked SDK), `e2e/sponsor-listing.spec.ts` (Playwright, picked up by A52's root glob `apps/web/src/features/**/e2e/*.spec.ts`) and `e2e/agent-parity.spec.ts` (this node's own parity harness, below; A100 re-runs it later). Components come from `@realx/ui` (A05): `PageHeader`, `HorizontalStepper`, `TextField` (listing-wizard variant 11/13 px padding, 14 px text), `OptionCard`, `DropBox`, `CheckList` and `CheckRow`, `StatusPill`, `Banner`, `PreviewBanner`, `DecisionBanner`, `KpiGrid` and `KpiTile`, `OfferProgressCard`, `RaiseBar`, `DataTable`, `KeyValueList`, `Button` variants, `Toast`, `ValueChip`, `EmptyState`, plus A50's `TextArea` block for reasons and listing copy.
- Reads run in server components (the draft, the property, the due-diligence pack, the monitor); edits, uploads, validation, simulation, the claims check, submission, approvals and requests run in client components through `packages/sdk` (A09's generated client). No eligibility, money, limit or state rule is evaluated in the browser: hard cap, soft-cap cents and units, per-investor cap units, days left, raised percentages, the residency floor and every blocker come from the server as integers or `formatted` strings and are rendered with A05's `formatAud`, `formatPct`, `formatTokens` and `formatDateAest`; an ESLint rule in the feature forbids `*`, `/`, `%` and `Number(` on any value named `*_cents`, `*_units`, `*_bps`, `price` or `fee`, exactly as A57 does. Money strings follow A02's `formatAud(cents, {decimals})` rule, one decision per surface: the offer-level headline figures (SPN-05's soft-cap echo and hard cap, SPN-07's "Raised" and "To soft cap" KPI tiles, the progress card's raised, soft-cap and hard-cap labels) use `decimals: 0` (A02 rounds half-up in `bigint`, so `A$1,417,500`, `A$1,525,760`, `A$262,240`); the valuation headline on SPN-04 uses `decimals: 0` (A02's property-value rule); the price per token, the minimum and maximum ticket echoes, the fee lines, the escrow balance (client money) and the applications table's "Amount" always use `decimals: 2` (`A$189.00`, `A$2,395.92`). The screens never choose a precision per value. Dates typed by the sponsor are sent as civil `YYYY-MM-DD` strings; A36 converts them to `Australia/Sydney` instants once, and the screen shows the server's echo. No `Date.now()` in the feature (A52's lint).
- What this node does not do: it never creates an A14 request itself (A36's `submit` and `requests` do), never decides a gate, never computes a cap, never shows a listing to the public (A54, only after A36's `approved`), never renders the operator's review (A71's ADM-07) and never edits A62's dashboard, A64's reporting screens or A60's token screens; it links to them.

- (c) **Property order in J12 (settled against A34, A36 and A65).** A34 separates the sponsor sending the property to RealX (`POST /v1/properties/{id}/submit`, which sets `sent_to_realx_at`, needs the screening items present and changes no state; INVM or PART then runs `sourced -> screening`) from A36's offer submission, which requires the property at `spv_formed` or later. This node therefore adds the property's "Send to RealX" action on SPN-04 (below) and renders A36's property-state blocker on SPN-06 as a server blocker ("The property must finish RealX's checks before the offer can be submitted", from the server's sentence) with no rule of its own. The J12 pass criterion pins the property at `spv_formed` when the offer reaches `compliance_review`.
- (d) **The sponsor declaration.** SPN-06 lists "Sponsor declaration" (product map mock "Signed 20 Sep") and A03 marks it `"counsel"` in `delegable.ts`. It is the A35 envelope A36 names as the `sponsor_declaration` member: signed by an SP-OWN or a director of the sponsor in person, with a passkey step-up, over the offering's current `terms_sha256` (e-sign sandbox); submission is refused `OFR_DECLARATION_MISSING` while it is not `signed`, and a token attempting to sign it is refused `G22_SCOPE_PENDING_COUNSEL` (A12's runtime code). If A35 lacks the template row, that is a field-level back-edge to A35, not a local rule.
- (e) **Fields the screens need that the contracts may lack**, confirmed field by field against A36's OpenAPI document at Specify and raised as back-edges when missing: `escrow_balance_cents` and `investors_count` on the sponsor monitor (the product map's SPN-07 components; A36's `raise_snapshot` has the first); `allowed_requests[]` with a reason per disabled kind (extensions used and left, close early only after the soft cap, re-confirmations pending); `review_due_at` on a listing in review (A13's `listing.review` clock); an `etag` on the sponsor draft for `If-Match` so two teammates cannot overwrite each other silently; `last_changed_by` with principal kind and `on_behalf_of` for the agent marker; the soft-delete route for "Discard draft"; and, on the offering and monitor reads, the viewer's own permitted actions (`can_edit`, `can_submit`, `can_request`, `can_decide`) from A03's matrix so no role check runs in the browser.
- (f) **Seed checks.** A06's seed must produce the design's wizard values for Marine Parade (10,000 tokens, A$189.00, soft cap 75 % = A$1,417,500, offer period 1 Oct – 12 Nov 2026, holder cap 5 %, wholesale only) without an unplanted out-of-limits item: A36's default `min_ticket_units = 1` is A$189.00, below its own `min_ticket_cents_min` of A$1,000, so either the fixture sets a minimum ticket of at least 6 tokens or the default changes; that is A36's or A06's data fix, never a UI patch.
- (g) **G17 at submission is the server's, not the screen's (back-edge to A36, raised now; numbered in A36's loop log).** A43 seeds listing copy and IM text so that guarantee and comparison phrases are `review` severity (`block` elsewhere), and A36 treats open flags as warnings at submission and blockers at go-live; A36's precondition only asks that A43's check "has run". This node therefore has no local "block flag disables Submit" rule: the CTA is inert only when the server's blocker list names a G17 blocker. For that list to be complete, A36's `submit` must refuse with A43's `gate_failed` (`gate: G17`, `reason_codes` = A43's rule codes) when the latest A43 report on the listing copy or the current IM version has any `block` flag (possible whenever the severity rows change, which is counsel's data) or is stale for the current `body_sha256`, and `validate` must return the same item as a `claims` blocker. That A36 change must land before this node's build starts. `errors.ts` renders `gate_failed` G17 as "Remove or reword the blocked phrases before you submit" with each flag from the payload; challenge 2 and the G17 pass criterion test the forced SDK submit.
- (h) **Agent mandates on create actions (back-edge to A12, raised now; numbered in A12's loop log).** A12 lists `allowed_vehicle_ids` and `allowed_property_ids` on a mandate but does not say how `vehicles.create` and `properties.create` behave under them. Expected reading, to be confirmed in A12: a mandate with `allowed_vehicle_ids` set refuses `vehicles.create` with `G22_VEHICLE_NOT_ALLOWED` (a new vehicle is by definition outside the list) and allows `properties.create` only when the body's `vehicle_id` is in the list, otherwise `G22_VEHICLE_NOT_ALLOWED` with nothing created; a mandate with `allowed_property_ids` set refuses `properties.create` the same way. This node renders whatever A12 fixes and has no rule of its own; challenge 7 asserts A12's answer.

- Layout as designed (dc lines 558-579): one white card (border `#E3DED4`, radius 14, padding 28, column gap 22) under the page header (eyebrow the step code, h1 Newsreader 40 "New listing", subtitle "Four steps. - Creation: "New listing" on SPN-02 (A62, design line 538) opens `/listings/new/property` with no `listing` parameter; the first successful save creates, in this order, the vehicle if the sponsor chose "New trust" (`POST /v1/sponsor/vehicles`, A33, a `proposed` vehicle), the property (`POST /v1/properties`, A34, `sourced`, due-diligence template instantiated) and the draft offering (`POST /v1/sponsor/offerings` from the property, A36, `draft`, version 1), each under its own `Idempotency-Key` minted once per creation attempt (UUID in `sessionStorage` under a local draft key, wrapped in try/catch), so a double click or a lost response never creates two properties or two drafts. The URL is then replaced with `?listing=OFR-…`.
- Entry guards from the server: a listing in `draft` opens editable; in `compliance_review` it opens read-only (below); in `approved` or any later state the wizard route redirects to `/s/[tenant]/offers/[code]`; `cancelled` opens read-only with the "Withdrawn" banner; a draft of another tenant or an unknown id renders the group's not-found page with no hint. Before the first step renders, the wizard reads the tenant's pause facts (A34 `GET /v1/tenants/{id}/offers-paused` for G19, A33's billing pause, A33's tenant suspension) and shows a Banner at the top of the card when any is set ("New listings are paused: the Bay Road Apartment August report is 11 days overdue (G19)" with a link to SPN-10, A64; "New listings are paused while invoice INV-… is more than 30 days overdue" with a link to SPN-15, A62). Drafting stays possible; "Submit to RealX" is inert and the server refuses anyway (`OFR_SPONSOR_PAUSED_G19` naming the overdue property, or A33's `TENANT_LISTINGS_PAUSED` passed through by A36).
- Who sees what: SP-OWN and SP-OPS see all four steps (product map roles); SP-FIN and SP-IR see the wizard read-only; a valuer or property-manager member of the tenant (A98's TP-VAL, TP-PM, listed on SPN-14) sees only SPN-04 with the due-diligence kinds their role may supply (valuation for TP-VAL; leases and rent roll, compliance certificates for TP-PM, per A98's role table as A34 applies it) and gets the not-found page on the other three steps. The list comes from the server's permissions, never from a role check in the browser.

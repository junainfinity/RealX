# A48 - Halts, kill switches and wind-down

**The single owner of journey J21 and of the emergency controls: one `Halt` record per vehicle and cause (manual, meeting, valuation stale, recon break, token pause, distress, legal hold, kill switch) that is refused unless it carries published reopening criteria, an end date and an approval id, mirrored on chain through the HaltModule; ratification under A14's own `SGL+R` rows (time-boxed halt: the board delegate within 24 h; resale halt, human or system-triggered: CCO within 2 h; kill switch: a second person within 2 h plus the board within 24 h in every phase) that escalates through the alerts service and never lifts anything by itself; three platform kill switches as `SGL+R` (global inflow freeze, marketplace suspend, all agent traffic) that act at once by setting the platform flags in the perimeter module, with marketplace suspend stopping offers and trading but never outflows; the effects of the distressed and legal-hold states of SM-03 (new offers held, distributions paused); the reaction to `exit_approved` (A34's exit by resolution or its court-directed sale from `legal_hold`), the exit-waterfall hand-off to the distributions engine, the single `burn_final` of the whole supply, the register close and `sold → wound_down`; a 7-year retention flag on every trust record; and runbooks R1-R9 as data with "declare incident" as their entry point. G10 reads the halt state from here. The agent-traffic switch here is the platform-wide tier; the per-user and per-tenant tiers are [[A101 - Agent guard- anomaly detection, sensitive-action notices and tiered kill switches]] and plug into the same interface.**

**1. `Halt` records (G10) with mandatory reopening criteria and end date**

- `halts` row: `halt_id` (`HLT-<yyyymmdd>-<6 base32>`), `tenant_id`, `level` (`vehicle` or `platform`), `vehicle_id` (null only for a platform-level halt: the marketplace-suspend halt, cause `kill_switch`, and the R1/R6 platform distribution hold, cause `manual`), `cause` (`manual`, `meeting`, `valuation_stale`, `recon_break`, `token_pause`, `distress`, `legal_hold`, `kill_switch`), `scope[]` (any of `resale_board`, `new_offers`, `distributions`, `withdrawals`, `all`; the default per cause is a seeded row: `manual`, `meeting`, `valuation_stale` and `token_pause` = `resale_board`; `recon_break` = A20's nine actions; `distress` and `legal_hold` = `resale_board`, `new_offers`, `distributions`; `kill_switch` = `resale_board`, `new_offers`, never `distributions` or `withdrawals`, so outflows keep running under marketplace suspend), `opened_by` (a human id, or `system:<module>` for system causes), `approval_id` (mandatory on every row: the `SGL+R` act for human halts and for system-triggered `resale.halt` acts, A34's `M22.distress` or `M22.courtOrder` approval for distress and legal hold, A28's `token.pause` approval for token pause, the engage approval for the kill-switch halt), `trigger_ref` (the source event of a system-triggered act, as A14 stores it), `reopening_criteria[]` (at least one; each `{code, text, check}` where `check` is `system:<criterion code>` for a criterion this module can evaluate, or `human` for one the clearing checker must attest), `ends_at` (mandatory; after the clock and at most `halt.max_days` from the time of the decision that sets it — `opened_at` on open, the extension's decision time on extend — a policy row with default 30 and `set_with_counsel`), `published_at`, `ratify_by` and `ratification_state` (`n/a` only where the approval is already a two-person `4E` decision, that is distress and legal hold, or where the ratification belongs to A28's `token.pause` row; otherwise `pending`, `ratified` or `lapsed`, mirrored from A14), `ratifying_board` (for `offering.halt`, the body the `board_ratifier` row names), `extensions` (count), `chain_op_id` and `chain_state` (`not_required`, `requested`, `confirmed`, `clear_requested`, `cleared`, `failed`; the halt's view of the per-vehicle, reference-counted `halt_chain_state` below, so a halt that joins an already-halted chain is `confirmed` without an op of its own), `state` (`open`, `review_due`, `criteria_met`, `clearing`, `cleared`), `cleared_at`, `cleared_by`, `cleared_reason`, `cleared_by_run_id` (recon cause), `source_ref` (the source module's id, unique per vehicle and cause), plus the standard RLS and audit columns from A04.
- Refusals, enforced in the API and asserted by test whatever the caller: empty `reopening_criteria` is 422 `HALT_CRITERIA_REQUIRED`; no `ends_at` is `HALT_END_DATE_REQUIRED`; `ends_at` in the past is `HALT_END_DATE_PAST`; `ends_at` more than `halt.max_days` after the decision time is `HALT_END_DATE_TOO_FAR` (a longer halt needs `6E`, CCO → CEO + LEGL, action `halt.open_long`, and still carries an end date); a second open halt with the same vehicle and cause is 409 `HALT_ALREADY_OPEN` (extend the existing one). System causes are no exception: when the forwarder or a source event opens one, this module stamps the source's criterion (or the seeded one for that cause) and a default end date (`halt.system_default_days`, default 14), so "no halt without criteria and an end date" holds for every row in the table.
- Who opens what, each under an A14 row from reconciliation 3 and never a second code for the same control: the J21 step 2 **time-boxed halt** on ADM-34 (one vehicle, or `level = platform` for the R1/R6 distribution hold) is `offering.halt` (`SGL+R`: CCO acts with a hardware-key assertion; the board delegate, never the actor, ratifies within 24 h through A14's `ratify`, and the body the `board_ratifier` row names is stored in `ratifying_board`); ADM-21's **"Halt instrument" / "Halt all"** (P2, A91) is `resale.halt` (`SGL+R`: MKTO acts; CCO ratifies within 2 h). Both are cause `manual`. A **meeting** halt (A93, criterion "the vote closes and the result is certified"), a **valuation-stale** halt (A34 when G20 fails for the vehicle; "a new valuation is published and approved; G20 passes") and a **recon-break** halt (A20, reconciliation 1) arrive through the forwarder and are opened as A14 system-triggered `resale.halt` acts: `SVCA` is the actor, the `act` row carries `trigger_ref` = the source event (the break, meeting or valuation id), and a human CCO must ratify within 2 h exactly as for a human act (A14's system-trigger rule); the halt's own scope, not the action code's name, decides what G10 stops. A **token-pause** halt mirrors A28's confirmed `pause` under that `token.pause` approval id (itself `SGL+R` with its own 2 h ratification in A28), criterion "unpause confirmed on chain; SM-06 `active`". **Distress** and **legal-hold** halts arrive from A34's effectors through the forwarder under A34's `M22.distress` or `M22.courtOrder` approval id, already a two-person `4E` decision, so they carry `ratification_state = n/a` (§3). The **kill-switch** halt is §2's, under the engage approval id. No row is ever written without an approval id (`G13_APPROVAL_MISMATCH` otherwise). The effector writes the row, publishes it (below) and sets the G10 read at once; the one ratification clock is A14's (reconciliation 2).
- On-chain mirror, reference-counted. A24's `HaltModule` holds one `halted` flag and one cause per token (`halt(compliance, cause)` / `lift(compliance)`), while this module allows several open halts per vehicle, one per cause. So the chain is driven per vehicle, not per halt: a `halt_chain_state` row per vehicle (`vehicle_id`, `contributors[]` = the ids of every open resale- or all-scope halt on the vehicle plus the platform halt while it is open, `chain_cause`, `chain_state` `open | set_requested | halted | clear_requested`, `chain_op_id`). A halt whose scope includes `resale_board` or `all` on a vehicle whose token is `active` or `paused` joins the contributors; `halt_set` (`HaltModule.setHalt`, V3 restrict-only, "requested only by the halts module under its own rows" in A28) is requested only when the contributor count goes from 0 to 1, under that halt's approval id. The cause sent uses the A24 vocabulary (`MANUAL`, `MEETING`, `VALUATION_STALE`, `RECON_BREAK`, `OTHER` for distress, legal hold and kill switch); when several contributors arrive in the same tick the fixed priority `RECON_BREAK` > `OTHER` > `MANUAL` > `MEETING` > `VALUATION_STALE` picks the cause. The module never re-sends `halt_set` while the chain is halted (the chain cause is informational; the record's cause list is authoritative, and G10 and every screen read the record). `token_pause` halts are not contributors because the token is already paused; the vehicle's other resale-scope halts are, so a later unpause (for example before the wind-down burn) never reopens transfers. The platform halt (marketplace suspend, `vehicle_id` null) fans out: it joins the contributors of every vehicle whose token is `active` or `paused`, so every such token gets a `halt_set` with cause `OTHER` under the engage approval id unless it is already halted, because holders' non-custodial wallets could otherwise transfer on chain past an off-chain G10. The off-chain G10 read takes effect at once and does not wait for the chain; a `halt_set` that fails is Sev1 `recon.break` with sub-code `rule_drift` through A13 and is retried by `halts.chain_sync`. Clearing is the other way round and also counted: a clearing halt leaves the contributors; if others remain, the halt goes straight to `cleared` and no chain op is sent (the chain stays halted); if it was the last one, the halt goes to `clearing`, A28 runs `halt_clear` (V2 `lift`), and the halt is `cleared` (and G10 stops reporting it) only when A28 reports the lift confirmed, so the chain is never more open than the record. A halt that opens while a `halt_clear` is in flight re-requests `halt_set` as soon as the lift confirms. Mints and burns pass the HaltModule (A24), so a halted token can still be burned at wind-down.
- Ratification lapse (A14's sweep at `ratify_by`): the halt stays in place. The breach of A14's one clock raises Sev2 `approval.unratified` (owner CCO, target ADM-34) through A13, and A13's seeded ladder upgrades it in place to Sev1 with CEO as owner (at 4 h for the 2 h policies, at 48 h for `ratification.board`); this node flags the row `ratification_state = lapsed` so ADM-34 and ADM-01 show it red. A system-triggered `resale.halt` lapses and escalates the same way. In every phase a kill-switch engage also gets a second clock, `ratification.board` (24 h, `set_with_counsel`; J21 step 2 "Board ratifies within 24 h"; N06), met by `kill_switch.board_ratify` (`4E`, LEGL or TP-RE → CCO, the board resolution's document hash from A35 mandatory); for `offering.halt` the board delegate's A14 ratification is itself the board's, so no second clock opens. The phase and scope decide which board ratifies, not whether the clock exists: a `board_ratifier` policy row (`set_with_counsel`) seeds P1 vehicle halt = the trustee's board (TP-RE) where the trustee is a partner principal, otherwise the RealX board; P1 kill switch or platform halt = the RealX board; P2 = the RE's board; the body is stored in `ratifying_board`. Nothing is ever lifted because a ratification was missed: lifting is restorative and always needs the clearing decision below.
- End date and criteria: `halts.end_date_sweep` (every minute, idempotent) moves a halt that is still `open` after `ends_at` to `review_due`, emits Sev2 `halt.review_due` once per halt (dedup key `halt:<id>:review_due`), and notifies holders through A43 that the halt has reached its published end date and is under review. `halts.criteria_check` (every 5 minutes and on every source event) evaluates each `system:` criterion; when all criteria of a **system-cause** halt pass, it clears by system (`cleared_by = system:<module>`) only if the source module has also sent its clear event (the source is the authority); when all `system:` criteria of a **manual** halt pass, the row moves to `criteria_met` and CCO gets a Sev3 task, but a human still clears it. A halt is never cleared by the passing of `ends_at`, and it cannot drift silently either (the Assetora failure, a freeze that ran for 13 months): `review_due` is an owned alert and every extension is a recorded decision.
- Extend: `halt.extend` (`4E`, CCO → CEO) requires a new `ends_at` (after the clock and at most `halt.max_days` after the extension's decision time, so a halt can be extended past day 30 of its life one recorded decision at a time), restated criteria (may be identical, but re-entered), a reason; it increments `extensions`, re-publishes and re-notifies holders. The third and every later extension of one halt needs `6E` (CCO → CEO + LEGL). Clear: `halt.clear` (`4E`, MKTO maker → CCO checker, as ADM-21 "Resume" and J07 give it; a CCO-drafted clear with CEO as checker is an added path for P1, when the resale desk may not be staffed, and is listed as founder-visible in `app/evidence/A48.json`) for `manual` only; a system cause is 409 `HALT_SYSTEM_CAUSE` (its clear comes from the source module); a clear while any `system:` criterion fails is refused with `HALT_CRITERIA_NOT_MET` naming the criterion, unless the request is `halt.clear_override` (`6E`, CCO → CEO + LEGL, reason mandatory), audited as an override. Every `human` criterion is ticked by the clearing checker with a sentence stored on the row.
- Publishing: `published_at` is set on open, extend and clear. The holder-facing text (cause in plain words, scope, criteria, expected end) is generated from the row by `haltNotice(halt)` in this module (no free-text copy in screens), delivered through A43 to every holder of the vehicle with notice type `halt.opened | halt.extended | halt.review_due | halt.cleared`, and readable by any signed-in holder of the vehicle at `GET /v1/halts/vehicles/{id}/state`; the sponsor's SP-OWN and SP-IR read the same endpoint under RLS. Kill-switch and platform halts publish to every holder and every tenant.
- G10 read: `haltState(vehicleId) -> {halted, halts: [{halt_id, cause, scope, criteria, ends_at, state, since}], platform: {suspended, since}}` in the public interface and `GET /v1/halts/vehicles/{id}/state` for the SDK. A19's G10 result for a vehicle is the union of this source and A20's; a vehicle with an A20 `recon_break` halt but no mirrored row here (outbox lag) is still `halted`, and `halts.mirror_check` closes the gap within one tick and raises Sev3 `queue.sla_breach` if the gap is older than 5 minutes.
- Batch: `POST /v1/halts/batch` (the ADM-21 "Halt all" on the resale board, built by A91) takes a vehicle list with shared criteria and end date and opens one `resale.halt` request per vehicle (A14's rule of exactly one `object_ref` per request holds); whether one hardware-key assertion may cover the canonical list of payload hashes is an A14 contract question confirmed at Specify, and if it may not, the endpoint asks for one assertion per vehicle.

Releasing `platform.inflow_freeze` lets A17 re-offer the held unapplied lines to its matcher. The person who engaged the switch, and any agent of that person, cannot be the maker or the checker of its release (`KS_ACTOR_CANNOT_RELEASE`); a release while the engage is still `pending` ratification is allowed, because a second person correcting a false alarm is the same protection.

The `agents.traffic` release is out of reach of every token: while the switch is off A12's gateway refuses every token, MCP and session-key call with `423 AGENTS_PAUSED` (`tier: platform`) before any module runs, this module's endpoints included (there is no self-exemption), and A21's `setFlag` refuses 403 `self_subject` as the backstop if an approval whose maker principal is a token ever reaches it (A21 case d).

**4. Wind-down pipeline: waterfall, burn, register close, retention (J21 steps 4-7)**

- `wind_downs` row per vehicle from `exit_approved`: `steps[]` in a fixed order with state and evidence: `halt_published` (§1), `holders_notified` (A43 delivery record), `trustee_control` (J21 step 4: the replacement trustee or RE recorded as an A33 `Entity` with its appointment document; `winddown.trustee_control`, TP-RE → CCO, `4E`), `sale_recorded` (A34's `sold`), `waterfall_closed`, `burn_closed`, `register_closed`, `retention_flagged`, `final_report_filed`. `GET /v1/vehicles/{id}/wind-down` returns the checklist with each step's state, approval ids, blocking reason and the next allowed action; a step attempted out of order is 409 `WD_STEP_ORDER` naming the step that must finish first.
- Waterfall hand-off (J21 step 5): `POST /v1/vehicles/{id}/wind-down/waterfall` calls A40's `POST /v1/distributions/exit-waterfall/preview` with exactly the inputs of A40's `computeExitWaterfall`: `{vehicleId, gross_proceeds_cents, secured_debt_cents, costs_cents[] (itemised: agent, legal, wind-up; integer cents from the settlement statement), fees_cents[] (sponsor performance fee, platform fee; from the fee schedule), settlement_date, record_date (default the settlement date)}` (A40 resolves the snapshot and the tax provider itself), then creates a run with `kind = exit` through A40's `index.ts`. A40 computes debt, costs, fees, withholding and per-holder lines under its own oracle and its `distribution.exit.approve` row (FINA → CFO + LEGL, `6E`, plus the TP-RE countersign where the trustee is a partner principal) and reports `closed` (paid = approved) through its outbox; this module never computes a line, never rounds and never writes a ledger entry. The step is `waterfall_closed` only on A40's `closed` event; `partially_failed` or `manual_payout` keep it open with A40's exception codes shown. If A40's preview returns `shortfall_cents > 0` (A40 then refuses the run with `DST_WATERFALL_SHORTFALL`), the step can close only through `winddown.waterfall_shortfall_accept` (`6E`, CFO → CEO + LEGL, trustee countersign, the preview hash and shortfall in the payload), which records zero holder lines; until then the step shows `WD_SHORTFALL_UNACCEPTED`. - Burn (J21 step 6): `POST /v1/vehicles/{id}/wind-down/burn` is refused with `WD_WATERFALL_OPEN` until `waterfall_closed`, with `WD_LINES_UNSETTLED` naming the line while any holder's final line is not paid in A40, and with `WD_RESOLUTION_REQUIRED` unless it carries the trustee's resolution to burn and close as an A35 document hash (required by default; `set_with_counsel`). If the token is `paused` (R1, an engage with `pause_tokens`, or a sanctions pause), the step shows `WD_TOKEN_PAUSED` and this node opens A28's `unpause` (`6E+Q`, CCO → SECO + CFO) pre-filled for humans to decide; the vehicle's resale-scope halts keep the HaltModule set (§1), so the unpause does not reopen transfers. Before any approval exists this node checks the breakdown against A18's cap table and the chain's `totalSupply` and refuses with `WD_BURN_SUM_MISMATCH` if the per-holder units do not sum to both; A28 enforces `units = totalSupply` again (`TOP_BURN_NOT_FULL_SUPPLY`), and an ordinary `burn` that would take supply to zero is refused by A28 with the same code, so this node never sends one. The op runs under A28's `burn_final` row: TOKO maker → CFO checker, `4E+Q`, plus the owner quorum (3 of the 5 V1 key holders, distinct from the maker) as an approval-only quorum, signed and executed on V2. A28 drives SM-06 `redeeming → retired` at zero supply. The step is `burn_closed` only when the op is `closed` (confirmed on chain, every redeem instruction acknowledged by A18, A20's targeted reconciliation clean with `totalSupply = 0` and register units 0); a registry rejection of any holder's instruction leaves the op `broken` (A28) and the step open.
- Register close: `POST /v1/vehicles/{id}/wind-down/close-register` (`winddown.close_register`, REGO → CCO, `4E`) is refused with `WD_BURN_NOT_CLOSED` until the burn step closes; it asks A18 for a certified final `RegisterVersion` (zero units, every holder's closing lot history intact) through A18's certification request (the trustee's decision is an A14 decision there) and records that version id as the closing version. After `wound_down`, A36 refuses any offering for the vehicle (A34's state), A40 refuses any run, and A28 refuses every operation except reads (SM-06 `retired`). A hard "register closed" refusal inside A18 does not exist today; any `adjust` would still need A18's `6E` with the trustee and would raise an A20 break against a retired token. Whether A18 should add one is recorded as an open question for A18's owner, not built here.
- Retention flag (J21 step 7, N01): this node owns `trust_retention` rows (`vehicle_id`, `retention_until`, `placed_at`, `anchor_event_id`). On `exit_approved` it sets `retention_until = max(existing, clock + retention.years)` (`set_with_counsel`, default 7) and emits `retention.hold_placed` into A10's chain; on `wound_down` it restamps to `wound_down_at + 7 years`.

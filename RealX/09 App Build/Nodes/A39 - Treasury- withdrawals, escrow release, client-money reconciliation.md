# A39 - Treasury: withdrawals, escrow release, client-money reconciliation

**The treasury desk of the marketplace as one API module: an investor's cash leaves the client-money trust account only to the nominated-account version that was snapshotted when the withdrawal was requested, in that investor's verified name, after a fresh sanctions re-screen and an AML check through the registered G14 evaluator, under A14's `withdrawal.release` rows (`AUTO` up to the allow-listed threshold, `4E` System threshold → TRSY then CFO above it, so two different people approve every non-`AUTO` withdrawal, `6E` System threshold → CFO + CCO or CEO at A$50,000.00 and above; every threshold is an A14 policy row changed only by `policy.change`); a joint "both to sign" account needs both holders and an entity account needs a verified signatory; an agent's withdrawal inside its mandate's band proceeds; one above the per-action cap waits for the human's passkey (`428 CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED`, G22), and one past the daily or monthly cap is refused (`403 G22_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`), as the A12 §5 cap table fixes; an AML restriction holds every payout and shows support nothing but "under review"; escrow is released by this node only (FINA maker, `6E` CFO + CCO, one effector, one A16 `escrow.release` journal) after A38's `offering.finalised` fact for the current allocation version and for exactly the allocated subtotal, and paid once to the vehicle's approved, KYB-verified trustee settlement account and never while R10 is not clean; a trustee return for an exclusion after release is tracked as a receivable; refunds go back to the bank only when the offering's `return_to_bank` flag is on; unmatched funds go back to the payer and money moves between trust accounts or to operating (fee sweep) only with an approval plus two human bank authorisers; the daily R10 status gates every payout this node makes; a payout-funding-short alert looks seven days ahead; and the fee schedule and float-interest policy are published as data for the public fees page.**

**Oracle rule:** The node QA writes hidden oracle tests from this spec to `<workspace>\gauntlet\hidden\A39\` before the build starts ([[App Gauntlet]] §2). The builder never reads that folder; the cases are published into the repo only after the gate passes. The oracle scope is the five money invariants of this node:

1. **Withdrawal payouts.** No withdrawal payout (investor, agent or refund-to-bank origin) is ever submitted to A17 unless all of these hold at submit: the destination is the nominated-account version recorded in the row's destination fingerprint and that version is still the investor's active version; the account's operating instruction was met (both holders' assertions over the same hash for a "both to sign" joint account, a verified signatory for an entity); the G02 `ScreeningResult` is no older than `treasury.checks.max_age_minutes`; G14 was evaluated by the registered A42 evaluator and was clear; and the A14 approval has `object_ref = withdrawal:<id>`, `payload_sha256` equal to the row's canonical hash, the `policy_code` of the `withdrawal.release` row that the amount and the same-day sum resolve to, and, for every non-`AUTO` row, decisions by at least two distinct humans (a `4E` withdrawal approved by the CFO alone is never paid).
2. **Escrow release and bank settlement.** No A14 `escrow.release` request is created, no A16 `escrow.release` journal is posted and no `escrow_settlement` payout is submitted for an offering unless A38's `offering.finalised` fact has been received for the offering's current allocation version and the amount equals that version's `allocated_subtotal_cents` (fees excluded). Each offering has at most one release and at most one bank settlement. The settlement is for exactly the approved amount, under the approved `6E` id, and goes to the approved, KYB-verified trustee settlement-account version snapshotted in the approval payload. It never happens while R10, `assertClear(vehicleId, 'escrow_release')` or `assertClear(vehicleId, 'payout')` is not clean.
3. **Agent band.** A mandated agent's withdrawal request inside the mandate's per-action and daily caps, with a valid mandate signature, is accepted and its counter reserved under the row lock; above the per-action cap, or without a signing key, the call returns `428 CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED` with a `confirmation_id`, and past the daily or monthly cap it is refused `403 G22_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (A12 §5 cap table); either way it writes no withdrawal row, no ledger reservation and no counter; a request confirmed by the human's passkey re-executes through exactly the same policy and gates; a request that fails downstream releases its counter in the same transaction.
4. **Precondition.** `assertBankLedgerClear()` returns clean only when the latest platform R10 run covers every client-money account with no open `cash` or `source_unavailable` break on it, and that run is within the freshness window.
5. **Fee sweep boundary.** A fee sweep never moves more than `fee_payable_realx` less the fees of offerings whose A38 run is not yet `settled` or `refunded`. It goes only to `realx-operating`, under its own approval and two distinct human bank authorisers, and never on a token's authority.

Money in this node is sandbox money posted through A16's ledger and A17's sandbox bank; no ADI, no NPP participant and no real trust account is touched. Every approval threshold and role set is an A14 `approval_policies` row changed only through A14's `policy.change`; every TTL and window is a configuration key. This note is not legal advice: the client-money and float-interest treatment, the wholesale-track trustee settlement mechanics, whether refunds return to the bank, and any threshold-transaction duty are counsel and MLRO questions recorded under Human gates, and both possible answers are built behind flags.

## Build brief

This node is API plus worker jobs (module `treasury` in the Fastify monolith; jobs in `services/worker/src/jobs/treasury/`). It owns no screen: POR-05's Withdraw card and withdrawal rows are rendered by A59, ADM-18's queues by A74, the APR-2286 inbox row by A66 through A14, and PUB-06's fee and float-interest text by A54. It never posts a ledger journal outside A16's catalogue, never calls the bank outside A17's payout primitive, and never decides an approval outside A14. **A39 is the single owner of escrow release.** It owns the `POST /v1/treasury/escrow/{offeringId}/release` route, the A14 `escrow.release` request (`6E`, FINA → CFO + CCO), the one registered `escrow.release` effector, the A16 `escrow.release` journal and the A17 `escrow_settlement` payout to the trustee. A38 only emits the `offering.finalised` fact and waits for this node's `treasury.escrow.released`, as A38 F4, A37, A16's catalogue and A79 J04 step 9 already say. A37 exports no `postEscrowRelease`. The Drizzle schema and migration live in the module folder, as A20 does, and are registered through A04's per-module migration hook; nothing is written under `packages/db/migrations`.

**Withdrawals (`withdrawals/`)**

- **Data.** `Withdrawal`: `id` (`WDR-<yyyymmdd>-<6 base32>`), `tenant_id` (platform), `investor_id`, `origin` (`investor` | `agent` | `refund_return_to_bank`), `amount_cents` (integer, A02 `Cents`), `nominated_account_id`, `nominated_account_version` (the A17 `NominatedAccount` version resolved at prepare time), `destination_fingerprint`, `destination_masked` (`CBA ••4471`), `destination_allowlisted` (boolean, computed at prepare time, see below), `canonical_hash`, `reservation_id` (A16 `withdrawal` reservation), `policy_code` (`AUTO` | `4E` | `6E`, A14's code of the matched `withdrawal.release` row), `tier_label` (A14's display label: `AUTO`, `4E` or `4E_SECOND_APPROVAL`; a label only, never stored as a policy code), `approval_id`, `checks` (JSON: `bank_in_name`, `g02_rescreen`, `g14_aml`, each `pending | pass | fail | held` with a result reference and `evaluated_at`), `hold_reason` (see the state table), `blocked_reason` (`recon_not_clear` | `halted`, on `approved` rows only), `payout_id` (A17), `ledger_journal_id`, `requested_by_principal`, `signing_rule` (`single` | `either` | `both` | `entity_signatory`, snapshotted from A12's `SignatoryPort` at request), `assertions` (JSON: each `{ principal_id, assertion_id | mandate_signature_ref, asserted_at }` over `canonical_hash`), `on_behalf_of_id`, `mandate_id`, `source_event_id` (refund origin only, unique), `idempotency_key` (copied from A09's `Idempotency-Key` header, never from the body), `state`, timestamps. Every row is tenant-scoped through A04's RLS.
- **Destination fingerprint and canonical hash.** `destination_fingerprint = sha256(JCS{ bsb, account_number, account_name_normalised, nominated_account_id, version })` over the decrypted A17 row, computed inside the service and never returned. The canonical request payload is `{ v: 1, withdrawal_id, investor_id, amount_cents, currency: 'AUD', nominated_account_id, nominated_account_version, destination_fingerprint, origin, prepared_at }`, hashed with A11's `canonicalPayloadSha256` (RFC 8785 JCS, SHA-256). The investor's passkey step-up, the A12 mandate signature (through `body_sha256` of a body that carries the `prepare_id`), and the A14 approval (`payload_sha256`, including the `AUTO` exact match) all bind to this one hash.
- **Allow-listed destination.** `destination_allowlisted` is true only when the snapshotted nominated-account version is `active`, its A17 name check (`name_check_attempt_id`) matched a verified name of the investor, and it was created at least `treasury.destination.cooling_hours` (default 48) before `prepared_at`. It is the value A39 passes to A16's `resolveWithdrawalPolicy` and the fact A14's `destination_allowlisted` resolver reads; a newly changed account therefore never qualifies for `AUTO`.
- **State machine `SM-WDR`** (this node's own, registered with A03's runner as exactly this table, because the product map has no withdrawal machine; QA lesson BQA-16). Terminal states: `settled`, `failed`, `returned`, `cancelled`, `rejected`, `expired`. Every pair not in the table returns `409 WDR_INVALID_TRANSITION`.

  Hold reasons: `destination_name_mismatch`, `destination_changed`, `destination_missing` (refund origin), `aml_restricted`, `aml_alert_open`, `aml_check_unavailable`, `screening_hit`, `screening_unavailable`. An `approved` row whose release is blocked by R10 or a G10 halt stays `approved` with `blocked_reason` and is retried each cycle; if its approval lapses meanwhile it becomes `expired`.
- **Reservation TTL.** The reservation must outlive the approval. A39 declares `treasury.release.window_hours` (default 2) and validates at boot that A16's `ledger.withdrawal.reservation_ttl_hours` ≥ `treasury.withdrawal.cosign_window_hours` + A14's `withdrawal.release` TTL (72 h) + `treasury.release.window_hours`; otherwise the API refuses to start with `TRSY_CONFIG_RESERVATION_TTL_TOO_SHORT`. An oracle case lands the approval at 71 h 59 m and the payout must still go out.
- **Prepare** `POST /v1/me/withdrawals/prepare { amount_cents }` (scope `wallet:withdraw`, header `Idempotency-Key`). Resolves the investor's single active nominated account from A17's `index.ts` (no destination parameter exists on any route, so no principal, agent or staff member can point a withdrawal elsewhere; an extra `destination` field is a `422` schema error), computes the fingerprint, `destination_allowlisted`, the canonical payload and its hash, and returns `{ prepare_id, payload_sha256, destination_masked, policy_preview, expires_at (5 minutes) }`. Nothing is reserved.
- **Request** `POST /v1/me/withdrawals { prepare_id, amount_cents }` (scope `wallet:withdraw`, header `Idempotency-Key`). In one database transaction: (1) re-read the active nominated version; if it differs from the prepare snapshot, `409 WDR_DESTINATION_CHANGED` and nothing is written. (2) Confirmation: a session principal presents a fresh A11 assertion (`verifyAndConsumeFreshAssertion`, purpose `withdrawal_request`, over `payload_sha256`); a token principal presents the A12 `RealX-Mandate-Signature` over a body that carries `prepare_id`. (2a) Operating instruction, read through A12's `SignatoryPort` (the real implementation is A30's; both are ancestors): `signingRule(accountId) = either` or a single holder → one assertion suffices; `both` → the row is written `awaiting_cosign` (see the table) unless the request is a token principal's under a mandate whose signing key both signatories registered (A12 §4), which counts as both; an entity account (SMSF, company, trust) → the asserting principal, or the human who owns the agent's token, must satisfy `isVerifiedSignatory(principalId, entityId)`, else `403 WDR_SIGNATORY_REQUIRED` and nothing is written. The rule is snapshotted in `signing_rule`. (3) A12's G22 step reserves the mandate counter (below). (4) A16 `reserve(investor_cash, amount, 'withdrawal', WDR id)`; insufficient available → `409 LDG_INSUFFICIENT_AVAILABLE` passed through and no row written; a frozen account → the row is written as `held: aml_restricted` (the investor sees `under_review`, never the reason). (5) A16 `resolveWithdrawalPolicy(investor_id, amount_cents, destination_allowlisted, now)` sets `policy_code` and `tier_label`. (6) Audit `withdrawal.requested` with `on_behalf_of_id` when the actor is an agent; outbox `treasury.withdrawal.requested` (and `treasury.withdrawal.cosign_requested { withdrawal_id, investor_id, amount_cents }` for an `awaiting_cosign` row, so the second holder is told). The worker also emits `treasury.withdrawal.held` on every entry to `held` and `treasury.withdrawal.settled` on `settled`, with exactly the fields A43's consumer contract reads (`withdrawal_id`, `investor_id`, `amount_cents`; no reason field).
- **Agent path (G22), aligned with A12 §4.** A12's `agentAuth` evaluates the route's `x-realx-scope` and passes `amount_cents`, `vehicle_id = null` and the action cell to `evaluateG22`. Inside the mandate's `per_action_cap_cents` and `daily_cap_cents`, with a valid mandate signature, the request proceeds and the day's counter is reserved under the `agent_counter` lock in the business transaction. The outcome of a failed cap is A12 §5's cap table, verbatim: above `per_action_cap_cents`, or when no signing key is registered, the call returns `428 CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED` with a `confirmation_id` (naming `cap: per_action` and the remaining cents); past `daily_cap_cents` or `monthly_cap_cents` it is refused `403 G22_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` naming the cap and the remaining cents, a hard refusal that no confirmation lifts (it wins when the per-action cap fails too). Either way no withdrawal row, no ledger reservation and no counter are written. When the human confirms through A12's `POST /v1/agent-access/confirmations/{id}/confirm` with a passkey, the stored request re-executes through this same route logic, the same policy resolution and the same gates (a A$30,000 confirmed request goes to `4E` like any other). A confirmed request counts toward the day's and month's totals, and the daily and monthly caps are checked again when it re-executes. A39 adds no refusal of its own on caps. A mandate can lower what an agent may ask for, never raise what the policy allows. Every accepted agent withdrawal emits `agent.action.money` for A101's notice to the person. Whether a withdrawal under a standing mandate is a personal legal declaration is a counsel question (Human gates); if counsel says yes, the signing-key path is disabled by config and every agent withdrawal takes the 428 path.
- **Checks (`checking`), run by the worker within seconds of the request.** In order, each stored as a `GateEvaluation` through A19 so any decision replays:
  - `bank_in_name`: the snapshotted nominated version's captured account name equals one of the investor's verified names under A17's normalisation table (exact after normalisation, no fuzzy score); failure → `held: destination_name_mismatch`, shown to the investor plainly (not an AML matter).
  - `g02_rescreen`: a fresh A22 call now, never merely a result within 24 h: `screenPerson({ name, dob, nationality, aliases })` for an individual and for each joint holder, `screenEntity({ name, abn, acn, country })` for an entity. The `ScreeningResult` (`screeningRef`, `listVersion`, `outcome`, `hits`, `screenedAt`, `signature`, verified with A22's `verifyResultSignature`) is evaluated through A19 `evaluateAction({ kind: 'withdrawal', ... })` (A42's G02 evaluator for the `withdrawal` kind), and its `screeningRef` is the `screening_result_ref` that A17's `createPayout` requires. `review` → `held: screening_hit` with no investor-facing detail; adapter unavailable → `held: screening_unavailable`, retried by the worker, never bypassed.
  - `g14_aml`: evaluated through A19 `evaluateAction({ kind: 'withdrawal' })`, whose G14 evaluator is A42's (`restrictionStatus(investorId)` → `none | payouts_held | full`, plus A42's open-alert fact `GET /v1/aml/alerts?subject=<investor>&state=open`). `payouts_held` or `full` → `held: aml_restricted`; an open alert → `held: aml_alert_open` for the MLRO; A17's `rapid_in_out` hint is written for any withdrawal within 24 h of a deposit of the same order of magnitude. **Fail closed:** outside `RUN_MODE=test`, if A19's registry reports that the G14 evaluator for the `withdrawal` kind is not A42's (owner ≠ `A42`, or the baseline fixture provider is active), the row is held `aml_check_unavailable`; it never passes by default.
  - All three pass → `AUTO` rows go to `approved` through A14's service path (`auto_condition = destination_allowlisted_and_amount_lte`, exact match on `canonical_hash`; the `AUTO` approval id is real and audited like any other); `4E` and `6E` rows create the A14 request (see Approval rows) with the three checks attached exactly as the design's APR-2286 row shows them: "Bank account in investor name", "Sanctions re-screen (G02) Clear", "AML pattern check (G14) No alerts".
- **Approval rows (`withdrawal.release`, A14 `approval_policies` data).** The thresholds live only in these A14 rows. Only an approved A14 `policy.change` (`6E`, 24 h timelock) changes them, as A16's `policy.ts` section says. No threshold is a configuration key in A16 or here, and this node holds no copy or literal of them. A16's `resolveWithdrawalPolicy` reads the rows, and A16's wallet contract serves `withdrawal_policy.second_approval_cents` and `auto_max_cents` from the same rows.

| `applies_when` | `policy_code` | Maker → `checker_role_sets` | `tier_label` | Risk shown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `destination_allowlisted` and `amount_cents lte` the AUTO cap (seed A$25,000.00) and same-day sum `lte` the cap | `AUTO` | System threshold → `SVCA` (no human) | `AUTO` | Low |
| not the `AUTO` predicate and `amount_cents lt` the second-approval amount (seed A$50,000.00) | `4E` | System threshold → `[[TRSY], [CFO]]` (two human decisions, in that order) | `4E` | Medium |
| `amount_cents gte` the second-approval amount | `6E` | System threshold → `{CFO, CCO}` or `{CFO, CEO}` | `4E_SECOND_APPROVAL` | Medium |

  **One maker flow, two humans on every non-`AUTO` row.** The worker creates every withdrawal request (every origin) with maker `System threshold` (the `SVCA` service principal), the label the design's APR-2286 row shows. There is no TRSY submit step and no `POST .../release` route, as A74 already expects. G13 says two different people approve every money movement above the threshold, so the service principal never counts as one of them. On the `4E` row, TRSY's approval in A14 is the product map's "TRSY releases" (ADM-18 "Release withdrawal: TRSY maker, CFO checker"; J15 "TRSY releases, CFO approves"; POR-05 "needs two approvals in Treasury"), and the CFO's approval is the check. A14's check constraint requires each checker to be a human outside the request's maker identity set and outside every other checker set's decider, so TRSY and CFO are two different people. A staff token can never decide either. The `6E` row already has two human checkers. TRSY may also hold, re-check or reject a row before `paying`. **This is an A14 row change.** A14's seeded `4E` row today reads "TRSY or System threshold → CFO", so it must become "System threshold → `[[TRSY], [CFO]]`". It is recorded in `app/evidence/A39.json` and under Human gates as a founder confirmation. Until A14 carries it, this node's policy test fails, and Build does not start. **Boundary:** the rule is inclusive (A$50,000.00 needs the second approval, A$25,000.00 is still `AUTO`-eligible, A$25,000.01 is `4E`). The investor-facing sentence is rendered by A50 and A59 from `withdrawal_policy.second_approval_cents` ("Withdrawals of A$50,000 or more need a second RealX approval"); this node serves no copy of its own. The design's "over A$50,000" wording is a recorded copy discrepancy for A50 and A59. Whether to make the rule exclusive instead is A14's founder question (one boundary in data).
- **Release (`approved → paying`).** The release worker (`treasury.withdrawal.release`, every 30 s) takes `approved` rows in order and, whatever the age of the checks: (a) compares the investor's current active nominated version and a freshly computed fingerprint with the row's snapshot; any difference → `held: destination_changed` (the approval is cancelled; the investor sees `needs_confirmation` and may either cancel or confirm the new destination with a fresh passkey through `POST /v1/me/withdrawals/{id}/confirm-destination`, which is `requiresHuman: true`, re-prepares the canonical hash and re-enters `checking` for a new approval); (b) re-runs any check older than `treasury.checks.max_age_minutes` (default 60); (c) asserts A20 `assertClear(null, 'payout')` (A20's platform-level recon guard for payouts), A19 G10 through `evaluateAction({ kind: 'withdrawal' })` (the union of halt sources, including A48's platform halts and kill switches) and this node's `assertBankLedgerClear()`; (d) posts A16 `withdrawal.pay` (consumes the reservation; refused by the ledger with `LDG_APPROVAL_MISMATCH` if the approval's object or hash does not match the row) and enqueues A17 `createPayout({ purpose: 'withdrawal', nominated_account_version, approval_id, screening_result_ref, idempotencyKey: WDR id })` in the same transaction through the outbox, so a crash after the journal never double-submits. The payout goes only to the snapshotted version; A17 independently refuses a superseded version with `PAYOUT_DESTINATION_SUPERSEDED`. A hold placed between approval and release (A42 freeze) makes the ledger refuse with `LDG_ACCOUNT_FROZEN`; the row goes to `held: aml_restricted`.
- **Staff actions** under `/v1/treasury/withdrawals`: `GET` (queue: id, investor masked, amount, destination masked, policy code, tier label, checks, age, state, `allowed_actions[]`; ordered by state then age), `GET /{id}` (checks with result references, approval, payout, journal, audit ids), `POST /{id}/hold {reason_code}` (TRSY, before `paying`), `POST /{id}/re-check` (TRSY, from `held`), `POST /{id}/reject {reason_code}` (TRSY human only, from `held`; releases the reservation; the investor sees `rejected` with a plain reason unless the reason is an AML code, in which case `under_review` persists until A42 says otherwise). Support (`SUPP`) reads the queue with amounts and states only; any AML hold reason is rendered `under_review` for SUPP, TP-OBS and every investor-facing surface (tipping-off ACL from A42).
- **Investor read** `GET /v1/me/withdrawals` (scope `wallet:read`) → rows for the POR-05 DataTable and POR-04 activity: `{ id, date, amount_cents, destination_masked, status, expected_by, formatted }` with `status` mapped from every state, explicitly (a generated test covers every state and hold reason): `requested` → `requested`; `awaiting_cosign` → `awaiting_cosign`; `checking` → `processing`; `awaiting_approval` → `processing`; `approved` (blocked or not) → `processing`; `paying` → `processing` (the bank has not confirmed; a `paying` row can still fail or be returned); `held: screening_unavailable` or `held: aml_check_unavailable` → `processing`; `held: aml_restricted`, `held: aml_alert_open` or `held: screening_hit` → `under_review`; `held: destination_name_mismatch` → `held_name_mismatch`; `held: destination_changed` or `held: destination_missing` → `needs_confirmation`; `settled` → `settled`; `failed` → `failed`; `returned` → `returned`; `cancelled` → `cancelled`; `rejected` → `rejected` (or `under_review` while the reject reason is an AML code); `expired` → `expired`. The wallet's `withdrawal_policy` amounts come from A16 (`second_approval_cents`, read from A14's rows); A50 and A59 render the sentence. `POST /v1/me/withdrawals/{id}/cancel` releases the reservation only while cancellable. `POST /v1/me/withdrawals/{id}/cosign` (scope `wallet:withdraw`, `requiresHuman: true`) is the second holder's passkey step for an `awaiting_cosign` row; the first holder presenting it again is `409 WDR_COSIGN_SAME_SIGNATORY`, a principal who is not a signatory of the account is `403 WDR_SIGNATORY_REQUIRED`, and an assertion over any other hash is `422 WDR_COSIGN_HASH_MISMATCH`. The design's toast copy "arrives in minutes" and the product map's "usually same day" are both served as `expected_by` from configuration (`treasury.withdrawal.expected_settlement`: `minutes` for NPP payouts, `same_day` when the sandbox reports a non-NPP destination).

**Escrow release, trustee settlement account, bank settlement, exclusion returns and refund-to-bank payouts (`escrow/`)**

- **Ownership.** A39 is the single owner of escrow release: the route, the `6E` request, the one effector, the A16 journal, the bank settlement and the `treasury.escrow.released` event. This matches A38 lines 36 and 93, A37 §"Not exported", A16's catalogue (`escrow.release`, caller A39), A74's Release escrow control and A79 J04 step 9. A14 must hold exactly one `escrow.release` effector, and it is this node's. A38 emits `offering.finalised` and waits for `treasury.escrow.released`; it never drafts, effects or posts a release. There is no `settlement.escrow_released` event anywhere.
- **Finalised facts (consumer of A38's `offering.finalised { offering_id, vehicle_id, allocation_id, allocation_version, allocation_sha256, allocated_subtotal_cents }`).** This event's payload is mirrored as a zod schema in `escrow/contracts.ts`, and a contract test pins it to A38's frozen spec. Each event is stored once in `offering_finalised_fact` (unique on the event id and on `offering_id + allocation_version`). The highest `allocation_version` is the current fact. A fact for a higher version cancels any pending `escrow.release` request for a lower version through A14 (`ESC_ALLOCATION_SUPERSEDED`), and a new request is needed. "Finalised" in this node means SM-04 `finalising` plus this event, as A38 F4 defines it. A39 does not call A37, which is not an ancestor: the release basis is this event plus A16's `offer_escrow` sub-balances for the offering.
- **Trustee settlement account (`vehicle_settlement_account`, owned here).** This is the only destination of an escrow settlement. It is separate from A33's `Vehicle.bank_account_ref`, which is the client-money escrow trust account reference and never a payout destination. The columns are `id` (`VSA-<yyyymmdd>-<6 base32>`), `vehicle_id`, `version`, `trustee_entity_id` (the vehicle's trustee entity in A33), `bsb_enc`, `account_number_enc`, `account_name`, `masked` (`••1234`), `bank_label`, `evidence_doc_id` (the trustee's bank confirmation letter, an A35 document), `name_check` (pass only when `account_name`, under A17's normalisation table, equals the A33 trustee entity's registered legal name exactly), `kyb_case_ref` (the approved A30 KYB case of that trustee entity, read through A33's admission record), `fingerprint` (`sha256(JCS{ bsb, account_number, account_name_normalised, vehicle_id, version })`, never returned), `state` (`proposed → awaiting_approval → active → superseded`, or `rejected` or `expired`), `approval_id`, `proposed_by`, `fresh_assertion_id` and timestamps.
  - `POST /v1/treasury/vehicles/{vehicleId}/settlement-account { bsb, account_number, account_name, evidence_doc_id }` may be called by SP-FIN of the vehicle's tenant or by FINA. It is `requiresHuman: true` and needs a fresh A11 assertion (purpose `settlement_account_change`), so no token of any kind may call it (`G22_SCOPE_NON_DELEGABLE`). The account name must match the trustee entity's name, else `422 VSA_NAME_MISMATCH`. The trustee entity must be KYB-approved, else `409 VSA_TRUSTEE_KYB_NOT_APPROVED`. The call writes `proposed`.
  - `POST .../settlement-account/{version}/submit` (FINA, human, fresh assertion) creates the A14 request `vehicle.settlement_account.change` (`4E`, FINA → CFO) over `{ vehicle_id, version, fingerprint, trustee_entity_id, kyb_case_ref, evidence_doc_id }`. When FINA proposed the account, the submit happens in the same call. The FINA who submits is the maker, and SP-FIN is never a checker.
  - On approval, this node's effector for that code makes the version `active` and supersedes the previous one. Every change, including the first account, follows this path. A39 registers A17's `VehicleSettlementProvider.settlementAccount(vehicleId)` from the active version, and no other node registers it. `GET /v1/treasury/vehicles/{vehicleId}/settlement-account` returns the masked account, name check, KYB reference, state and history. Staff readers and SP-OWN or SP-FIN of that tenant may read it.
- **Release request** `POST /v1/treasury/escrow/{offeringId}/release` (scope `M16.escrow.release`, FINA maker). A FINA staff token may draft it as maker only; SoD counts the agent as its human. The route creates the A14 `escrow.release` request (`6E`, FINA → CFO + CCO, `object_ref = offering:<id>`) over the canonical payload `{ v: 1, offering_id, vehicle_id, allocation_id, allocation_version, allocation_sha256, amount_cents, fee_cents, settlement_account_id, settlement_account_version, settlement_account_fingerprint }`, hashed with A11's `canonicalPayloadSha256`. Here `amount_cents = allocated_subtotal_cents` of the current fact, with fees excluded, and `fee_cents` = the offering's `offer_escrow` balance − `amount_cents`. The route and A14's precheck enforce the same five preconditions, each returned as a named check for A74's precheck panel:
  1. A current `offering.finalised` fact exists for this allocation version, else `409 ESC_NOT_FINALISED`. A release before finalise is always refused.
  2. The amount equals that fact's `allocated_subtotal_cents`, and `fee_cents ≥ 0`. Otherwise the code is `ESC_AMOUNT_MISMATCH` or `ESC_ESCROW_SHORT`.
  3. A20 `preflight` and `assertClear(vehicleId, 'escrow_release')` pass, else `409 ESC_RECON_NOT_CLEAR`.
  4. `assertBankLedgerClear()` passes, else `TRSY_BANK_LEDGER_NOT_CLEAR`.
  5. An `active` trustee settlement account exists with `name_check = pass` and an approved KYB case, else `409 ESC_SETTLEMENT_ACCOUNT_UNVERIFIED`.

  A second release for an offering that already has an approved or executed release is `409 ESC_ALREADY_RELEASED`. The request's `approval_id` is held on an `escrow_release` row (`id`, `offering_id`, `allocation_version`, `payload_sha256`, `approval_id`, `state` `awaiting_approval → approved → released`, or `blocked(reason)`, `cancelled`, `rejected` or `expired`), unique per offering among non-terminal rows.
- **The one effector (`escrow.release`, registered with A14; `execute`, `postcondition`, `revert = null`).** On `approval.approved`, the `treasury.escrow_release` job runs the effector. It first re-runs the five preconditions against the approved payload. A changed allocation version or settlement-account fingerprint cancels the approval (`ESC_ALLOCATION_SUPERSEDED`, `ESC_SETTLEMENT_ACCOUNT_CHANGED`), and a new request is needed. A recon failure leaves the row `blocked: recon_not_clear`, retried after each A20 run until the approval's TTL, and never overridden. Then, in one transaction, it:
  - posts A16 `escrow.release`: one `offer_escrow` debit leg per order line (each line's remaining sub-balance), `payout_clearing` = `amount_cents` and `fee_payable_realx` = `fee_cents`, with `approval_id`, `external_ref = offering id`, and A16 refusing any residual (`LDG_ESCROW_RESIDUAL`) or approval mismatch;
  - inserts the `escrow_settlement` row;
  - inserts the offering's `fee_lock` row (below);
  - writes the outbox event `treasury.escrow.released { offering_id, approval_id, amount_cents, journal_id }`, which is exactly the shape A38 F4 accepts.

  A redelivered approval event finds the journal by `external_ref` and posts nothing. The postcondition is that the offering's `offer_escrow` balance is zero and exactly one release journal exists.
- **Bank settlement (`escrow_settlement`: `id`, `offering_id`, `vehicle_id`, `amount_cents`, `approval_id`, `journal_id`, `settlement_account_version`, `destination_fingerprint`, `checks`, `payout_id`, `state` `pending → checking → held(reason) | paying → settled | failed | returned`).** There is at most one row per offering (unique). In order:
  1. Resolve the destination through `VehicleSettlementProvider`. Its active version must equal the approval payload's `settlement_account_version` and fingerprint, else `held: settlement_account_changed` with a Sev1 `treasury.escrow_destination_changed` to TRSY, CFO and FINA. The money stays in `payout_clearing` and is never paid to the new account on the old approval. Only an `escrow.settlement.redirect` request (`6E`, FINA → CFO + CCO) over `{ escrow_settlement_id, new version, new fingerprint }` releases it.
  2. Re-check the name against the A33 trustee entity's registered name, else `held: trustee_name_mismatch`.
  3. Run G02 on the trustee beneficiary: A22 `screenEntity({ name, abn, acn, country })`, evaluated through A19. `review` → `held: screening_hit`; unavailable → `held: screening_unavailable`.
  4. Run `assertBankLedgerClear()` and A20 `assertClear(vehicleId, 'payout')`. If either is not clean → `held: recon_not_clear`, retried after each A20 run and never overridden.
  5. Apply the cut blackout.
  6. Call A17 `createPayout({ purpose: 'escrow_settlement', approval_id, screening_result_ref, idempotencyKey: escrow_settlement id })` for exactly `amount_cents`. A17 posts `withdrawal.settle` (`payout_clearing` / `bank_mirror`) on `payout.settled`.
- **Exclusion return receivable (consumer of A38's `settlement.exclusion_return_requested { offering_id, vehicle_id, order_id, amount_cents, approval_id }`).** Each event is consumed once (unique on the event id and on `offering_id + order_id`). It opens a `trustee_receivable` with these fields: `TRC-` id, the event's fields, `reference = <allocation_id>:<order_id>` taken from the current fact, `state` `open → received` (or `cancelled` by a superseding A38 event), `bank_txn_id`, `journal_id`. The event also opens A13's clock `treasury.trustee_receivable` (5 business days, owner TRSY, breach Sev3). A38 raises the run-level Sev2 `settlement.trustee_return_overdue` itself, so no second Sev2 is raised here.
  - A trustee credit that A17 reports on the offer-escrow account is matched automatically only when all four agree: the reference, the exact amount, the payer name (normalised, equal to the trustee entity's registered name) and the account. On a match, this node posts A16's return journal (escrow `bank_mirror` / `offer_escrow` with `line_ref` = the order id, `external_ref` = the reference, under the exclusion `approval_id`), marks the receivable `received` and emits `treasury.exclusion_return.received { offering_id, order_id, amount_cents, journal_id }` in the same transaction.
  - Any mismatch, or a partial amount, leaves the credit in A17's hold and the receivable `open`, with a Sev2 to TRSY. TRSY may link the credit with `POST /v1/treasury/escrow/receivables/{id}/link { bank_txn_id }`, which creates a `4E` request (`escrow.exclusion_return.link`, TRSY → CFO). A partial return is never accepted as full.
  - `GET /v1/treasury/escrow/receivables` lists the receivables.
- **Fee lock (G11, the fee-sweep boundary).** When a release journal posts, `fee_lock { offering_id, fee_cents, state: locked }` is written. It is released only on A38's `settlement.run.completed { offering_id, run_id, state: 'settled' | 'refunded' }`. The rule fails closed: with no event the fees stay locked. This keeps the fee share A37 may draw from `fee_payable_realx` for an exclusion after release out of every sweep, as A38 requires.
- `GET /v1/treasury/escrow/{offeringId}` (scope `treasury:read`; sponsor users of that vehicle's tenant read their own) → `{ commitments_cents (Σ orders funded), received_cents (A16 offer_escrow credits), refunded_cents, released_cents (A16 escrow.release payout_clearing leg), allocation_total_cents, bank_escrow_cents (A17 statement for the escrow account attributable to this offering), delta_cents, offering_state (from the stored `offering.finalised` facts: `open` until a fact exists, then `finalised` with its `allocation_version`), release: { state, approval_id, journal_id, prechecks[] (the five named checks with pass or fail and code) }, settlement_account: { masked, version, name_check, kyb_ok }, bank_settlement: { state, hold_reason, payout_id, settled_at }, fee_lock: { fee_cents, state }, receivables: [{ id, order_id, amount_cents, state }], recon: { last_run_id, clean, as_of }, allowed_actions[] }`. The product map's "commitments vs received vs released" line is this contract. `GET /v1/treasury/escrow?state=open|finalised|released|refunding` returns one such row per offering (the list A74 requested as A39.1).
- **Refund-to-bank payouts (only when the offering flag `return_to_bank` is on; default off, a counsel question).** A38 R3 credits every refund to the investor's AUD wallet and owns the per-line 5-business-day `refund.pay` clock. When the flag is on, A38 emits `settlement.refund.return_to_bank { investor_id, amount_cents }`; A39 consumes each event once (unique `source_event_id`) as a system-initiated withdrawal with `origin = refund_return_to_bank`, maker `System threshold`, no passkey (the offering flag is the standing instruction), its own `withdrawal:<id>` approval under the same `withdrawal.release` rows (`AUTO` or `4E`/`6E`) and the same three checks and release assertions. It opens A13's bank-leg clock `treasury.refund_payout.bank_leg` (5 business days from the event to `settled`, owner TRSY), separate from A38's clock. **Hold and remind:** no active nominated account → `held: destination_missing`; a name mismatch → `held: destination_name_mismatch`; every `treasury.refund_payout.remind_days` (default 3), up to `treasury.refund_payout.max_reminders` (default 5), the worker emits the outbox event `treasury.refund_payout.reminder_due { withdrawal_id, investor_id, amount_cents, reminder_no, of }`, idempotent on `withdrawal_id + reminder_no`. A43 turns that event into the investor's reminder to add or fix the account (A43 is not an ancestor, so this node never calls it). A test counts the events against the schedule; the money stays in the wallet on trust and is always available to the investor; after the last reminder the row is closed `cancelled` with reason `retained_in_wallet`. Every outcome emits `treasury.refund_payout.resolved { source_event_id, investor_id, state: 'settled' | 'retained_in_wallet' | 'returned' }` for A38's R4 step and "held at bank" display. With the flag off, nothing in this path runs and no refund leaves the wallet.

**Returns, internal transfers, fees and float (`transfers/`, `fees/`)**

- **Return of unmatched funds.** `POST /v1/treasury/returns { receipt_id, reason_code }` (TRSY maker) creates the `4E` request (`suspense.return`, TRSY → CFO); on approval the worker posts A16 `suspense.return` and calls A17's `return_to_payer` payout with the approval id (A17 enforces the originating BSB and account as the only destination). A17's `POST /payments/receipts/:id/return` remains the payout step and refuses without this node's approved id. The 48 h and 5-business-day ageing alerts on held receipts are A17's. `GET /v1/treasury/returns` lists them.
- **Internal transfer.** `POST /v1/treasury/transfers { from_account, to_account, amount_cents, reason_code }` between the three client-money accounts (wallets, offer escrow, distributions) only; `operating` as source or target is refused `422 TRSY_CLIENT_MONEY_BOUNDARY` before any request exists. The request is `4E` TRSY → CFO in A14 **and** needs two `BankAuthorisation` rows (`POST /v1/treasury/transfers/{id}/authorisations`) from two distinct humans who may act `treasury.bank_authorise`. That action is defined in this node's own action rows (`treasury/agent-matrix.ts`, registered as A03 action rows, `delegable: false`), and its eligible roles are data: TRSY and CFO in the seed. Every other role, including FINA, CCO, CEO and SUPER, is refused with `403 TRSY_NOT_BANK_AUTHORISER`. Each authorisation is a fresh WebAuthn assertion over the transfer's canonical hash, and neither authoriser may be the maker; a token can never authorise. With the sandbox bank the two authorisations are recorded here and passed as `authorisations[]` on the A17 `createPayout`; with a real bank the second authorisation happens in the bank's own portal and the adapter records the bank's confirmation. That swap note is recorded in `app/evidence/A39.json` and requested as a line in A17's adapter manifest `whatChanges` (A17 owns the manifest; `SWAP-POINTS.md` is generated by A08 and never edited here). On A17's `payout.settled` the worker posts A16 `bank.internal_transfer` (`bank_mirror` / `bank_mirror`); nothing posts on approval alone. `GET /v1/treasury/transfers` lists transfers with their authorisations (authoriser, time, assertion id; never a credential).
- **Cut blackout.** No internal transfer, escrow settlement, return or fee sweep is submitted to A17 within `treasury.cut_blackout_minutes` either side of 00:00 Australia/Sydney civil time (default 10, so 23:50-00:10); items reaching release in that window wait for its end. This keeps two-account movements from straddling the 00:00 statement cut (see R10 mapping below).
- **Fees.** `GET /v1/treasury/fees/schedule` (public read; A54 renders PUB-06) → every fee with `code`, `basis` (bps or fixed cents), `rate`, `effective_from`, `effective_to`, plain-English description, from the `FeeSchedule` data (transaction fee 50 bps as the design's "0.5% transaction fee"; platform fee rows for sponsors). `POST /v1/treasury/fees/sweep { amount_cents, period }` works as follows:
  - **Maker and approval.** TRSY is the maker, and a TRSY staff token may draft the request as maker only. The request is `4E` TRSY → CFO on `fee_sweep:<id>`, A16's row.
  - **Sweepable amount.** At draft and again at submit, the amount may be at most `sweepable_cents = fee_payable_realx − Σ fee_lock.fee_cents (locked)`, floored at zero, else `409 TRSY_SWEEP_EXCEEDS_SWEEPABLE`. The fees of offerings whose run is not yet `settled` or `refunded` therefore never leave.
  - **Destination.** It is `realx-operating` only, and the route takes no destination field.
  - **Authorisation.** Two distinct human bank authorisers with `treasury.bank_authorise` authorise through `POST /v1/treasury/fees/sweeps/{id}/authorisations`. Neither may be the maker, and a token never can.
  - **Bank call.** The bank call is A17 `createPayout({ purpose: 'fee_sweep', approval_id, authorisations[], idempotencyKey: sweep id })`, a purpose requested in loop A17.1 (destination `realx-operating` only, amount equal to the approval). A16's two-sided `fee.sweep` posts only on A17's `payout.settled`.
  - **Before A17.1 lands.** Until A17.1 is in A17's frozen interface, the route returns `503 TRSY_SWEEP_NOT_AVAILABLE` and creates nothing. The sweep cases of the gate tests and the integration run are then recorded in `app/evidence/A39.json` as "not available" and do not count toward the gate. They become mandatory once A17.1 lands.
  - **Reads.** `GET /v1/treasury/fees/payable` returns `{ fee_payable_cents, locked_cents, sweepable_cents, locks[] }`. `GET /v1/treasury/fees/sweeps` returns the sweep history.
- **Float interest.** `GET /v1/treasury/float-interest` → accrued `float_interest_payable` per client-money account (from A16 `interest.float` journals A17 posts on bank credits), the policy setting `treasury.float_interest.policy` (`undecided` default | `pro_rata_to_investors` | `to_operating_with_disclosure`) and the disclosure text served to PUB-06 with the policy's effective date. `interest.sweep` stays disabled (`treasury.float_interest.sweep_enabled = false`) until counsel answers; enabling it is a config change plus a `4E` per sweep through A17.1's `interest_sweep` purpose (requested disabled), and the route returns `409 TRSY_FLOAT_POLICY_UNDECIDED` while the policy is `undecided`.

**Client-money reconciliation R10 and the precondition (`reconciliation/`)**

- A20 runs R10's account leg in its **platform** run (scope `platform`, started when A17 has published the 00:00 statements for every client-money account and no later than A20's 03:00 sweep, Australia/Sydney civil time); this node compares nothing itself. It subscribes to A20's `recon.run.completed` (scope `platform`: `run_id`, `statement_ids[]`, `result`, `open_break_ids[]`), `recon.break.opened` (`type = cash` or `source_unavailable`, `subject_ref` = the account, `delta_cents`) and `recon.break.closed`, and keeps `ClientMoneyReconStatus` per client-money account: `account_ref`, `last_run_id`, `statement_id`, `bank_closing_cents` (A17 `getStatement` for that statement id), `ledger_total_cents` (A16 balance at the cut of the ledger accounts mapped to it), `delta_cents` (from the open `cash` break, 0 when none), `clean`, `as_of`, `break_ids[]`. A contract test parses A20's exported event and `ReconNotClear` types.
- **Ledger-to-bank mapping for R10** (A20 derives it from A16's `owner_ref`; A39 asserts the expected mapping in a test): wallets account ↔ `investor_cash`, `suspense_unmatched`, `float_interest_payable` (its share) and the `payout_clearing` sub-balance of payouts drawn from it (withdrawals, refund-to-bank, returns); offer escrow account ↔ `offer_escrow` and the `payout_clearing` sub-balance of escrow settlements; distributions account ↔ `distribution_payable`, `withholding_payable`, `rounding`. A withdrawal moves `investor_cash → payout_clearing` at submit and `payout_clearing → bank_mirror` on settlement, both inside the wallets mapping, so a withdrawal submitted before the cut and settled after it leaves bank = ledger at the cut. For this to hold, `payout_clearing` must be tracked per source bank account (see the spec dependencies below). Two-account movements (internal transfers, fee sweeps) are kept away from the cut by the blackout; any item still in flight across the cut is published by A39 through A20's `registerAuxProvider('treasury_in_transit', ...)` as a reconciling line `{ payout_id, from_account, to_account, amount_cents, submitted_at, settled_at }` for A20 to net (requested as loop A20.1). Until A20.1 lands, a straddling item opens a Sev1 `cash` break that closes on the next clean run: fail-closed, documented, and exercised by a challenge.
- `assertBankLedgerClear({ as_of? })` exported from `index.ts` (and as `GET /v1/treasury/reconciliation/status`): clean only when every client-money account (never `operating`) is covered by the latest completed platform run, has no open `cash` or `source_unavailable` break, and that run's `as_of` is within `treasury.recon.freshness_hours` (default 26, so a missed run fails closed). This node calls it before every withdrawal release, escrow settlement, refund-to-bank payout, return, transfer and sweep.

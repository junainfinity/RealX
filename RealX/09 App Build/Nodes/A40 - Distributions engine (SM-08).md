# A40 - Distributions engine (SM-08)

**The monthly income run for every trust and sub-fund, from the sponsor's rent roll to money in investors' AUD wallets: an `IncomePeriod` whose net is gross rent minus PM fee, maintenance and insurance, reserve top-up and management fee; a record-date snapshot locked from A18's registry `RegisterVersion` at the record-date `as_of` (compare `match`, certified before RealX approval) before any line is calculated; per-line gross as units times net over total units rounded down with the remainder booked to the vehicle's rounding account; dust carried forward through a dedicated `distribution_carry` client-money account; variance checks against last month and the forecast; exceptions for AML holds (no notice), closed accounts and dust; an approval chain sponsor accountant to sponsor owner to FINA to CFO (CEO added above A$1,000,000.00 or at 25 % absolute variance, per A14's rows) bound to the payment file hash; funding of exactly the declared net, matched by this node's `DistributionFundingProvider` in A17, with the run's carries released into the pool once, only while reconciliation is clear; execution to AUD wallets with retries capped at the approved amount and a per-line `4E` bank payout for what cannot be credited; a run that closes only when paid equals approved; zero or reduced declarations under `6E` (ASTM → CFO then CEO, with board-resolution evidence); pauses read from A34's legal hold and any registered halt source; and the exit-waterfall computation the halts and wind-down node calls. Every transition stays inside A03's 18 SM-08 catalogue edges.**

**Oracle rule:** The node QA writes hidden oracle tests from this spec to `<workspace>\gauntlet\hidden\A40\` before the build starts ([[App Gauntlet]] §2). The builder never reads that folder. The oracle scope is the calculation, the money model and the binding:
- per line `gross_cents = floor(units_at_record_date × net_cents ÷ total_units)`, `Σ gross + remainder = net` with `0 ≤ remainder < holder_count`;
- the run identity `Σ line net (payable lines) + Σ withholding (payable lines) + remainder + Σ carry_out = net_cents + Σ carry_in`;
- the per-account money model below: the sponsor funds exactly `net_cents`; after the carry release (posted once) the run's `distribution_payable` account equals `net_cents + Σ carry_in`; after closure it is zero, `rounding` has grown by the remainder, `withholding_payable` by Σ withholding, `distribution_carry` by `Σ carry_out − Σ carry_in`, and each investor's `investor_cash` (or the bank payout) by the line's net;
- changing any line, amount, rate, destination or exception after submission makes the approval unusable for execution;
- this node's closure check matches each line to its journal and re-hashes the payment file, and A20's R8 (aggregate) and R13 (snapshot = A18's canonical CSV hash of `register_version_id`) pass (see Closure);
- the run cannot reach `executing` unless the file hash equals the approved hash, cannot reach `funded` unless the funding identity holds and A20 reports clear, and cannot reach `closed` unless paid equals approved to the cent;
- no retry or manual payout can pay a line more than its approved net;
- no agent token can ever be a checker on any distribution action.

## Build brief

This node is API-only (module `distributions` in the Fastify monolith plus the worker jobs under `jobs/distributions/`). It owns no screens: SPN-09 is rendered by A64, ADM-19 and ADM-20 by A74, POR-03 and POR-07 by A58 from A47's read models. It publishes the read contracts those screens need and enforces every rule below in the service layer, never only in a screen or a caller. Money moves only through A16's ledger, holdings are read only from A18's registry versions at the record date (compare `match`, certified before RealX approval), every approval goes through A14, and property facts (rent roll, attestation, reserve balance, legal hold) come from A34 through its `index.ts`. Withholding rates come from a `WithholdingProvider` interface that A41 will implement; until A41 lands the node ships a fixture provider (TFN quoted and AU resident: 0 bps; no TFN: 4,700 bps; foreign resident: the rule pack's MIT rate) so every line still carries a rate, a withholding amount and a net, and the contract does not change when A41 replaces it. Reconciliation, AML, receipt-matching and forecast facts are read through the interfaces named above, each behind a fake in this node's tests.

**Money model (one model, used by the calculation, the ledger postings, the checks and the oracle)**

Accounts, all client money in A16: `distribution_payable`, **one account per run** (A16's model: "one per distribution run", owner `distribution_run:<run_id>`); `distribution_carry`, one per vehicle (new; the dust owed to holders between runs); `withholding_payable` and `rounding`, one per vehicle; and each investor's `investor_cash`. Journals, in the order a run posts them:

| Step | Journal (A16 row) | Legs (debit / credit) | Amount | Approval object | `external_ref` (unique per journal type; a replay returns the original journal) | Posted when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Sponsor pays | `distribution.fund` (posted by A17 on the matched receipt) | `bank_mirror` distributions / `distribution_payable` (run) | exactly `net_cents` | none (receipt matched through this node's `DistributionFundingProvider`, reference `run_id`) | A17's `bankTxnId` | the receipt is matched |
| 2 Carries released | `distribution.carry_release` (new) | `distribution_carry` (vehicle) / `distribution_payable` (run) | `Σ carry_in` (omitted when zero) | the run's `distribution.run.approve` on `distribution_run:<run_id>` | `<run_id>:carry_release` | once, inside the `approved → funding` effector's transaction; never by `funding_check` |
| 3 Residual | `distribution.residual` (new) | `distribution_payable` (run) / `rounding` (remainder) + `distribution_carry` (`Σ carry_out`) | `remainder + Σ carry_out` (legs omitted when zero; journal omitted when both are zero) | the run's approval | `<run_id>:residual` | first posting of the execute job |
| 4 Each credited line | `distribution.pay` (without its per-line `rounding` leg, which moves to step 3) | `distribution_payable` (run) / `investor_cash` (net) + `withholding_payable` (withholding) | the line's `net + withholding` | the run's approval | `line_id` | execute and retry batches |
| 5 Each bank-paid line | `distribution.pay_bank` (new) | `distribution_payable` (run) / `payout_clearing` (net) + `withholding_payable` (withholding); A17's bank confirmation settles `payout_clearing` | the line's `net + withholding` | two references: `approval_id` = the run's approval (the draw) and `line_approval_id` = the per-line `distribution.line.manual_payout` (`4E`, object `distribution_line:<line_id>`, amount = the line's net) | `line_id` | after the per-line approval |

- `funded_gross_cents = net_cents + Σ carry_in`. Every run-scoped journal (steps 2 to 5) draws against it in A16's `ledger_approval_draw`, and A16's end-of-run check (`LDG_RUN_TOTAL_MISMATCH`) is redefined as `Σ amounts of steps 3 to 5 = funded_gross_cents`, checked when the run's last step 4 or 5 journal posts.
- `payable_cents` = Σ `net_cents` of every line that is not `carried`: normal lines, `aml_hold` lines (credited into the frozen account), and `closed_account` and `manual_payout` lines (paid by bank). `paid_cents` = Σ net of the posted step 4 and step 5 journals.
- Identities stated per account and checked by the service, the oracle and the nightly job:
  - funding check: `distribution_payable(run) = funded_gross_cents` after steps 1 and 2;
  - closure: `distribution_payable(run) = 0`; `paid_cents = payable_cents`;
  - nightly `distributions.carry_reconcile`: `distribution_carry(vehicle)` balance = Σ `amount_cents` of that vehicle's carries in states `open` and `reserved`, to the cent; a difference raises Sev1 through A13. Because a carry becomes `open` in the same transaction as the step 3 journal and becomes `consumed` in the same transaction as the step 2 journal, the identity holds at every instant, including while a run is `executing`, `partially_failed`, `retrying`, `manual_payout` or `reconciling`.
- **Required A16 changes (A16 must be reopened in the plan; this node does not build around them).** A16's current spec has `distribution_payable` one per run (kept here), and only `distribution.fund` and `distribution.pay` (with a per-line `rounding` leg and `LDG_RUN_TOTAL_MISMATCH` = Σ lines = funded gross). A16 must add: (a) the account type `distribution_carry` (liability, client money, one per vehicle, mapped by A20 to the Distributions bank account like `rounding`); (b) the rows `distribution.carry_release`, `distribution.residual` and `distribution.pay_bank` with exactly the legs, approval objects and `external_ref` rules in the table above; (c) removal of the `rounding` leg from `distribution.pay`; (d) `LDG_RUN_TOTAL_MISMATCH` redefined over steps 3 to 5 against the draw base `funded_gross_cents`; (e) a second, mandatory approval reference `line_approval_id` on `distribution.pay_bank`, bound to `distribution_line:<external_ref>` with `amount_cents` = the net leg. Until A16's spec carries these, the Specify step escalates (see Escalate).

**Calculation (pure functions in `calc.ts`, no database access; the oracle imports them directly)**

- `calculate(period, snapshot, openCarries, provider)`, run by the job `distributions.calculate` on `draft → calculating → calculated`. Inputs are the locked period's `net_distributable_cents` (the declared pool for a `reduced` run; the waterfall's `residual_cents` for an `exit` run), the snapshot and the vehicle's open carries; nothing else. A `zero` run is the one exception, by a single rule: **carries are excluded** — it reserves no carry, writes no line and no carry row, and has `net_cents = carry_in_cents = carry_out_cents = remainder_cents = funded_gross_cents = payable_cents = 0`; the vehicle's open carries stay `open` and untouched for the next `income` run. Otherwise the order is:
  1. `gross_cents = mulDivFloor(units, net_cents, total_units)` per snapshot holder (A02 bigint);
  2. `remainder_cents = net_cents − Σ gross`;
  3. `carry_in` = the holder's open carries; an investor with an open carry who is not in the snapshot gets a carry-only line (`units = 0`, `gross = 0`) that is not counted in `holder_count`;
  4. withholding from the provider on `gross + carry_in`, rate in basis points, A02 `mulBp(…, 'half_up')`;
  5. `net = gross + carry_in − withholding`;
  6. dust rule: a line whose `net < distributions.dust_threshold_cents` (config, default 100) is not paid this run: `carry_out = gross + carry_in`, `exception_code = dust_carried`, `state = carried`, `net_cents = 0`, `withholding_cents = 0` (withholding is assessed when the money is paid), and a `pending` carry of that amount is written. A line whose `gross + carry_in` is 0 is `carried` with `carry_out = 0` and no carry row. A line at or above the threshold is paid in full, including its `carry_in`.
  7. after `calculate()` returns, the job (not `calc.ts`) classifies exceptions from the facts it read (below), so every line leaves `calculating` with a handling code or is counted as an open exception.
- Invariant checked after every calculation and by the oracle: `Σ net (lines not carried) + Σ withholding (lines not carried) + Σ carry_out + remainder = net_cents + Σ carry_in`.
- **Variance.** `variance_vs_last_bps` = floor(|net − last closed income run's net| × 10,000 ÷ last net) with the sign stored separately; `variance_vs_forecast_bps` likewise against `forecast_cents`. Either is `null` when its base is missing or zero (no earlier closed run, a zero declaration last month, no forecast). `variance_abs_max_bps` = the larger of the two absolute values that are not null (null when both are). The approval payload carries `amount_cents = funded_gross_cents` and `variance_bps`, the field A14's rows read: `variance_bps = variance_abs_max_bps` (always a non-negative integer), and **when `variance_abs_max_bps` is null, `variance_bps = 2500`**, so a run with no comparable base always adds the CEO (the conservative reading, recorded for the founder). A14 owns and seeds the two rows: `amount_cents lte 100000000 and variance_bps lt 2500` → `4E` (FINA → CFO) and `amount_cents gt 100000000 or variance_bps gte 2500` → `6E` (FINA → CFO + CEO). This node follows A14 exactly: A$1,000,000.00 → `4E`, A$1,000,000.01 → `6E`; 2,499 bps (±24.99 %) → `4E`, 2,500 bps (±25.00 %) → `6E`; a run 25.00 % below last month adds the CEO exactly as one 25.00 % above does. The product map's "above $1m" is A14's reading; changing it is an A14 policy-row change, not a code change here. A run cannot be submitted without `variance_reason` (`DST_VARIANCE_REASON_REQUIRED`) when `variance_abs_max_bps ≥ distributions.variance_reason_bps` (config, default 1,000) or when either variance is null because its base is zero.
- **Exceptions**, classified by the job at the end of `calculating`, re-evaluated on entry to `in_review` and at submit (an A42 flag that changed since is applied then and regenerates the file), and confirmed by FINA on ADM-19 (`exception_confirmed_by` per line; the submit precheck `exceptions_confirmed` fails until every exception line is confirmed):
  - `aml_hold` when A42's restriction flag (read through its interface) is set on the investor: the line is still credited into the frozen `investor_cash` account, where A16 blocks every debit; the exception suppresses the "Distribution paid" notification; no reason code ever reaches an investor-facing field;
  - **who may see `aml_hold` (one rule, need-to-know as the product map's ADM-19 has it):** MLRO, CCO, the MLRO's delegate, and FINA and CFO for the runs they make or check (A11's need-to-know check), plus the service principal. For every other principal (TRSY, ASTM, TP-RE, support roles, any sponsor user, any investor, and any agent token whose human is not in that list) the code is masked everywhere it could leak: in `lines[]` the line shows `exception_code: null` and the `exceptions[]` list counts it as `restricted_count` without a line id or investor; the rendered file views and CSV downloads replace `aml_hold` by `\N`, and the raw stored file (whose hash the approval binds) is downloadable only by the permitted roles; the A14 payload's `exceptions_summary` carries counts by code with `aml_hold` folded into `restricted` and no investor id; this node's audit event for a held line (`distribution.exception.held`) names only the run and `restricted` (no line id, no investor id, no code, so it cannot be joined to a line by a reader of `lines[]`), so A10 needs no restricted field class; the line-to-investor mapping is read from `distribution_lines` under the rule above;
  - `closed_account` when the investor is `closed`: the line is paid by bank (step 5) to the verified bank account on file under a per-line `4E` TRSY → CFO approval, or held for the unclaimed-money path once counsel answers;
  - `dust_carried` as above.
  - An **open exception** is a line the job could not classify: A42's flag read failed or timed out, the investor's account status is unknown, or a `closed_account` line has no verified bank account on file. `openExceptions` (A03's `exceptions_handled` guard on `calculated → in_review`) = the count of such lines; the run stays `calculated` until the job re-reads the facts and the count is 0, or FINA assigns `manual_payout` to the line.
  - FINA may change `exception_code` only between `null`, `closed_account` and `manual_payout`, only in `in_review` (or in `calculated` for an open-exception line, to `manual_payout`), and only while no A14 request on the run is pending (`DST_RUN_FROZEN`); every change is audited and regenerates the payment file. Amounts are never editable.

**Payment file and approval binding**

- `buildPaymentFile(run)` writes the canonical CSV: UTF-8, LF, header `run_id,line_id,holder_ref,investor_id,units,gross_cents,carry_in_cents,carry_out_cents,withholding_rate_bps,withholding_cents,net_cents,exception_code,destination,record_date,register_version_id,snapshot_sha256`, one row per line sorted by `holder_ref`, no quoting of numbers, `\N` for null; `file_sha256` is the hash of that byte stream, stored in `distribution_payment_files` and shown on ADM-19's file-hash line. The file is regenerated and re-hashed on entry to `in_review`, on every exception change, and at submit; submit refuses when the hash it computes differs from the stored `file_sha256` of the page the maker saw (the submit body carries `file_sha256`; `DST_FILE_HASH_MISMATCH`).
- **Approval.** `POST /v1/distributions/runs/{id}/submit` (FINA, or FINA's agent token as maker) creates the A14 request `distribution.run.approve` on `distribution_run:<id>` with payload `{ run_id, vehicle_id, period, kind, net_cents, carry_in_cents, carry_out_cents, funded_gross_cents, amount_cents, payable_cents, withholding_cents, remainder_cents, holder_count, total_units, register_version_id, snapshot_sha256, block_number, file_sha256, variance_vs_last_bps, variance_vs_forecast_bps, variance_abs_max_bps, variance_bps, exceptions_summary }` (`exceptions_summary` = counts by code, `aml_hold` folded into `restricted`, no investor id). From that moment the run is frozen: exception edits are refused with `DST_RUN_FROZEN` until A14 decides.
- **Precheck** items this node registers with A14 (`registerPrecheck`) for `distribution.run.approve` (the design's APR-2291 check vocabulary): sponsor owner approved (`sponsor_signoff_approval_id` is `approved`); rent roll attested for the period (A34 attestation `attested`, G19); snapshot matched (`holder_count` and `total_units` equal A18's cap table at the record date); `snapshot_certified` (the snapshot's registry version is `certified` in A18); `exceptions_confirmed`; client money covers payout (the run's `distribution_payable` account plus the open carries it reserved cover `funded_gross_cents`, or the sponsor's funding instruction for `net_cents` is on file when funding follows approval); `A20.preflight({ vehicleId, purpose: 'distribution_approval' })` returns `clear` (A20 blocks this purpose on vehicle Sev1 and Sev2 breaks and on a `cash` break of a mapped client-money account); no pause source reports the vehicle paused; `variance_reason` present where required. A hard failure keeps the run in `in_review`.
- **Precheck for `distribution.run.fund`** (the request A06 seeds as APR-2291, "Distribution funding · Marion Street Warehouse · A$20,228.50"): the items "Sponsor owner approved" and "Rent roll attested" are carried from the run's decided `distribution.run.approve` request (for MRN01, APR-2289) with that request id as their source, never re-evaluated against a different approval; "Snapshot matched" is carried the same way; "Client money covers payout" is `A20.preflight({ vehicleId, purpose: 'distribution_approval' })` `clear` plus the funding identity inputs (`net_cents` expected from the sponsor, `Σ carry_in` in `distribution_carry`); the approved payment-file hash still matches; no pause source is set. `distribution.declare_reduced` has its own precheck `board_resolution_evidence` (below).
- **Reject.** The checker's rejection is A14's decision endpoint; this node registers the effector for `in_review → draft` (A03: CFO, human only): the run's lines move to `cancelled`, its reserved carries go back to `open` and the `pending` carries it wrote become `void`, the income period returns to `draft` so SP-FIN can correct it, the sponsor sign-off is marked superseded (a fresh `distribution.propose` is needed on the recalculated figures), and the rejection reason is stored and audited. The snapshot is kept unless the record date changes.
- **Binding.** The run stores `approval_id` and `approved_file_sha256` only when A14 reports `approved`; the approved `payload_sha256` includes `file_sha256`. Before `approved → funding`, before `funding → funded`, before `funded → executing`, before every execute or retry batch and before every manual payout, the service regenerates the payment file from the stored lines and compares it with `approved_file_sha256`. A difference sets `hash_mismatch = true`, refuses the step with `DST_FILE_HASH_MISMATCH`, writes audit `distribution.run.hash_mismatch` and raises Sev1 through A13. The run stays in its state (no catalogue edge leads back from these states). The only way forward is `POST …/restore-lines` (`distribution.run.restore_lines`, `4E` FINA → CFO): it rewrites the non-execution columns of every line from the approved file's stored bytes, keeps the execution columns, clears `hash_mismatch` only when the regenerated hash equals `approved_file_sha256`, and lets the run continue on the same approval, because what executes is then exactly what the checker approved. If the approved content itself is found to be wrong after approval, this node does not re-approve it: money already moved is corrected by an A39 `adjustment` under `6E`, and an unfunded run waits for the escalation below. "Changing a line after approval invalidates it" is true at three levels: the trigger refuses the write, the hash check refuses every money step, and R13 refuses closure.

**Funding, execution, retries, closure (worker jobs)**

- **Pause sources.** `index.ts` exports `registerDistributionPauseSource(name, (vehicleId) => { paused, cause, ref })`. This node registers A34's `distributions_paused` fact (set by A34's `legal_hold` effector, cause `legal_hold`) through A34's `index.ts`; A48 registers its halt state (scope `distributions` or `all`, including distress and the platform kill switch) when it lands. A batch in progress finishes the line it is posting and stops; lines already paid stay paid; when the sources clear, execution resumes from the first unposted line with no double post. This node has no legal-hold route or flag of its own; `paused` on the run is a read-only mirror.
- **Funding provider for A17.** `index.ts` registers the real `DistributionFundingProvider` through A17's `index.ts` at start-up (A17 ships only the interface and a fake): `expectedFunding(reference)` → `{ runId, vehicleId, expectedCents, payerNames[] }` where `reference` must equal a `run_id` exactly (the `DST-<yyyymmdd>-<6 base32>` form; A17's challenge text that uses a `RUN-…` reference must use this form), `expectedCents = net_cents`, and `payerNames` = the verified names of the vehicle's settlement account holder (the trustee or sponsor entity's verified bank account name, read through A33's `index.ts`). It returns non-null **only** while the run is `approved` or `funding`, `net_cents > 0`, and no `distribution.fund` journal has yet posted to the run's `distribution_payable` account; otherwise `null`. Consequences, all A17's existing hold paths: a payment that differs from `net_cents` by any amount (short or over) is held as `distribution_amount_mismatch`; a second exact payment after step 1 posted is held as `distribution_unknown_reference`; a payment for a `zero` run, a cancelled run or an unknown id is held as `distribution_unknown_reference`. A held sponsor payment is returned to the payer by A17's path and never reaches the run.
- **Funding.** The sponsor pays exactly `net_cents` into the Distributions client-money account (••8810) with reference `run_id`; A17 matches the receipt through the provider and posts step 1. `POST …/fund` (TRSY maker → CFO checker, `4E`, `distribution.run.fund`; A03 guard `signoffs [[TRSY],[CFO]] ordered`) moves `approved → funding`, and the effector posts step 2 (`external_ref = <run_id>:carry_release`) and marks the reserved carries `consumed` in the same transaction; a replay of the effector returns the original journal and changes nothing. The job `distributions.funding_check` posts nothing; it moves `funding → funded` only when: the run's `distribution_payable` account equals `funded_gross_cents` exactly (`DST_NOT_FUNDED` with the shortfall in cents; an excess cannot arise, because A17 holds any payment that is not exactly `net_cents` and any second payment); `A20.assertClear(vehicleId, 'distribution_execution')` passes (a `cash` break on a client-money account mapped to the vehicle maps to `DST_CLIENT_MONEY_DELTA`, any vehicle Sev1 or Sev2 break to `DST_RECON_BREAK_OPEN`, per A20's contract table); the hash check passes; and no pause source is set. For a `zero` run there is no step 1 or step 2 and the check compares 0 with 0.

- **Lateness sweep.** The daily job `distributions.lateness_sweep` (07:00 `Australia/Sydney`) checks every vehicle that A34 reports `operating` for every period whose `due_date` has passed: a vehicle-period with no income period, no non-cancelled run, or a run not yet `executed` (or later) on `due_date` is flagged once in `distribution_lateness` with `stage_at_flag`, sets `late = true` on the run when one exists, and raises one Sev2 alert through A13: "Payout funding short" (owners TRSY, SP-FIN) when the run is in `approved` or `funding`, otherwise "Distribution late" (owners SP-FIN, FINA). The sponsor (SP-FIN, human or agent) must supply `late_reason` through `POST /v1/sponsor/vehicles/{vehicleId}/periods/{period}/late-reason`; A47 surfaces the period to investors as "delayed" with the reason. A run executed after `due_date` stays `late`. A period the sponsor has declared `zero` in time is not late.
- **Execution.** `funded → executing` is an A14 `AUTO` row (`distribution.run.execute`, condition `file_sha256_equals_approved`); the job `distributions.execute_due` raises it for each `funded` run once the clock reaches 00:00 `Australia/Sydney` on `scheduled_pay_date` (at once when the run is funded later than that); SVCA is the decider and the decision is audited. The job `distributions.execute` calls `assertClear(vehicleId, 'distribution_execution')` and the pause sources before each batch, posts step 3 first (`external_ref = run_id:residual`), then one step 4 journal per credited line (normal and `aml_hold` lines), `external_ref = line_id`, in batches of `distributions.execute_batch_size` (default 200), idempotent per line (a replayed post returns the original journal). `closed_account` and `manual_payout` lines are set to `manual` and not attempted; `carried` lines are skipped. Each posted non-held line emits `distribution.line.paid { run_id, line_id, investor_id, net_cents, on_behalf_of }` on the outbox for A47 and the comms module; `aml_hold` lines emit nothing investor-facing. At the end of the pass: every payable line `paid` → `executing → executed`; otherwise (any line `failed`, or any line `manual`) → `executing → partially_failed`, with A03's guard fed `paymentsFailed` = lines `failed` + lines `manual` not yet paid and `paymentsPending = 0`.
- **Retries and manual payouts, inside the catalogue edges.**
  - From `partially_failed`, FINA (A03's actor; human or FINA's agent as maker) chooses: `POST …/retry` moves to `retrying` when at least one line is `failed` with attempts left; `POST …/manual-payout-mode` moves to `manual_payout` when the only unpaid lines are `manual`, **or when there is no unpaid line at all** (the run came back from `reconciling` on a mismatch that was not a missing payment, for example an R13 drift or a bank-statement break): then it is accepted only when `A20.assertClear(vehicleId, 'distribution_execution')` passes and `paid_cents = payable_cents` (otherwise `DST_RECON_BREAK_OPEN` or `DST_PAID_NE_APPROVED`), and `manual_payout → executed` fires at once (no failed, pending or unpaid manual line), after which `executed → reconciling` requests a fresh A20 targeted run. A run whose mismatch is a real under-payment has an unpaid line and follows the ordinary paths.
  - In `retrying`, the job `distributions.retry` makes up to `distributions.max_retries` (default 3) attempts per failed line with back-off, always with the line's approved `net_cents` (`DST_RETRY_EXCEEDS_APPROVED` if any caller supplies a different amount). A line that exhausts its attempts becomes `manual`. The run stays in `retrying` (the catalogue has no `retrying → partially_failed` or `retrying → manual_payout` edge) and its `manual` lines are paid by per-line manual payout while it is there. `retrying → executed` fires when no line is `failed`, `pending` or unpaid `manual`.
  - Manual payout, in `retrying` or `manual_payout`: TRSY raises `distribution.line.manual_payout` (`4E`, TRSY → CFO, object `distribution_line:<line_id>`, amount = the line's approved net) per `manual` line; on approval the service calls `A20.assertClear(null, 'payout')` and posts step 5 to the verified bank account on file, carrying the per-line approval id and drawing on the run approval, and A17's confirmation settles it. A manual payout for a line already `paid` is refused (`DST_LINE_ALREADY_PAID`). `manual_payout → executed` fires when no line is `failed`, `pending` or unpaid `manual`.
- **Closure.** `executed → reconciling` runs this node's own closure check and triggers A20's targeted run for the vehicle, within A20's frozen contract:
  - this node's **closure check** (`closure_check.ts`, in the `distributions.reconcile` job; A20's R8 is aggregate and its R13 does not touch the payment file, so these checks live here): a per-line match — for every non-carried line, the journal found through A16 by `external_ref = line_id` (step 4 or 5) has a net leg equal to the line's `net_cents` and a withholding leg equal to its `withholding_cents`; the step 3 journal (`<run_id>:residual`) has legs equal to `remainder_cents` and `carry_out_cents`; the totals equal the run's `payable_cents`, `withholding_cents`, `remainder_cents` and `carry_out_cents`; and the payment file regenerated from the lines hashes to `approved_file_sha256`. A failure is written as audit `distribution.run.closure_mismatch` with the failing lines and raises Sev1 through A13;
  - A20's **R8** (aggregate: Σ ledger credits = Σ approved net, Σ withholding = the run's tax total, bank payouts match the file) and **R13** (the snapshot recomputed from `register_version_id` and the chain holders at `block_number` reproduces `snapshot_sha256`, which is A18's canonical CSV hash of that version, so A20 needs no A40-specific format), fed through A20's `distributions` and `snapshots` inputs.
  - `reconciling → closed` only when the closure check passes, A20 reports clean, `paid_cents = payable_cents` and the run's `distribution_payable` account is zero (`DST_PAID_NE_APPROVED` otherwise; the run goes back to `partially_failed` by the catalogue's "mismatch" edge and Sev1 is raised). `closed` sets `paid_at` and writes the closing audit event; lateness was already decided by the sweep. Carry states do not change at closure. `closed` is terminal; corrections are a new `adjustment` journal by A39 under `6E` and never touch a closed run.
- **Zero or reduced declaration.** `POST …/declare` (ASTM maker, `distribution.declare_reduced`, `6E`, checkers **CFO then CEO**, exactly A03's row 36: "ASTM → [CFO], [CEO] + board resolution evidence"; A03 has no board role, so the board acts through the evidence, not as a checker) with `kind = zero | reduced`, `reason`, `board_resolution_sha256` and `board_resolution_doc_ref` (the signed board or trustee resolution, uploaded with the request into this node's insert-only `distribution_evidence` table: `doc_ref`, `sha256`, `bytes`, `uploaded_by`, `on_behalf_of_id`; A35 is not an ancestor, so nothing here depends on its document store), and for `reduced` the reduced pool (at most `net_distributable_cents`; the difference stays in the trust and is never funded). This node registers the A14 precheck `board_resolution_evidence`: it fails (`DST_BOARD_RESOLUTION_MISSING`) when the hash or document reference is absent, or when the stored document's sha256 differs from `board_resolution_sha256`, and a failed precheck keeps the request from reaching the checkers. It is accepted only on a run in `draft` (a run past `draft` is first rejected back to `draft` by the checker; otherwise `DST_DECLARE_NOT_DRAFT`). On approval `declaration_approval_id` is set and the run follows the ordinary catalogue path with the declared pool: `draft → calculating → calculated` (a `zero` run excludes carries, as stated under Calculation: no lines, no reservations, `funded_gross_cents = 0`; a `reduced` run handles carries like an `income` run, dust rule included), `calculated → in_review` after the sponsor owner's sign-off (A03's guard applies to every kind), `in_review → approved` under `distribution.run.approve` on the file hash, `approved → funding → funded` (A03's TRSY → CFO guard still applies; for a zero run no step 1 or step 2 posts and the check compares 0 with 0), `funded → executing → executed` with nothing to post for a zero run, `executed → reconciling → closed`. Investors see the declaration and reason on POR-03 through A47.
- **Cancellation.** `draft → cancelled` by the maker or any checker (A03's actors FINA, SP-FIN): `POST /v1/sponsor/distributions/runs/{id}/cancel` (SP-FIN, human or agent) and `POST /v1/distributions/runs/{id}/cancel` (FINA, human or agent), each with a required `cancel_reason`; the transaction moves reserved carries back to `open`, `pending` carries to `void` and lines to `cancelled`, and frees the income period for a new run. Nothing later than `draft` can be cancelled (`DST_INVALID_TRANSITION`).
- **Exit run.** Created by `POST /v1/distributions/runs` with `kind = exit` (FINA, human or agent as maker; A48 calls the same service through `index.ts`) from a waterfall preview. The create writes a **synthetic exit period** (`income_periods` row with `period_kind = exit`, `period = EXIT-<yyyy-mm-dd>` of the sale settlement, `gross_rent_cents` and the four cost lines 0, `net_distributable_cents = residual_cents`, `forecast_cents` null, `state = locked`, `locked_at` set, `rent_roll_import_id` and `attestation_id` null); so `income_locked` holds and the G19 rent-roll precheck is replaced for this kind by "sale settlement recorded" (A34's `under_sale → sold` fact). The record date is 00:00 on the day after sale settlement (an A18 `as_of`), and the snapshot follows the same one rule above; `net_cents = residual_cents`, carries are handled as in an `income` run (every open carry is paid out, since there is no later run; the dust rule does not apply to an exit run). `calculated → in_review` needs A03's `sponsor_owner_approved`: the sponsor owner signs off `distribution.propose` as usual, **or**, when A34 reports the sponsor in default or the vehicle `distressed`/`legal_hold`, a TP-RE (the vehicle's trustee) decision on `distribution.propose` substitutes for it. A03's guard today requires role SP-OWN, so this substitute needs the named A03 extension "SM-08 exit sign-off substitute" (`sponsor_owner_approved` accepts TP-RE when `kind = exit` and the sponsor-default fact is set); until A03 carries it, an exit run for a defaulted sponsor escalates at Specify. `in_review → approved` is `distribution.exit.approve` (below), fed into A03's `linked_approval_approved` as the run's linked approval; A03's row 35 is the run-approval row for `income`, `zero` and `reduced` runs, and the same guard reads whichever approval `approval_id` names (if A03 binds the guard to row 35's action code only, that is the same named extension). Funding comes from the sale proceeds already in client money: the sponsor or trustee pays `residual_cents` with reference `run_id` exactly like an income run.
- **Transitions.** Every transition goes through A03's `transition()` against the SM-08 table (`DST_INVALID_TRANSITION` for anything else), writes exactly one hash-chained audit event through A10 with `actor_id`, `on_behalf_of_id` and `approval_id`, and emits `distribution.run.state_changed` on A09's outbox. Time comes from A09's injectable clock.
- **Due date.** `due_date` = the last day of the period plus `distributions.pay_within_business_days` (default 10) business days, counted from the period end, not the record date, so an early record date does not shorten the window. Business days use the vehicle's state public-holiday calendar from A08 config (the state of the vehicle's property from A34; NSW when unknown) in `Australia/Sydney` time. September 2026 for a NSW vehicle: due 15 Oct 2026, because Labour Day, 5 Oct 2026, is skipped.

**Exit waterfall (for A48 and J08 step 9)**

- **Dust carried across runs (money).** Run 1 has two dust lines (A$0.40 and A$0.73) and a remainder of 3 cents; run 2 next month has the first of those holders still in the register and the second sold out: the sponsor funds exactly `net_cents` both times; after run 1 closes `distribution_carry` holds 113 cents and `distribution_payable` for run 1 is zero; in run 2 the carry release posts 113 cents, the sold-out holder gets a carry-only line, the funding check compares against `net + 113`, A16's run-total check passes, and after run 2 closes `distribution_carry` equals the open carries to the cent. Variants: the sponsor pays `net + carry_in` by mistake: A17 holds the payment as `distribution_amount_mismatch` (the provider's `expectedCents` is `net_cents`), nothing posts to the run, and the run stays `funding` with `DST_NOT_FUNDED` naming the full shortfall; the sponsor then pays exactly `net_cents` twice: the first posts step 1, the second is held as `distribution_unknown_reference`, and `distribution_payable` for the run never exceeds `funded_gross_cents`.
- **Carry release posted once (money).** `distributions.funding_check` runs three times before the sponsor pays, then the `approved → funding` effector is replayed: exactly one `distribution.carry_release` journal exists (`external_ref = <run_id>:carry_release`, the replay returns it), `distribution_carry` fell by `Σ carry_in` once, the carries are `consumed` once, and the funding check never posts a journal.
- **Nightly carry reconciliation mid-run (money).** Run 1 (with two dust lines) is stopped in `retrying` after its step 3 posted, and run 2 of the same vehicle is `funding` after its step 2 posted; `distributions.carry_reconcile` runs: `distribution_carry` equals Σ `open` + `reserved` carries to the cent (run 1's carries are already `open`, run 2's are `consumed`), no Sev1 is raised, and a third run calculated that night can reserve run 1's carries.
- **Sponsor funding matched by the provider (money).** With the real provider registered in A17, a payment of exactly `net_cents` from the vehicle's verified settlement name with reference `run_id` posts step 1; the same from another payer name is held as `distribution_payer_mismatch`; a payment for a run still `in_review`, and for a `zero` run, is held as `distribution_unknown_reference`.
- **Line changed after approval (money, G13).** After CFO approval, a test edits one line's `net_cents` by a cent directly in SQL, then another test changes a `destination`, then a third re-orders two holders' `holder_ref` values: the trigger refuses the first two, and for the one that slips through in a copy without the trigger the funding check and the execution precheck must both refuse with `DST_FILE_HASH_MISMATCH`, set `hash_mismatch`, raise Sev1 and keep the state; `restore-lines` under `4E` must restore the approved lines from the stored file, clear the flag and let the same approval continue; nothing else may move the run forward.
- **Edit after submit (money, G13).** FINA submits, then tries to change an exception from `null` to `manual_payout` while the A14 request is pending: refused with `DST_RUN_FROZEN`, the file hash is unchanged, and the approved payload names the file the checker saw. A variant edits the exception before submit and submits with the old hash in the body: `DST_FILE_HASH_MISMATCH` and no request.
- **Register version swapped after lock (money, register).** Between lock and execution the sandbox registry certifies a later version for the same record date with one extra holder and 2 units moved: the run keeps paying the locked `register_version_id`, the extra holder receives nothing, R13 reproduces the locked snapshot hash at the stored `block_number`, and A20's targeted run opens a register break rather than let the run close on the new version.
- **Record-date snapshot (money, register).** (1) A06's BAY01 September run: record day 22 Sep stored as `2026-09-23T00:00+10:00`; the lock uses A18's registry version of that `as_of` with compare `match`, the snapshot includes the trustee transfer OP-7728 acknowledged 22 Sep 17:04, holds 186 holders and 10,000 units, `snapshot_sha256` equals A18's `file_sha256` for the version, the basis is `compare_match`, and submit is refused by `snapshot_certified` until REGO's `register.certify` is approved by HCO as trustee, after which the same version and hash are recorded as `certified` with nothing recalculated. (2) A record date entered as 22 Sep 23:59 through the API is refused with `DST_RECORD_DATE_NOT_AS_OF`. (3) Only a month-end certified version (30 Sep) and an older one (20 Sep, with a transfer acknowledged 21 Sep) exist and no version at the record date: neither is used; lock requests the on-demand internal version and extract for `2026-09-23T00:00` and is refused with `DST_NO_REGISTER_VERSION` until the compare arrives. (4) The compare for the record date is `drift`: `DST_SNAPSHOT_MISMATCH`. (5) An instruction submitted but not acknowledged at the record date: `DST_SNAPSHOT_STALE` until it is acknowledged or rejected and the version re-fetched.
- **Rounding that leaks or manufactures cents (money).** 10,000 generated runs with nets from 1 cent to A$50m, holder sets from 1 to 5,000 with random units and open carries: every line is `floor(units × net ÷ total_units)`, the remainder is below the holder count, the run identity holds, and after execution the run's `distribution_payable` is exactly zero, the remainder is in `rounding`, the carries are in `distribution_carry` and nothing is in any other account.
- **Retry that pays twice or pays more (money).** The execute job is killed after posting line 137 of 186 and restarted; then a retry is requested for a failed line with an amount one cent above its net; then a line that fails all three attempts in `retrying` is paid by manual payout while the run stays in `retrying`; then a line already `paid` is submitted to manual payout: the restart posts lines 138 to 186 exactly once (A16 replay returns the original journal for 137), the retry is refused with `DST_RETRY_EXCEEDS_APPROVED`, the exhausted line is paid once by `distribution.pay_bank` and the run reaches `executed` by `retrying → executed`, the paid-line payout is refused with `DST_LINE_ALREADY_PAID`, and `paid_cents` equals `payable_cents` at closure.
- **Closed account at closure (money, G14).** One of 186 holders is `closed`: the line is `manual` from execution, the run goes `executing → partially_failed → manual_payout`, TRSY's per-line `4E` request posts `distribution.pay_bank` with that line approval id after `assertClear(null, 'payout')`, A17's sandbox confirmation settles it, the run reaches `executed → reconciling → closed`, the closure check finds the bank journal by `line_id` with both approval references, and `distribution_payable` for the run is zero.
- **Reconciling mismatch with nothing unpaid (SM-08).** Every line is paid, then A20's targeted run reports an R13 `snapshot_drift` (planted), so `reconciling → partially_failed`; `manual-payout-mode` is refused with `DST_RECON_BREAK_OPEN` while the break is open; once REGO resolves it, `manual-payout-mode` is accepted with zero unpaid lines, the run goes `manual_payout → executed` at once and `executed → reconciling → closed` on a fresh clean A20 run, with no journal posted in the loop.
- **Zero declaration end to end (compliance, SM-08).** ASTM declares a zero run in `draft` for a vehicle with two open carries (A$0.40 and A$1.20, the second above the dust threshold) and attaches the board resolution; CFO then CEO approve (`6E`); the run walks the ordinary edges to `closed` with no line, no journal and `funded_gross_cents = 0`; both carries are still `open` with `reserved_by_run_id` null, `distribution_carry` is unchanged at 160 cents, no transition outside the catalogue is used, and investors see `declared_zero` with the reason; the next `income` run reserves and pays both. Variants: the declare without `board_resolution_sha256`, and with a hash that differs from the stored document, is refused by the precheck with `DST_BOARD_RESOLUTION_MISSING` and never reaches the CFO; a declaration where the CFO approves and a sponsor user or any agent token tries to act as the second checker is refused; a declare on a run in `in_review` is refused with `DST_DECLARE_NOT_DRAFT`.
- **Exit run end to end (money, SM-08).** A waterfall preview with proceeds A$2m gives `residual_cents`; FINA creates the exit run: a synthetic `EXIT-<date>` period is written `locked`, the snapshot is taken at 00:00 after settlement, every open carry is paid with no dust rule, the sponsor owner signs off, `distribution.exit.approve` (FINA → CFO + LEGL, plus TP-RE when the trustee countersign resolver says so) approves, and the run walks `funding → funded → executing → executed → reconciling → closed` with the lines equal to the preview's lines. A variant with the sponsor in default: SP-OWN is absent, TP-RE's decision on `distribution.propose` moves the run to `in_review` only when A03's exit sign-off extension is present (otherwise the build escalates, as stated).
- **Cancel (SM-08, G22).** A sponsor agent cancels a `draft` run with a reason through `SPN.distribution.cancel` after a rejection: no carry is left `reserved` or `pending` for the run (the rejection already returned them to `open` or `void`, and the cancel asserts it), the lines stay `cancelled`, and the period can start a new run; a staff agent cancels through `M12.run.cancel`; a cancel on a `calculated` run is refused with `DST_INVALID_TRANSITION`; both routes pass the agent-parity suite through a token and through MCP.
- **Reject and resubmit (compliance, SM-08).** CFO rejects a run in `in_review`: the run is `draft`, its lines are `cancelled`, its reserved carries are `open`, the period is editable, and the next calculation needs a fresh sponsor sign-off.
- **AML hold with a notice (compliance, tipping-off, G14).** An investor restricted by A42 holds 480 units: the line posts into the frozen account, no `distribution.line.paid` event reaches the comms outbox for it, `me/distributions` shows the row as `paid` with no exception field, `distributions/runs/{id}` lists the exception for MLRO, CCO, and FINA and CFO on this run, and for TRSY, ASTM, TP-RE, a sponsor user and a FINA agent whose human is not on the run shows `exception_code: null` in `lines[]` and only `restricted_count` in `exceptions[]`; the payment-file view and CSV download show `\N` for those roles and the raw file download is refused to them; A14's `exceptions_summary` shows `restricted: 1` and no investor id; the `distribution.exception.held` audit row names only the run and `restricted`; and a support-role read contains no marker at all.
- **Agent or single person as checker (security, G13, G22).** A sponsor agent token acting for SP-FIN proposes the run and then tries to decide `distribution.propose`; a staff agent acting for FINA submits the run and then tries to decide `distribution.run.approve` as CFO; a sponsor whose only user holds both SP-FIN and SP-OWN tries to sign off its own proposal: all three are refused (A14 refuses tokens as deciders; SoD counts the agent as its human), the audit rows read "agent <prefix> on behalf of <person>" for the maker steps, and the run stays `calculated`.
- **Agent outside its mandate (security, G22, D04).** A sponsor agent whose mandate allows only BAY01 proposes a HAW01 run (`G22_VEHICLE_NOT_ALLOWED`, resolved from the run id); a TRSY agent with a per-action cap of A$2,000 requests funding of the A$2,592.00 run (`G22_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, cap `per_action`); a FINA agent submits twice in a day past its daily cap (`G22_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, cap `daily`); a revoked token (`TOKEN_REVOKED`); and each kill-switch tier in turn, user, tenant and platform (`423 AGENTS_PAUSED` naming the tier). Each refusal leaves no run change and writes A12's three rows; each successful agent fund, retry, manual-payout, declare and restore request produces one sensitive-action notice to the person and none to the agent.
- **Funding with a hidden delta or an open break (money, compliance).** A16 shows the run's `distribution_payable` equal to `funded_gross_cents`, but A20 reports a −1 cent `cash` break on the Distributions client-money account mapped to the vehicle, then separately a Sev2 break open on the vehicle: `funding → funded` is refused with `DST_CLIENT_MONEY_DELTA` and `DST_RECON_BREAK_OPEN` respectively, no line posts, and clearing the condition lets the same run proceed without a new approval (the hash is unchanged). The submit precheck on another run of the same vehicle fails `preflight` for `distribution_approval` while the Sev2 break is open.
- **Court order mid-batch (compliance).** While the execute job is on line 90 of 186, A34's `property.record_court_order` effector (through A34's fake) sets `distributions_paused`: the job finishes line 90, posts nothing further, the run shows `paused = true` with cause `legal_hold` in `executing`, the investor rows for 91 to 186 show `scheduled`, the next batch is refused with `DST_LEGAL_HOLD`, and the discharge resumes at line 91 with no line posted twice. A48's fake source reporting `distress` produces `DST_DISTRIBUTIONS_PAUSED` the same way. No route of this node can set or clear a pause.
- **CEO threshold boundary (compliance, G13).** Runs of A$1,000,000.00 and A$1,000,000.01 with 0 % variance; A$10,000 runs at +24.99 %, +25.00 %, −24.99 % and −25.00 % against last month; 25.00 % against forecast only; a run whose last closed run was a zero declaration and which has a forecast 3 % away; and a vehicle's first run with no forecast: A14 resolves `4E` (FINA → CFO) for A$1,000,000.00, both 24.99 % cases and the zero-base run with a forecast (`variance_bps = 300`), and `6E` (FINA → CFO + CEO) for A$1,000,000.01, both 25.00 % cases, the forecast case and the no-base run (`variance_abs_max_bps = null`, `variance_bps = 2500`), always from A14's seeded rows reading the payload's `variance_bps` and never from a literal; both zero-base runs cannot be submitted without a reason.
- **Guard inputs (SM-08).** For each row of the guard-input table: `funding → funded` refused when `distribution_payable` equals `net_cents` but a 113-cent `carry_in` makes `funded_gross_cents` larger; `reconciling → closed` accepted with `paid_cents = payable_cents` while `funded_gross_cents` is larger by the remainder and carries, and refused when paid is one cent short; `calculated → in_review` refused with the sign-off approved while one line's A42 flag read timed out (`openExceptions = 1`), and accepted after the re-read.
- **Staff lock (G13).** FINA locks a sponsor's confirmed period through `M12.run.lock`: a `distribution.period.lock` request (`4E`, FINA → ASTM) is created and the period stays `confirmed`; ASTM approves and the period is `locked` with `lock_approval_id`; a FINA agent as maker succeeds and as checker is refused; the sponsor's own SP-FIN lock needs no request.
- **Lateness in every stage (compliance).** On `due_date` the sweep flags: a run still `funding` ("Payout funding short", TRSY and SP-FIN), a run stuck at `calculated` ("Distribution late", SP-FIN and FINA), and an operating vehicle with no run for the period (`stage_at_flag = no_run`) — each once, `late = true` where a run exists, and A47 shows "delayed" once a reason is given; a second sweep raises nothing new; a `scheduled_pay_date` after `due_date` is refused with `DST_PAY_DATE_AFTER_DUE`.
- **Due date across a state holiday (compliance).** For a NSW vehicle the September 2026 run's `due_date` is 15 Oct 2026 (5 Oct Labour Day skipped); switching the vehicle to a state without that holiday gives 14 Oct, which is A06's BAY01 (VIC) case; an early record date of 22 Sep does not move the due date; `scheduled_pay_date` defaults to 10 Oct 2026 for both, is returned by `sponsor/distributions/runs` and `me/distributions`, and the run is not `late` when executed on 10 Oct.
- **Design fidelity (read contracts).** From A06's seed and DF-A40-01, `sponsor/distributions/runs` for Bay Road returns `total_cents = 259200`, `per_unit_display = "0.2592"`, five chain stages with the design's labels and statuses for Mia Chen (`your_turn` for her, `waiting` for Leo P. and for any agent token), and three past runs Jun, Jul and Aug each at 259200 / "0.2592"; `me/distributions` for Alex Morgan returns the August Bay Road line as 120 units, gross 3110 cents, `paid` 10 Sep 2026, and the September line as 3110 cents `scheduled` for 10 Oct 2026; the summary returns FY27 paid net equal to the sum of his paid lines. A response carrying a float, a `number` money field, a missing stage or a plain "Approve" affordance for a token fails the contract test.
- **Exit waterfall that goes negative (money).** Proceeds A$1.5m against secured debt A$1.6m plus costs: the preview returns zero lines and `shortfall_cents = 10,000,000` plus costs, no negative line anywhere, and creating an exit run from it is refused with `DST_WATERFALL_SHORTFALL`; with proceeds A$2m the lines sum to the residual exactly under the same floor-and-remainder rule.

# A34 - Properties, valuations and proof of property (SM-03)

**The property side of every listing as one API module: property master data with the twenty-state lifecycle SM-03 (25 transitions, each with one named driver; title verification mandatory before `spv_formed`, owner of record the custodian or trustee), a due-diligence pack whose missing or expired items block IC review and the offer submission, valuation engagements and records from panel valuers (at most six months old, at most three months old for a listing, moves above 10% need comparable sales, committee publish under `6E` updating the token valuation and the resale band reference), the monthly proof-of-property attestation with evidence and public flags (a flag of its own, never the distribution-approval flag: design bug 2; ten days overdue pauses the sponsor's new offers through A33's listing pause), an obligations calendar (rates, land tax, insurance, certificates), distress signals and legal hold as data the halts node consumes, investment-committee approval under `6E`, and two sandbox adapters for rent-roll CSV import and valuer report upload.**

**Build status:** not started. **Milestone:** AM3 (Business services). **Lane:** Services. **Planner:** Opus 5.5 (high). **Builder:** Grok 4.7 (xhigh). **Node QA:** Opus 5.5 (high, every loop). **Graph QA:** Fable 5.1 (high, at milestone close).
**Estimate:** 10-14 agent-hours (build, challenge and verify loops).

**Depends on:** [[A33 - Sponsor tenancy, entities, licences of sponsors and admission pipeline]], [[A14 - Approvals engine and maker-checker (SM-00)]], [[A19 - Rules engine- gates G01-G22, rule packs, threshold monitors, simulator]]
**Unlocks:** [[A36 - Offerings- offer terms, listing review, raise monitor (SM-04)]], [[A40 - Distributions engine (SM-08)]], [[A48 - Halts, kill switches and wind-down]], [[A54 - Public site- home, investments, property page, sponsor profile, fees]], [[A63 - Sponsor console- new listing wizard SPN-03 to SPN-06 and offer monitor SPN-07]], [[A64 - Sponsor console- register, distributions, property reporting, valuations, announcements]], [[A70 - Console- property register, valuations workbench, property operations]], [[A97 - P3- minors, development and debt vehicles, licensed-market venue mode]], [[A98 - Partner principals- trustee-RE, valuer, property manager, auditor, regulator observer and licensee users]]

## Build brief

This node is API-only (module `properties` in the Fastify monolith plus its worker jobs) and two adapter packages (`property-data`, `valuation-feed`). It owns no screens: SPN-03 and SPN-04 are rendered by A63, SPN-10 and SPN-11 by A64, ADM-05, ADM-06 and ADM-22 by A70, the PUB-04 proof-of-property tab, key facts and details rows by A54. Every rule below is enforced in the API, never only in a screen. The module reads sponsors, entities and vehicles from A33, submits every privileged change through A14, supplies facts to A19's evaluators for G06, G09, G19 and G20 through the `FactProvider`, and sets A33's listing pause; it never evaluates a gate itself and never opens a halt except through A19's `openHalt` interface.

**Time basis (applies everywhere below).** Every age and due date is counted in whole civil days in `Australia/Sydney` (AEST or AEDT as the date requires), from A02's clock: `age_days(d) = civilDate(now, 'Australia/Sydney') - d`. A stored date such as `valuation_date` or `search_date` is a civil date; a stored instant carries its offset. "Current while `age_days <= N`" means stale from 00:00 Sydney time on day N+1. Deadlines stated as "23:59" are 23:59:59 Sydney local time on that civil date.

- A03 §2.7 names PROP, ASTM and SVCA as actors of rows 15 and 16, while the product map's ADM-05 action lets LEGL flag distress; this node follows A03 (maker ASTM, or PROP for remediation) and lists the LEGL question for A03 (Specify).
- Structural guards beyond the table (the "RealT lesson"): row 10 is refused with `PROP_TITLE_REQUIRED` unless a `TitleVerification` row in state `verified` exists for this property, with `PROP_VEHICLE_REQUIRED` unless `vehicle_id` names an A33 vehicle whose `trustee_entity_id` equals the property's, and with `PROP_VEHICLE_TAKEN` when that vehicle's `property_id` slot is already filled; the effector fills A33's `vehicle.property_id` once. Linking needs no separate A14 request because both bound facts are already approved (the title under row 5, the vehicle's trustee under A33's admission) and the guards re-check both in the same transaction; the act is human-only (§8.1) because it binds the legal owner to the trust.
- Database protection of the title rule: (a) a check constraint `lifecycle_state NOT IN ('title_verified', 'spv_formed', 'listed', 'funded', 'withdrawn', 'operating', 'distressed', 'legal_hold', 'exit_proposed', 'exit_approved', 'under_sale', 'sold', 'wound_down') OR (title_verified_on IS NOT NULL AND title_verified_approval_id IS NOT NULL)`; (b) a `BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE OF title_verified_on, title_verified_approval_id` trigger that refuses the write unless a `title_verifications` row exists for the same `property_id` with `state = 'verified'` and `approval_id = NEW.title_verified_approval_id`, and refuses any change once the column is set; (c) the API database role has no `UPDATE` privilege on those two columns: only the `SECURITY DEFINER` function `properties.apply_title_verified(property_id, approval_id)`, called by the row 8 effector, writes them. A direct `UPDATE properties SET title_verified_on = now()` through the API role therefore fails on privilege, and through any role on the trigger.
- `POST /v1/properties` (SP-OPS, SP-OWN or their agents) creates a `sourced` property with its due-diligence template instantiated (item 2); RealX never creates a property of its own (D02). `PATCH /v1/properties/{id}` edits master data only while the state is `sourced`, `screening` or `due_diligence` (`PROP_EDIT_LOCKED` afterwards); a later change is an amendment request `M03.propertyAmendment` through A14 (§8.2) with the diff in the payload.
- `POST /v1/properties/{id}/submit` is the sponsor sending the property to RealX: it sets `sent_to_realx_at` and emits `property.sent_to_realx` for the INVM queue; it changes no state (A03 gives `sourced -> screening` to INVM and PART, row 1). It is refused with `PROP_DD_INCOMPLETE` (naming the items) or `PROP_DD_EXPIRED` when any **screening item** (item 2) is missing or expired. It is not an A36 call: A36's offer submission is a separate, later act (item 2's last bullet) and carries the sponsor's two-person approval (`listing.submit`, J12's "two people at the sponsor"); this node has no sponsor-side approval.
- `GET /v1/properties?state=&type=&sponsor=&cursor=` is the ADM-05 register list (staff) and the sponsor's own list (tenant-scoped by RLS); each row carries `code`, `sponsor`, `type`, `lifecycle_state`, `valued_on`, `nav_per_unit_cents`, `lvr_bps` (from the vehicle's debt and the current valuation), `proof_score` (item 5), `top_holder_bps` (A18 `getTopHolderBps`; `null` until issued). `GET /v1/properties/{id}` returns the detail with tabs' data: master, due diligence, title, valuations, attestations, obligations, distress signals, lifecycle history. A property id the caller cannot see returns `404`, never `403`.
- Supply calculator: `GET /v1/properties/{id}/supply-preview?units=` returns `nav_per_unit_cents` for the requested unit count using item 4's formula and the current valuation, defaulting `units` to `offer.default_supply_units` from A19's `getEffectiveParams` (10,000); it writes nothing.

**2. Due-diligence pack (SPN-04, ADM-05).**
- `DueDiligenceItem` (`dd_items`): `property_id`, `kind` (`title_search`, `contract_of_sale`, `valuation`, `building_pest`, `insurance_certificate`, `rates_notice`, `land_tax_assessment`, `strata_report`, `leases_rent_roll`, `compliance_certificates` (smoke alarm, pool, fire safety, electrical as sub-items), `planning_zoning`, `firb_evidence` where a zone flag needs it), `required` (from the template for the property type: residential requires `strata_report` only for `unit` and `multiunit`; commercial requires `leases_rent_roll` and `planning_zoning`; every type requires `title_search`, `contract_of_sale`, `valuation`, `building_pest`, `insurance_certificate`, `rates_notice`, `land_tax_assessment`, `compliance_certificates`), `screening_item` (true for `title_search`, `contract_of_sale` and `valuation`: the three items INVM needs to screen), `doc_id` (an A35 document; the fixture document store satisfies the interface until A35 lands), `issued_on`, `expires_on` (by rule: title search `issued_on + title.search_max_age_days`; valuation `valuation_date + valuation.listing_max_age_days`; insurance from the certificate's own date; the rest never), `state` (`missing` -> `uploaded` -> `accepted` | `rejected`; any of `uploaded`, `accepted` -> `expired` when `expires_on` passes; `rejected` or `expired` -> `uploaded` on a new document), `uploaded_by`, `on_behalf_of`, `reviewed_by`, `review_note`.
- `PUT /v1/properties/{id}/due-diligence/{kind}` attaches a document (sponsor users and their agents, or a TP-VAL or TP-PM partner user for the kinds §9 allows); `POST .../due-diligence/{kind}/review` (INVM, ASTM; LEGL for `title_search`) accepts or rejects with a note; a rejection writes `property.sponsor_notice.requested` and keeps the property where it is.
- The order of checks (one order for this node and A36, consistent with J12): the sponsor's send needs the screening items present (item 1); `due_diligence -> ic_review` needs **every** required item `accepted` and none expired (row 4); A36's offer submission reads `getDueDiligenceStatus(propertyId) -> { complete: boolean, missing: kind[], expired: kind[], not_accepted: kind[] }` (exported from `index.ts`) and refuses with its own `OFR_DD_INCOMPLETE` when `missing` or `expired` is non-empty, alongside its own `spv_formed`-or-later precondition. The design's SPN-04 "Missing or expired item: blocks submission" is therefore enforced twice: at IC review here and at the offer submission in A36. The nightly job `properties.dd_expiry` moves items past `expires_on` to `expired`; after `spv_formed` an expired insurance certificate also becomes an attestation item and an obligation (items 5 and 7), not a lifecycle block.
- The design's SPN-04 rows are this table filtered to five kinds for a residential house (`title_search` "Uploaded 12 Sep", `valuation` "Panel valuer · A$1,924,000" Uploaded, `building_pest`, `leases_rent_roll`, `insurance_certificate` Missing); A63 renders the full required set for the property type with the same CheckList pattern. The seed's Marine Parade sits in `due_diligence` with the insurance certificate `missing`, which this order allows (screening needed only the three screening items), and its `POST .../ic-review` is refused `PROP_DD_NOT_ACCEPTED` naming `insurance_certificate`.

**3. Title verification (LEGL, `4E`).**
- `TitleVerification` (`title_verifications`): `property_id`, `title_ref`, `search_doc_id`, `search_date`, `registered_proprietor_entity_id` (an A33 entity), `proprietor_capacity` (`custodian` | `trustee`), `encumbrances[]` (mortgages, caveats, easements with holder and document), `dealing_numbers[]`, `verified_by` (LEGL), `approval_id`, `state` (`draft` -> `submitted` -> `verified` | `returned` | `defective`), `document_hash_anchor` (optional; the search document's sha256, handed to A24's `anchorDocumentHash` when the token exists).
- `POST /v1/properties/{id}/title-verification` drafts the record from the search document; `POST .../title-verification/submit` creates the A14 request `M03.titleVerification` (row 5, `4E`, LEGL -> CCO) with the canonical record as payload. Precheck (registered through `registerPrecheck('M03.titleVerification', ...)`): `registered_proprietor_entity_id` must equal the property's `custodian_entity_id` or `trustee_entity_id` and that A33 entity's `entity_type` must be `custodian` or `trustee` (`PROP_TITLE_OWNER_MISMATCH`, a hard `fail`: A14 ends the request `rejected` at submit, the record becomes `returned`, the property stays in `title_verification` so LEGL can submit a fresh search, for example once settlement to the custodian is registered); `age_days(search_date) <= title.search_max_age_days` (`PROP_TITLE_SEARCH_STALE`); an undischarged mortgage or caveat not declared in the vehicle's debt or listed as an accepted encumbrance is a `warn` the checker must acknowledge. On approval the effector sets `verified`, calls `apply_title_verified`, runs row 8 and writes audit `property.title.verified`. A defect that cannot be cured (LEGL's `defect` route, or CCO rejecting with reason `title_defect`) runs row 9; the sponsor is told the category, not the counterparty details.

**4. Valuations (G20) and the token valuation.**
- `ValuationEngagement` (`valuation_engagements`): `engagement_id` (`VEN-<6 base32>`), `property_id`, `valuer_entity_id` (an A33 `valuer_firm` entity on the panel, `VAL_NOT_PANEL` otherwise), `purpose` (`listing`, `periodic`, `event`), `commissioned_by`, `on_behalf_of`, `due_by`, `state` (`commissioned` -> `report_received` -> `closed` | `cancelled`). `POST /v1/properties/{id}/valuations/commission` (SP-FIN, SP-OWN and their agents; VALO for a RealX-scheduled periodic valuation) creates it and writes `property.sponsor_notice.requested` to the firm's TP-VAL users; the ADM-06 "commissioned" row state is this record. The SPN-11 "Commission or upload" choice is: commission (this route) or upload a report the sponsor already holds (next bullet, `engagement_id` null).
- `Valuation` (`valuations`): `valuation_id` (`VAL-<yyyymmdd>-<6 base32>`), `property_id`, `vehicle_id`, `engagement_id` (nullable), `valuer_entity_id` (panel valuer with a current registration; `VAL_NOT_PANEL` otherwise), `valuer_user_id` (TP-VAL, when uploaded by the valuer), `report_doc_id`, `valuation_date`, `property_value_cents`, `basis` (`market_value`, `desktop`, `kerbside`, `full`), `purpose`, `comparable_sales[]` (each: address, sale date, price cents, source doc id, distance or relevance note), `cash_cents`, `debt_cents`, `liabilities_cents` (the vehicle's figures at `valuation_date`, supplied by the sponsor's fund accountant; when the vehicle exists, `cash_cents` must equal A16 `getCashBalance(vehicleId, valuation_date)` or the submission is refused `VAL_CASH_MISMATCH`), `units` (A18 `getUnitsOnIssue`, or the planned supply before issue), `nav_per_unit_cents`, `change_bps`, `property_value_change_bps`, `independent_attestation_doc_id`, `state` (`draft` -> `submitted` -> `in_review` -> `committee` -> `published` | `rejected`; `in_review -> draft` when VALO requests more evidence; `published -> superseded` when the next one publishes), `publish_approval_id`, `published_at`, `valid_until` (`valuation_date + valuation.max_age_days`).
- Formula, computed only on the server with A02's `navPerUnit({propertyValueCents, cashCents, debtCents, liabilitiesCents, units})` (half-up, A02's documented rule). Two change figures, both from exact rationals rounded half-up: `change_bps` is the change in `nav_per_unit_cents` against the previous published valuation (the "Change" the screens show); `property_value_change_bps` is the change in `property_value_cents` (the basis of the comparables rule, because comparable sales evidence the property value, not cash or debt movements); both `null` for a first valuation. Fixed vectors: PAD01 previous published valuation A$1,000,000 value, A$30,200 cash, A$0 debt, A$6,200 liabilities, 10,000 units = A$102.40 (ADM-06 "Old $102.40"); new valuation `(1,180,000 + 38,400 - 0 - 6,200) / 10,000 = A$121.22`, `change_bps = 1,838` (+18.4%, SPN-11 and ADM-06), `property_value_change_bps = 1,800`; Marine Parade `1,924,000 / 10,000 = A$192.40` with zero cash, debt and liabilities before issue. A client-supplied `nav_per_unit_cents` that differs from the server's is refused with `VAL_FORMULA_MISMATCH` (the SPN-11 preview calls `GET /v1/valuations/preview`, never computes locally).
- Age rules, all read from A19's `getEffectiveParams`, never literals in code: a valuation is current while `age_days(valuation_date) <= valuation.max_age_days` (183: current at 183, stale at 184). `POST /v1/valuations/{id}/submit`, the publish request and the publish effector each refuse a valuation with `age_days > valuation.max_age_days` with `VAL_TOO_OLD`, and one with `purpose = listing` and `age_days > valuation.listing_max_age_days` (92: allowed at 92, refused at 93) with `VAL_LISTING_TOO_OLD`. When `|property_value_change_bps| > valuation.comparables_required_move_bps` (1,000), at least `valuation.comparables_min_count` (3) comparable sales, each with `age_days(sale_date) <= valuation.comparables_max_age_days` (365), are required or the submission is refused with `VAL_COMPARABLES_REQUIRED` naming the count found.
- Flow (J13): SP-FIN or the valuer uploads (`POST /v1/properties/{id}/valuations`, or the `valuation-feed` adapter's intake) -> `draft`; `POST /v1/valuations/{id}/submit` (SP-FIN, SP-OWN or their agents; TP-VAL for its engagements) -> `submitted` (a submission to RealX, not an approval: the product map's SPN-11 chain is "Sponsor submitted → RealX valuation officer → Second approver" with no second sponsor person); VALO takes it (`in_review`), attaches the review note and may return it for more evidence; `POST /v1/valuations/{id}/publish-request` creates `M03.valuationPublish` (row 6, `6E`; maker VALO; checker sets from A03 §4.1 row 6, currently `[CFO]` and `[CCO]` interim, with the valuation committee's independent member's signed attestation attached as evidence: the request is refused `VAL_INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` without `independent_attestation_doc_id`; this node never names roles in code) -> `committee`. - Attestation transitions (normative; anything else `ATT_ILLEGAL_TRANSITION`):

| From | To | Driver |
|---|---|---|
| (none) | open | job `properties.attestation_open` |
| open | prepared | item fill: every required item `complete` or `not_due` |
| prepared | open | an item edited back to `missing` |
| open, prepared | overdue | job `properties.attestation_sweep` at `grace_until` (sets `went_overdue`) |

| attested | reviewed | `POST /v1/attestations/{id}/review {result: accepted}` (PROP) |

- The attestation flag is its own field (design bug 2): nothing outside this module writes `attested_at` or `state`; A40's distribution approval reads `getAttestation(vehicleId, period)` for its G19 check and cannot change it; approving a distribution leaves an `open` attestation `open`.
- G19 facts for A19's `FactProvider` (fact name `attestation`, shape agreed with A19 at Specify): for vehicle-scoped action kinds (`listing_go_live`), the vehicle's latest attestation `{ attestation_id, period, period_end, due_at, grace_until, state, went_overdue, late_days, items: [{kind, state}] }`; for tenant-scoped kinds (`offer_submit`), `{ tenant_id, attestations: [same shape, one per property in funded, operating, distressed or legal_hold] }`. - Overdue and the pause: job `properties.attestation_sweep` (hourly) moves rows past `grace_until` to `overdue`, writes audit `property.attestation.overdue`, raises A13 `property.proof_overdue` (Sev3, owner PROP, sponsor visible) and emits `property.attestation.overdue`. Existing live offers, distributions and holdings are untouched. The pause is recorded as a proposed sponsor-terms clause for counsel (Human gates).
- Public flags: `GET /v1/properties/{id}/proof` (public read, cached 60 s) returns `{ register_chain: { units, tokens, checked_at, result }, rows: [ { kind, title, detail, when, state } ] }` where `register_chain` comes from A20 `getLastRecon(vehicleId)` (fixture stub until A20 is wired; a break renders `result = break`) and `rows` are, in this order and with these titles: Title search ("Registered proprietor: custodian for the trust" | "trustee for the trust", `when` = search date), Insurance certificate ("Building and landlord cover, current", `when` = "to <valid until>"), Rent roll attested ("<period> · no arrears over 14 days" or "<period> · arrears A$x", `when` = attested date), Independent valuation ("A$<nav> per token", `when` = valuation month), Client-money reconciliation ("Trust bank ledger matches distributions paid", `when` = last clean run). `state` is `ok`, `due`, `overdue` or `failed`; PUB-04's card chip, tab row and PUB-05's sponsor profile render amber for `due`/`overdue` and red for `failed`; `proof_score` (0-100: the share of rows `ok`, minus `proof_of_property.failed_row_penalty` (20) for any `failed`, floored at 0) feeds ADM-05 and ADM-29.

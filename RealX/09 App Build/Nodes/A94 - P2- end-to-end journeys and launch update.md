# A94 - P2: end-to-end journeys and launch update

**One Playwright suite over the P1 launch stack plus the P2 modules that walks the three P2 journeys through the real screens with real passkeys and hardware-key staff sign-in: J07 a wholesale holder sells 100 units at A$99.80 on the guarded resale board, another holder buys from the cheapest asks, AUD and units settle together under the automatic settlement job, the trustee register records the trade only after the registry acknowledges, and a move of more than 10 percent in an hour halts the board for 30 minutes; J08 a five-year liquidity vote is proposed, published with a record date whose snapshot locks after that date, halts the board, is voted with signed ballots weighted by snapshot units, certified by the trustee or RE (never the proposer), resumes the board, and ends in a sale, an exit waterfall, a burn equal to supply and a retired token; J16 a retail investor answers the target-market questions, buys into a registered-scheme sub-fund with a PDS and a TMD, takes the cooling-off branch only when the listing sets it, and an outside-target-market dealing opens a report item on a 10-business-day clock. The four named negative tests (flag off refuses listing; a halt blocks the board; vote weight comes from the snapshot, not the live balance; no agent can vote while counsel's question on agent ballots is open) and every catalogue failure path run as negative tests, every P2 action is exercised once under a scoped token through the SDK and the MCP server, A88's P1 regression is re-run on the P2 build, and A89's launch profile gains a P2 overlay that keeps `offers.retail`, `resale.board` and `governance.votes` off by default and documents the licence-binding step as HG-LEGAL.**

**Build status:** not started. **Milestone:** AM9 (P2 features, from about 8 April 2027). **Lane:** P2. **Planner:** Opus 5.5 (high). **Builder:** Grok 4.7 (xhigh). **Node QA:** Opus 5.5 (high, every loop). **Graph QA:** Fable 5.1 (high, at milestone close).
**Estimate:** 16-24 agent-hours (build, challenge and verify loops). `verify:p2` alone runs the suite three times at up to 60 minutes each plus A88's regression, the node QA adds a fourth seed and scratch-copy runs, and every fix loop re-runs all of it; A92 and A93 are each estimated at 10-14. If the orchestrator needs shorter loops it may split the node into A94a (J07, J08, J16 and their negatives) and A94b (agents, the P1 regression re-run and the launch update) with the same owned paths; that split is a graph change and is not made here.

**Depends on:** [[A90 - P2- registered scheme, sub-funds, PDS and TMD, target market (G05), retail cooling-off]], [[A92 - P2- resale board screens (sell, buy on the board, market tracker, board control)]], [[A93 - P2- governance votes and meetings (SM-10) with screens]], [[A89 - Docker Desktop launch (P1)]]
**Unlocks:** [[A96 - P3- AUD stablecoin rail, V5-V6 vaults and travel rule (G15)]]

**Oracle rule:** this node has no hidden oracle (`oracle: no`). No rule is decided here: the band, lock-in, holder-cap, halt and settlement rules are A91's (an oracle node), the target-market, PDS and cooling-off rules are A90's, the proposal, ballot, snapshot and certification rules are A93's, the halt records are A48's, the flag bindings are A21's and the agent rules are A12's and A101's. This node proves those pieces work together through the screens a person uses. The node QA still writes its Challenge material to `<workspace>\gauntlet\hidden\A94\` before the build starts, from this note alone: an independent expected-state table for every numbered step below (state, gate result, policy code, error code, money figure to the cent), its own crops of the prototype's `sell` region and of the design-extension regions named in Design references, the boundary probes (band edges, the 10 percent breaker edge, the 30-minute lift, the bust only after three retries (four attempts), the 10-business-day clock across the NSW Labour Day holiday), the "green because the flag was on all along" probes, the snapshot-versus-live-balance probe, the agent-scope probes and the PII and token-pattern scans. The builder never reads that folder; the material is published into `app/` only after the gate passes ([[App Gauntlet]] §2).

Everything here runs on the local stack with sandbox adapters only: identity and sanctions sandboxes, the sandbox bank rail, the custody sandbox with no-value keys, the registry sandbox and the local Hardhat chain. P2 flags are switched on only inside this suite and the `launch-p2:preview` profile, bound to a sample licence record that exists only in `RUN_MODE=local|test`; nothing here is a licence, an ASIC relief or a counsel opinion, and no real investor, vendor, testnet or mainnet is touched. This note is not legal advice: where a step touches a question counsel must answer (the cooling-off period, what a "significant dealing" outside the target market is, whether target-market answers or a ballot are personal acts, whether a board may run at all before relief), the suite asserts the behaviour the owning node ships by default and cites the counsel question rather than deciding it.

## Build brief

This node writes no product behaviour, no product screen and no API. It owns a test suite, a staff-step driver over the console screens, a compose overlay and launch profile for P2, a binding runbook, a launch-guide addendum, an AM9 changelog and an evidence writer, under `<workspace>\app\e2e\p2\` and `<workspace>\app\infra\launch-p2\`. Investor and sponsor steps go through `apps/web`, staff steps through `apps/console`, agent steps through `packages/sdk` and `packages/mcp`. Nothing in `e2e/p2/` imports from `services/*`, `packages/db`, any adapter or any `apps/*` source file, and a lint in the suite fails if it does.

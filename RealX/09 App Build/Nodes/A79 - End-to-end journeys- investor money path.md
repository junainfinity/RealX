# A79 - End-to-end journeys: investor money path

**One Playwright suite that walks the whole investor money path through the built screens, with a real person clicking on each side: the investor in the web app and every RealX staff member in the operator console, each in their own browser context with their own passkey or hardware key. It covers J01 browse (all nine steps, now that the information pages exist), J02 sign up and verify (the referred case decided by a KYCA on ADM-10, the certificate approved on ADM-11, the on-chain claims issued), J03 an SMSF with two trustees, J04 first investment (escrow, close at the hard cap, pro-rata allocation with every scaled-back line refunded once, escrow release of exactly the allocation's subscription money, the mint approval `6E+Q` taken first, the trustee's register entry under that approval id, then the mint signed in the token console by a 2-of-3 custody quorum, a clean reconciliation, and an order that is `settled`), J05 a missed soft cap with every cent refunded, J15 withdrawals below the threshold, above it and above the second-approval line, and J17 the investor check-in. Every failure path in the requirements catalogue becomes a negative test, every oracle hook becomes an assertion, and each delegable investor step is repeated once under an agent token with the same gate results. The suite replaces A61's operator helper with the real console screens. It proves that the AM5 to AM7 screens and the AM3 engines work as one product, and it writes no product behaviour of its own.**

**Build status:** not started. **Milestone:** AM8 (Journeys, hardening and launch). **Lane:** Quality. **Planner:** Opus 5.5 (high). **Builder:** Grok 4.7 (xhigh). **Node QA:** Opus 5.5 (high, every loop). **Graph QA:** Fable 5.1 (high, at milestone close).

## Build brief

This node writes no product behaviour and no product screen. It owns one test folder: a Playwright suite (with a few Vitest files for the agent-parity steps), its fixtures and helpers, a checkpoint module and an evidence writer. The investor side runs in the browser against the `web` container; the staff side runs in the browser against the operator console; SDK and MCP calls are used only for reads that check the screens (never for a step a person takes on a screen), for the agent-parity steps, and for the background market that fills KST01 to its hard cap. Nothing in `e2e/investor/` imports from `services/*`, `packages/db`, any adapter, `apps/web` or `apps/console` source, and a lint registered with `npm run verify` fails if it does. A61's `preview:operator` helper is not used anywhere in this suite (A61 hands that job to this node): every staff decision is a click on the console screen that owns it, by the persona whose role it is, with a fresh simulated hardware-key assertion. The suite touches no `/__test/*` route except A11's authenticator bridge and A09's clock read and advance, never writes to the database, and triggers sandbox behaviour only through the documented panel verbs of A08's `SandboxControl`.

Because each receipt is below the threshold, a market order is at most 41 units (41 × A$238.40 = A$9,774.40 + fee A$48.87 = A$9,823.27; 42 units would need A$10,062.86), Order sizes are drawn from the run's seed. **The crossing order.** A36 makes the hard-cap close due as soon as funded units reach 10,000, so the 50 units above the cap must arrive in one funding event. One generated investor (the "crossing investor", selected by state and recorded) places an order of `c` units, with `c` drawn from the seed between 51 and 200 (inside the 500-unit holder cap), and leaves it `awaiting_funds`; the other market orders (each at most 41 units, all funded at placement) bring funded applications to exactly 10,050 − `c`, which is below the hard cap; the crossing investor's receipt (the order total, above the threshold, sent from its exact verified name with no reference, §3 (f)) is then matched in the console by Priya (TRSY) on ADM-18 and approved by Helen (CFO) on ADM-30, which funds the crossing order and takes funded applications from 10,050 − `c` to exactly 10,050 in one event, 50 above the hard cap and inside KST01's acceptance ceiling of 12,000 (spec point (d)). The market therefore places 3,540 units in total and needs at least `ceil((3,540 − c) ÷ 41) + 1` generated investors (at most 87). The crossing match is a console step by the named people, never a market SDK write. The market never uses a staff route, never touches a figure of the J04 persona, and its orders are the only SDK writes in the suite that stand in for people; the evidence lists them separately.

**3. Spec points settled at Specify (before the node QA writes its material)**

- **(a) The A$500,000 ticket path cannot pass the holder cap on any seeded trust.** J03 step 6 has the SMSF choose "invest A$500,000 or more in one offer". Every seeded trust has 10,000 units and a 5 % holder cap, so the largest holding one investor may apply for is 500 units, which is at most A$209,250.00 even on the dearest trust (MRN01 at A$418.50) and A$119,200.00 on KST01. A `ticket_500k` classification (A32) can therefore be approved but never used. Proposed reading: J03 runs the SMSF on whichever wholesale basis A32's rule pack currently allows for a superannuation fund trustee acquiring trust units (the accountant's-certificate basis on the fund's net assets if the pack allows it; otherwise the basis the pack names). Which bases an SMSF trustee may rely on is a counsel question recorded under Human gates (HG-LEGAL), not a settled reading here. The ticket path is run as far as the CCO's decision on ADM-11, after which a A$500,000 order is refused by G07 with the cap named; the conflict is recorded as a founder and counsel question (HG-LEGAL: the ticket test is only usable where 5 % of a trust is worth A$500,000 or more, that is a trust valued at A$10 million or more, or where a rule pack lifts the cap). If the user prefers a working ticket path, A06 adds a larger commercial trust in a follow-up loop (A06.n) and J03 is re-pointed at it.
- **(b) The catalogue's J04 figures are superseded by the seed.** The catalogue's J04 uses "WLG01 at $250, 100 tokens, fee $125, total $25,125"; the QA report's timeline resolution fixes KST01 Kent Street Terrace at A$238.40 as the open offer, so every J04 figure below is KST01's. The catalogue's J01 listings (PAD01, SBK02, WLG01, PAR01) map to the seed's open offers KST01 and ANN01 and the closed UNL01 in the same way.
- **(c) The console service.** The suite needs the console in A23's compose file (A53). If it is not there, A53 is reopened; this node never adds a compose file.
- **(d) KST01's allocation terms are pinned: `allocation_rule = pro_rata`, `oversubscription_cap_bps = 2,000`.** A06 does not state KST01's allocation rule, and it decides whether a scale-back can happen at all. Under A36, `first_come` forces `oversubscription_cap_bps = 0` (acceptance ceiling = the hard cap, so no order beyond 10,000 units is ever accepted and nothing is scaled back); only `pro_rata` accepts applications past the hard cap, up to `hard_cap_units + floor(hard_cap_units × oversubscription_cap_bps ÷ 10,000)` = 12,000 units at A36's default 2,000 bps, and A36 derives `allow_oversubscription = true`. The design's ADM-12 rule line "pro-rata, scaled to hard cap, 5 % cap" (A38, A71) is the pro-rata case. Proposed reading: KST01 is `pro_rata` at 2,000 bps, and A37's pro-rata rule then scales **every** line, the J04 persona's and the agent's included: each line gets `floor(requested × 10,000 ÷ 10,050)` and the leftover whole units go one each by largest fractional remainder, ties to the earliest `funded_at`, then id (A37). The checkpoint is therefore a per-line table that the node QA re-derives for each seed (§6), not one scaled-back line. The J04 persona's allocation `a_P` is 99 or 100 units (floor 99, remainder 0.5025, plus one if that remainder ranks inside the leftover), and the agent's `a_B` is 9 or 10. The suite reads KST01's terms from A36's offer read at Specify and stops with `E2E_TERMS_MISMATCH` naming A06 if they differ. If the founder prefers `first_come` for KST01, A06 is reopened (A06.n): the market then stops at exactly 10,000 units, J04 has no scale-back, and the G11 scale-back proof needs a `pro_rata` offer that A06 adds. This node does not choose between them silently.
- **(e) Agent acknowledgement of disclosures (CQ-02).** Where A12's delegable row for acknowledging a disclosure under standing consent is still `"counsel"`, the agent's order returns `428 CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED` exactly as the person's would need a confirmation; §7 compares outcomes, not a fixed state.
- **(f) The legitimate held-deposit path.** A16 (`deposit.matched`: at or above the manual-match threshold, `4E` TRSY → CFO) and A17 (an exact name-and-destination receipt is credited at once, with A16's `aml_hold`) disagree on what happens to a large deposit sent to the investor's own PayID. The disagreement is recorded as a spec defect for A16 and A17 to settle; this node does not rely on either reading. Every deposit in this suite that must pass through the console is sent **with no PayID and no reference, to the client-money wallets account, from the persona's exact verified legal name** (read at run time from `verifiedNames(investorId)` through the staff read token, never typed, so it matches whether or not A30 keeps the identity sandbox's ` REFER` marker in the legal name). A17 holds it `unknown_destination` under both readings. The manual match on ADM-18 then needs `4E` TRSY → CFO at any amount (A17 manual-match rule), and the amount is also above A16's threshold. No receipt in this suite is ever sent under a payer name that differs from the verified name, except the G14 and N14 negatives, which must never be credited.
- **(g) The V2 signers of the KST01 mint.** A15's P9 excludes the maker and every checker from the signer set of an op, and A15's default V2 slots are SECO, CEO and a TOKO who is not the maker. The KST01 mint approval has maker Tom Hargreaves (CAPM) and checkers Helen Tran (CFO) and Jordan Blake (CCO), so the eligible V2 signers are Kai Watanabe (TOKO), Aisha Karim (SECO) and Marcus Bell (CEO). The suite reads the V2 roster from A15's vault record at run time and uses two eligible members; it never picks a checker. A06's vault text lists V2 as "TOKO, CCO, CFO", which under P9 leaves one eligible signer for a CFO + CCO-checked mint (A15's `CST_QUORUM_UNREACHABLE`). This is an A06/A15 disagreement that must be settled on A06 before Specify; this node follows A15.

# A47 - Portfolio, holdings and transactions read models with statements

**The read side of the investor, sponsor and public views, composed only from the sources of truth: a portfolio whose value at valuation and at last price is always the sum of its holdings, cash available and reserved from the ledger, a twelve-month income series from paid distribution lines, holding detail with average cost, income received, lock-in end, on-chain balance and the register-match date of the last clean reconciliation ("We're checking this with the trustee" on any mismatch, never a delta), a transactions ledger with on-chain references and deterministic confirmation PDFs rendered from the orders module's confirmation records, payment advices and annual statements cut from ledger snapshots and stored in the documents library, one POR-07 statements listing that carries the tax module's AMMA rows, a sponsor register view with break banner and logged export, and a public transparency table that publishes only attested figures. Every holding, register and transparency row carries the G10 halt verdict with its published notice, reopening criteria and end date. Every row says who acted, so a person's activity reads "placed by your agent <name>" where a mandate acted, and every read is a token scope with UI parity.**

**Build status:** not started. **Milestone:** AM3 (Business services). **Lane:** Services. **Planner:** Opus 5.5 (high). **Builder:** Grok 4.7 (xhigh). **Node QA:** Opus 5.5 (high, every loop). **Graph QA:** Fable 5.1 (high, at milestone close).
**Estimate:** 8-12 agent-hours (build, challenge and verify loops).

**Depends on:** [[A18 - Unit register (legal record) and registry sandbox adapter]], [[A16 - Client-money ledger (double entry)]], [[A40 - Distributions engine (SM-08)]], [[A27 - Chain adapter, deployment manifests and indexer]], [[A20 - Three-way reconciliation engine with zero tolerance]], [[A48 - Halts, kill switches and wind-down]], [[A35 - Documents, disclosure library, e-sign and acknowledgements (G12)]], [[A37 - Orders, escrow, allocation and refunds (SM-05)]], [[A41 - Tax engine- profiles, withholding, AMMA and CRS-FATCA]], [[A44 - Support, complaints (SM-12) and Investor 360 read model]], [[A45 - Reporting, regulatory calendar, filings and metric governance]], [[A32 - Classification- wholesale certificates and target-market hooks (SM-02)]], [[A21 - Licences, feature flags and licence perimeter (G21)]], [[A06 - Seed data and scenario simulator]] (A34, A19, A12, A13, A02 and A09 are already ancestors through A40 and A18; none of the added nodes depends on A47, so no cycle. `final-graph.json` must carry the same list.)
**Unlocks:** [[A54 - Public site- home, investments, property page, sponsor profile, fees]], [[A55 - Public site- how it works, returns calculator, transparency, help and documents]], [[A58 - Portfolio, holding detail, transactions, distributions and statements]], [[A59 - AUD wallet, token wallet and account screens]], [[A64 - Sponsor console- register, distributions, property reporting, valuations, announcements]] (A55 and A59 need A47 added to their deps in `final-graph.json`).

**Oracle rule:** not an oracle node (`oracle: no`): this node moves no money, supply or holdings; it only reads them. The node QA still writes hidden challenge tests to `<workspace>\gauntlet\hidden\A47\` before the build starts, from this note alone: the sum identities (portfolio = Σ holdings at both bases, portfolio monthly income = Σ holdings' monthly income, cash-received FY-to-date = Σ lines paid in the FY, monthly income = Σ lines of the latest paid month, the portfolio series equal to the distributions series), the cross-node FY-basis test (cash-received on POR-01, tax-year from A41 on POR-07), the "we're checking with the trustee" masking, the G10 halt matrix over every A20 and A48 cause, the confirmation-PDF byte determinism, the statement-cut reproducibility, the scoping and PII matrix, the agent attribution rows and the seed comparison against the design's `port`, `activity`, `wallet`, `statements` and `spRegister` regions. The builder never reads that folder ([[App Gauntlet]] §2).

This note is not legal advice. What a statement, payment advice or confirmation must legally contain, and whether an AMMA statement may be shown before the tax module (A41) issues it, are counsel questions recorded under Human gates; this node fixes the data and its identities, not the legal wording.

## Build brief

This node is API-only (module `portfolio` in the Fastify monolith plus two worker jobs). It owns no screens: POR-01, POR-02, POR-03, POR-04 and POR-07 are rendered by A58; POR-05's third tile ("Tokens held") by A59; SPN-08 by A64; PUB-08 by A55; the property page's per-trust distribution history by A54; ADM-09 by A68 through A44's Investor 360 contributor registry (this module registers the `holdings` contributor, below). The module owns no source-of-truth table: every number it returns is composed at read time (or materialised nightly and rebuildable byte for byte) from A18's register, A16's ledger, A40's distribution lines, A27's holding read model, A20's reconciliation results, A48's halt records (through A19's G10 evaluation), A37's confirmation records and A41's tax summaries, each read only through that module's `index.ts` interface. It never writes to any of them. The documents it renders are stored as A35 document versions (below).

**Sources and the identities that must hold (the module's own invariants, tested on every read)**

- Units held per investor per vehicle come from A18 `GET /register/holders/{investorId}/entries?as_of=` (open entries, `units`, `lots[]`, `frozen_units`, `locked_units`); never from the chain. The chain balance (A27 `holding` row for the investor's wallet × token, with `block_number` and `state`) is shown beside the register figure, not instead of it.
- Prices: `offer_price_cents` and `valuation_per_unit_cents` (with `valuation_date`) come from the vehicle and its latest approved valuation through A34's read interface (A34 is an ancestor, so the real module is available; no fixture). `last_price_cents` comes from a `LastPriceSource` interface: in P1 it returns the offer price (no resale), in P2 A91 registers the last traded price behind the same interface. The response always says which basis produced `value_at_last_price` (`basis: 'offer_price' | 'last_trade'`).
- Cash comes from A16 `me/wallet`: `available_cents`, `reserved_cents` (A16's object `{ orders, withdrawals, holds_present }` passed through unchanged; `holds_present` stays boolean-only, never a reason), `awaiting_investment_cents`, `account_status`. This module adds nothing, subtracts nothing and never collapses `reserved_cents` into a scalar.
- Income comes from A40's read interface: `distribution_line` rows for the investor with A40's status vocabulary exactly (`paid`, `scheduled`, `delayed`, `declared_zero`, `reduced`), `gross_cents`, `withholding_cents`, `net_cents`, `paid_at`, `record_date`, `run_id`, `vehicle_id`, `units_at_record_date`, `per_unit_display`, `delayed_reason` (the public reason A40 approved); the next scheduled run date per vehicle from the same interface. A line A40 holds for an exception is reported by A40 as `paid` with its `paid_at` like any other line (tipping-off, A40's rule), and this module passes that through without adding any status, flag or field; there is no `held` status anywhere in this module.
- Financial-year bases (two, never mixed, each labelled): **cash received** = lines whose `paid_at` falls in the Australian financial year (1 Jul–30 Jun, Australia/Sydney), used only on POR-01's dashboard tile and POR-03's totals; **tax year** = A41's attribution by `fy(period_end)` of the income period, used on POR-07 and the AMMA, and always read from A41's `me/tax/summary` interface, never recomputed here.
- Reconciliation comes from A20: `GET /v1/recon/vehicles/{id}` (last run, `result`, `client_money_result`, `bank_delta_cents`, open breaks, recon `halted`, sponsor-visible breaks), the last clean run's `started_at` per vehicle, and `GET /v1/public/recon` rows for PUB-08.
- Halts come from A19's G10 evaluation, which is the union of A20's recon-halt source and A48's `haltState(vehicleId)` (`{ halted, halts: [{ halt_id, cause, scope, criteria, ends_at, state, since }], platform: { suspended, since } }`); the plain-words text is A48's `haltNotice(halt)`. This module never evaluates a halt, never reads A48's or A20's halt tables directly, and never writes halt copy of its own.
- Identities enforced in `identities.ts` (pure functions over the composed rows, run before every response is sent; a violation is never patched, it returns 500 `POR_IDENTITY_VIOLATION` with the identity name and raises A13's `portfolio.identity_failed` Sev2 to ENGO, because it means a source disagrees with itself): `portfolio.value_at_valuation_cents = Σ holdings.value_at_valuation_cents`; `portfolio.value_at_last_price_cents = Σ holdings.value_at_last_price_cents`; `portfolio.units_total = Σ holdings.units`; `portfolio.monthly_income_cents = Σ holdings.monthly_income_cents`; `portfolio.cash_received_fy_to_date_cents = Σ net_cents of paid lines with paid_at in the current financial year`; `portfolio.monthly_income_cents = Σ net_cents of paid lines in the latest calendar month with at least one paid line`; `income_series[m] = Σ net_cents paid in month m` for the last 12 months, and `me/portfolio.income_series` is byte-equal to `me/portfolio/distributions.series` for the same investor and instant (one function produces both); `holding.income_received_cents = Σ net_cents of that vehicle's paid lines`; `holding.average_cost_cents = round_half_even(Σ lot.units × lot.price_cents ÷ Σ lot.units)` over open lots; `holding.gain_cents = units × (last_price − average_cost)`; every cents figure is an integer produced by A02's `Cents` functions and carries an en-AU formatted twin.

**Read contracts (all `GET`, `/v1`, cursor-paginated where lists, `x-realx-scope` on every route so A12's tokens and the MCP server expose them with UI parity; investor routes are self-scoped, sponsor routes tenant-scoped by RLS, partner routes vehicle-scoped, staff reads through A44's Investor 360)**

Route uniqueness: this module registers only the paths listed here. `/v1/me/distributions` and `/v1/me/distributions/summary` are A40's routes and this module never registers them; `/v1/me/documents` (list) is A35's; `/v1/me/tax/*` is A41's. A route-uniqueness test in this module's suite loads every module's route table in the monolith and fails if any A47 path (method plus path template) is also registered elsewhere, and Fastify's duplicate-route error at boot is asserted as a second guard.
